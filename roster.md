## Lady Addee Ulphor — Lady of Shadowdale

- **Role:** Ruler of Shadowdale; Shadowdale's voice on the Dales Council
- **Affiliation:** Shadowdale; the Dales Council
- **Location:** The Ashaba House (the dale's modest lordly hall, built into the southern base of Old Skull)
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A practical, composed woman who has held a quiet dale together for twenty years — and is now watching that quiet erode from below.

**Appearance:** Mid-fifties. Brown skin weathered by years of outdoor inspections and border rides. Silver-streaked black hair kept in a tight braid. She wears the **Pendant of Ashaba** openly — a deep blue teardrop stone on a silver chain — and plain working clothes unless ceremony demands otherwise. Carries herself without theatrics. **(DM ADDITION, consistent with world.md)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Steady.
- *Public face:* Measured, fair, unhurried. She listens before she speaks, and when she speaks it lands. Shadowdalers trust her because she has never overpromised.
- *Private self:* Quietly frightened. Twenty years of calm governance have not prepared her for what the militia scouts are reporting from beneath Old Skull. She is determined not to show the fear, which is making her slightly rigid.
- *Mannerism:* Touches the Pendant of Ashaba absently when she's thinking hard about something. Rarely raises her voice.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* To keep Shadowdale free, peaceful, and fed. To resolve the Old Skull situation before it becomes a crisis she can't contain.
- *Fears:* That the drow threat is larger than the dale can handle alone — and that asking for outside help will cost Shadowdale its independence.
- *Secret:* She has not attended the Dales Council in person for over a year. She sends Brynn Ashford in her place because she doesn't dare leave Shadowdale with the Underdark activity growing. The Council knows but hasn't pressed the issue — yet. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Courteous but appraising — she sizes up strangers quickly and doesn't waste words on those she doesn't trust yet.
- *What she wants from the party:* Reliable people who can go into Old Skull or the Living Wood and bring back information (or solutions) without triggering a panic.
- *What she'd never tell them:* That she thinks the drow incursion may already be further along than her militia's reports suggest, and that she's been having the Ashaba House's lower cellars quietly sealed off.

**Source notes:** Name and the Pendant of Ashaba established in world.md (consistent with CG). Physical description and personality are DM additions.

## Brynn Ashford — Shadowdale's Delegate to the Dales Council

- **Role:** Shadowdale's working representative at the Dales Council in Ashabenford; Lady Ulphor's proxy
- **Affiliation:** Shadowdale; the Dales Council
- **Location:** Splits time between Shadowdale and Ashabenford (Mistledale). Currently in Shadowdale.
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A sharp-tongued young diplomat trying to punch above his weight at a table of older, better-resourced dales — and quietly resentful that he's been given the chair instead of the authority to do anything with it.

**Appearance:** Early thirties. Lean, angular face with ink-stained fingers — he writes everything down. Dresses carefully in Dalelands practical-formal: clean leather doublet, Shadowdale's thistle-and-ash badge pinned at the collar. Moves quickly and tends to perch at the edge of chairs rather than sit back in them. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Sharp.
- *Public face:* Composed, articulate, well-prepared. He knows Shadowdale's position on every agenda item before the meeting starts.
- *Private self:* Frustrated. He represents Shadowdale with genuine loyalty, but Lady Ulphor's instructions rarely extend his mandate far enough to make real commitments. He watches Archendale play both sides and Mistledale hoard its political capital, and he can't do much about either.
- *Mannerism:* Takes notes constantly — during conversations, at the tavern, apparently in his sleep. When he disagrees with something, he asks a clarifying question instead of stating the disagreement directly. This is both genuinely useful and occasionally maddening.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* To get Shadowdale taken seriously as a voice on the Council, not just a small dale that benefits from others' decisions. To eventually be trusted with a broader mandate from Lady Ulphor.
- *Fears:* That the Council will decide the free dales' best option is to seek Cormyrian protection — which would effectively end Shadowdale's independence.
- *Secret:* He has begun quietly building his own relationships with other delegates' staffers, gathering information Lady Ulphor didn't authorize him to gather. He tells himself it's for the dale's benefit. It might be. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Slightly guarded — he's used to evaluating people's usefulness before investing in them.
- *What he wants from the party:* If they have any standing with other factions (Myth Drannor, Harpers, Cormyr), he wants to know. Leverage is his primary currency.
- *What he'd never tell them:* The specific state of Council fractures he's observed — that information is his personal insurance.

**Plot hook:** If the party needs Council resources, Brynn can unlock them — but he'll extract something in return. And if his unauthorized information-gathering ever surfaces, it could embarrass Lady Ulphor badly at precisely the wrong moment. **(DM ADDITION)**

## Torval Dunmore — Innkeeper, The Old Skull Inn

- **Role:** Proprietor of Shadowdale's only inn; informal social center of the village
- **Affiliation:** None formal; loyal to the dale
- **Location:** The Old Skull Inn, central village
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A one-eyed veteran who has seen the dale at its worst and decided the best contribution he could make was to run a decent inn and keep his ears open.

**Appearance:** Late fifties. A big man run slightly to fat, with a close-cropped gray beard and a glass eye that doesn't quite track with the real one — he lost the original to a drow crossbow bolt when he was in the militia twenty years ago. Wears a permanent mild expression that makes it hard to tell if he's amused or just waiting for the conversation to become interesting. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Patient.
- *Public face:* Warm, slow to judge, generous with the first drink. A good listener.
- *Private self:* Observant and unsentimental. He has no illusions about the dale's dangers and has been watching the Old Skull situation with quiet dread. He knows more about what the militia scouts have been finding than most civilians because scouts drink.
- *Mannerism:* Wipes down the bar even when it's already clean. Has a way of hearing things without looking like he's listening.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* For the dale to stay safe. Specifically for Old Skull not to become what it was when he was young.
- *Fears:* That his grandchildren will grow up in a dale that's been overrun or absorbed.
- *Secret:* The cellar of the Old Skull Inn has a sealed passage — part of the old tunnel network beneath the village. He cemented the hatch shut himself after his wife disappeared down it thirty years ago. He has never told Lady Ulphor. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Professionally welcoming, privately curious. He'll serve them, hear their names and business, and say almost nothing revealing until they've been around long enough to be vouched for.
- *What he wants from the party:* Nothing directly — but if they're the kind of people who go into dark places and come back, he will eventually find a way to ask them about the cellar.
- *What he'd never tell them (initially):* Anything about the sealed passage.

**Practical notes:** Rooms 4 cp/night, stabling available, meals are simple but filling. He brews his own dark ale ("Skull Stout") that locals swear by. **(DM ADDITION)**

## Sela Wyndmere — Herbalist and Lay Healer

- **Role:** The village's primary healer and herbalist; informal liaison with the fey of the Living Wood
- **Affiliation:** None formal; quietly aligned with Mielikki and the fey community
- **Location:** A cottage on the north edge of the village, where the settlement bleeds into the Living Wood
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A half-elf who has lived at the edge of the forest long enough that the fey treat her as a neighbor — which means she knows things no one else in the village does, and doesn't always share them.

**Appearance:** Apparent age late thirties (true age closer to sixty — half-elf). Slight, with silver-green eyes and dark hair shot through with a white streak she's had since she was a child. Her clothes always have burrs and leaf debris in them. Her cottage smells of drying herbs, river clay, and something that might be foxfire or might just be imagination. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Knowing.
- *Public face:* Gentle, unhurried, dependably competent. Shadowdalers bring her everything from fevers to grief and she takes it all with equal quiet attention.
- *Private self:* Deeply embedded in the fey community of the Living Wood in ways she doesn't fully explain. She attends things in the forest at night that she describes, if asked, as "consultations." She is not entirely sure whether the fey are her friends or her patrons, and the distinction worries her.
- *Mannerism:* Seldom gives a direct answer when an indirect one will do. Often phrases things as questions. Has a habit of pausing mid-conversation to listen to something no one else hears.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* The Living Wood to remain healthy and the fey within it to remain — she knows that if the drow threat drives the fey out, something much worse will move in.
- *Fears:* That the Feywild bleed-through is not entirely benevolent. She has been noticing strange behavior from some of the fey near Old Skull — things she can't explain as typical territorial behavior.
- *Secret:* A fey lord of the Living Wood, one of the older and more powerful ones, has been asking her to carry messages. She doesn't know who the messages are intended for, and she's been saying yes because she doesn't know how to say no to him. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Friendly, quietly evaluating. She's seen enough adventurers to know they usually bring trouble for someone.
- *What she wants from the party:* If they're trustworthy, she may ask them to accompany her on one of her forest "consultations." Something has changed in the wood and she doesn't want to go alone.
- *What she'd never tell them (initially):* Who she's been carrying messages for, or to whom.

**Practical notes:** She can provide healing poultices, basic antitoxins, and herbal remedies. She won't charge people who genuinely can't pay. She'll also answer questions about the Living Wood — up to a point. **(DM ADDITION)**

## Corwick Helm — Blacksmith and Militia Captain

- **Role:** Village blacksmith; captain of Shadowdale's small standing militia
- **Affiliation:** Shadowdale militia; loyal to Lady Ulphor
- **Location:** The smithy on the east side of the village square
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A no-nonsense man trying to keep a ten-person militia ready to deal with something he's increasingly convinced they are not ready for.

**Appearance:** Mid-forties. Built like a door. Close-cropped red hair going gray at the temples, burn scars across both forearms from the forge. Usually in a leather apron during the day; militia surcoat in the evenings when he makes his rounds. Doesn't smile much, not because he's unfriendly but because smiling takes time he could be spending on something else. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Direct.
- *Public face:* Gruff, capable, respected. He gets things done without drama and expects the same from others.
- *Private self:* Deeply worried about Old Skull. His militia is ten people; two of them are reliable; the rest are farmers who showed up. He has been training harder than usual and not explaining why, because he doesn't want to start a panic.
- *Mannerism:* States opinions as facts. Has no patience for hedging. When someone dances around a point, he says the point out loud and watches their face.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* Better-trained militia. Better-equipped militia. Any help at all, frankly.
- *Fears:* That Old Skull is going to produce something that ten farmers with spears cannot handle, and that by the time the Dales Council gets around to doing anything about it, the village will already be gone.
- *Secret:* Two of his scouts went into the lower passages of Old Skull three weeks ago and came back one short. He told Lady Ulphor one; he hasn't told anyone they found signs of a fresh camp — not a patrol, a camp — forty feet below the surface. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Skeptical, functional. He'll assess their gear and bearing before he says much. If they look capable, he warms up fast because he needs capable people badly.
- *What he wants from the party:* Scouting help. Specifically someone to go back into Old Skull's upper passages and tell him what the scouts saw wasn't what he thinks it was.
- *What he'd never tell them (initially):* The full truth of what the scouts reported. He'll tell them the passages need checking; the detail about the camp comes later, when he trusts them.

**Practical notes:** Can repair or commission armor and weapons. His work is solid if not beautiful. He'll do it cheaper for people who are helping the dale. **(DM ADDITION)**

## Orvyn Tal — Bookseller; former Harper agent

- **Role:** Proprietor of a small book and curiosities trade; retired (semi) Harper operative
- **Affiliation:** Nominally independent; the Harpers (inactive, theoretically)
- **Location:** A cramped shopfront two doors north of the inn, packed floor to ceiling with books, maps, and bound manuscripts
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A man who "retired" from the Harpers a decade ago and now sells old books — which is a perfectly plausible reason to maintain correspondence with people in every city between Waterdeep and Selgaunt.

**Appearance:** Early sixties. Slight, tidy, with the careful posture of a man who spent decades making himself unremarkable in rooms full of dangerous people. Watery blue eyes that are sharper than they look. Dresses plainly. The only notable thing about his appearance is that his right thumb is missing from the second joint — he doesn't explain this. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Careful.
- *Public face:* A mild-mannered scholar and bookseller, moderately knowledgeable, always willing to help a customer find something if he has it.
- *Private self:* Still very much connected to the Harper network, though in an advisory and intelligence-gathering capacity rather than active fieldwork. He has not told Lady Ulphor he still operates. He is reasonably sure she suspects.
- *Mannerism:* Asks questions that seem like small talk but aren't. Very good at making you feel like you told him things voluntarily.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* To keep Shadowdale's Harper network functional. To understand what's happening under Old Skull before Netheril does — the Shadovar's interest in old drow passages is a known quantity and it worries him.
- *Fears:* That the Netherese already have an agent in the village. He has suspicions but no proof. He keeps revising the list.
- *Secret:* He knows approximately where a long-dormant Harper safehouse is beneath the village — a room sealed during the last bad period with drow. He doesn't know if it still has anything useful inside. He also doesn't know whether the drow have found it. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Pleasantly forgettable. He's good at this.
- *What he wants from the party:* To know who they are and whether they're trustworthy, before anything else. If they are, he has information to trade.
- *What he'd never tell them (initially):* That he's still Harper-active. He'll wait to see if they already know — if they're Harpers themselves, they'll find out through channels. If they're not, he'll decide whether to tell them based on what they do next.

**Practical notes:** His shop has genuine value: maps of the Dalelands and surrounding regions (some quite old), histories of Shadowdale, and one or two arcane texts. He can also source specific books for a price and a wait — through methods he doesn't describe. **(DM ADDITION)**
