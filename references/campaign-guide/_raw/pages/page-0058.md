## Marlspire of Najara 

One of the fabled _naga crowns_ created by the yuanti, the Marlspire of Najara is the royal crown of that nation. Members of the Hss’tafi tribe of yuan-ti brought the crown to Najara (page 158) when they were transported there in the Year of Netted Dreams (–320 DR). They gave the crown to the great naga Terpenzi, creator and first king of Najara, as an offering of fealty. Since then, the _Marlspire_ has been the crown of state in Najara, resting on the brow of every naga King of Snakes, up to and including Jarant (page 158).
