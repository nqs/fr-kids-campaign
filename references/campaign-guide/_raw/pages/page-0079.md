THE PRIMORDIALS<br>**----- End of picture text -----**<br>


Nineteen gods are worshiped in Faerûn, spread across all alignments and holding mastery over a wide range of spheres. Gods usually act independently of the greater god of their planes, though some deities, such as Bane, hold sway over all the divine inhabitants of their realms. 

The Elemental Chaos provides essential building blocks for all matter in the cosmos, the primordial seed of all that is. The gods fear this wild plane of unimaginable extremes, and they respect the primordials that call it home. 

Umberlee is an example of such a god. She dwells in the Deep Wilds of Silvanus, along with Auril and Mielikki. 

The few primordials that remained in Toril when Abeir split away never fought the gods as their fellows did. These primordials are sometimes worshiped as deities despite their elemental origin.

## THE PANDEMONIUM STONE 


![](references/campaign-guide/_raw/images/forgotten-realms-campaign-guide.pdf-0079-19.png)


**----- Start of picture text -----**<br>
EXARCHS<br>**----- End of picture text -----**<br>


The Pandemonium Stone isn’t a permanent feature of the Elemental Chaos. This rough spire of various materials, ever-changing and immense, features carvings that come alive with elemental forces, undulate, then die down again. The massive stone manifests at random in place and time. 

Exarchs are often referred to as demigods. Many are ascended mortal servants of greater gods, brought up from the world to serve as agents of their divine masters. Some attract worshipers of their own, but they are more often simply conduits that serve to connect the mortal world with the attention of the higher deities. 

When it appears, the spire calls to the slaads, and they gather to bathe in its energy. The greatest of them, including Bazim-Gorag the Firebringer, eventually come to the Pandemonium Stone if the pinnacle stays long enough in one place. Tracing the movements of the stone, if possible, is likely the easiest way to find and attack the great slaad. 

Exarchs are incapable of forming planes of their own. They occasionally maintain smaller pocket universes or independent fiefdoms on their home planes. 

Stories differ as to what the stone is. Some suggest that it is a sleeping and immensely powerful slaad lord. Others speculate that it’s a batrachi artifact, and that studying it could prove decisively whether slaads descend from that amphibious progenitor race. 

Marthammor Duin is an example of an exarch. He serves Moradin, along with four other exarchs and the goddess Berronar, in the halls of Dwarfhome. 

**Alignment:** Chaotic evil. 

**Connections:** None. 

**Primary Resident:** Bazim-Gorag (primordial).
