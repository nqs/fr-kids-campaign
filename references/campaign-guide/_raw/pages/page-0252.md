Jarlaxle and Bregan D’aerthe have contacts in surface cities such as Waterdeep, Baldur’s Gate, Athkatla, and Darromar, as well as Mulmaster and Lyrabar. A few underworld guilds even consider Bregan D’aerthe an ally. Through this set of connections, Jarlaxle and his followers expand their influence and grow ever richer.
