## Gibberling Lore 

A character knows the following information with a successful Dungeoneering check. 

**DC 10:** Gibberlings are darkness-loving creatures that hibernate beneath the earth. When they awaken, they gather and hunt in huge packs. 

**DC 15:** Outside the Underdark, gibberlings burrow into the soil during daylight hours. The creatures are vulnerable during this time, but they awaken if attacked or as soon as dusk comes. When roused, they emerge in unison and continue their rampage, moving slowly if in daylight. 

**DC 20:** Plaguechanged gibberlings have no affinity for their mundane kin, devouring them as readily as any other prey.
