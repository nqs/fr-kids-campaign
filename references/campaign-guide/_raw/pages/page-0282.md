## Zairtail Gazer Tactics 

Zairtail gazers have sticky pads on their many-toed feet that allow them to cling to trees, ceilings, and other ideal vantage points. A gazer’s bulging eyes make it look cute, but they are a hypnotic weapon for distracting prey while it calls the swarms of its fellows to feed.

## Zairtail Cutter Tactics 

A zairtail cutter never stands still but darts in and out of battle with its _slicing charge,_ using _evasive slash_ to gain combat advantage when possible.
