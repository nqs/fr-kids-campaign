## Tactics 

Sath’fiss’ith deals mercilessly with foes, unleashing the full fury of its mystical powers before turning to claw and fang. It uses _noxious whirlwind_ to lift as many foes as possible aloft on green vapors, and _hypnotic eyes_ to maneuver without risk from at least one adversary. It blasts opponents with forked emerald bolts of _viper lightning;_ against ranged attackers, it launches golden _sun orbs_ .
