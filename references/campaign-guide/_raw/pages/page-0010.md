- ~~A~~ ~~**Vexing Cloud** (standard; sustain minor; encounter) ✦~~ ~~**Zone**~~ Area burst 3 within 10; automatic hit; all enemies within the zone take a –2 penalty to attack rolls. The zone grants concealment to the goblin hexer and its allies. The goblin hexer can sustain the zone as a minor action, moving it up to 5 squares. 

**Well:** The well has a small lip to prevent people from falling in. However, a creature that is forced into one of the well’s squares might fall the 20 feet to the bottom. (The creature is entitled to a saving throw to avoid falling.) The fall deals 2d10 damage, and the water at the bottom is 5 feet deep. The walls are slick and require a DC 20 Athletics check to climb.
