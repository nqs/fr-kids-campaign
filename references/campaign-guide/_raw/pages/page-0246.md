## Tentacled Torment Tactics 

If possible, a tentacled torment prefers to find a lone individual and sneak up on it invisibly, then use _commanding tentacle_ to direct the creature’s actions. It causes the most dangerous melee combatants to flee from it, while forcing ranged threats to attack their own allies instead. 

**DC 35:** Ancient creatures of aberrant seed, tentacled torments were thought to have been wiped out in the Dawn Era. However, Rorn kept a few hidden away, and these have served him ever since. 

**DC 40:** Tentacled torments have developed a natural mind link with zairtails (page 280) and sometimes use these creatures to spy on those who train them. 

It prefers to work with allies, which can get into advantageous positions while they are invisible, perhaps to steal an important item or document. It uses _healing tentacle_ as needed to keep its allies healthy. If caught alone by a group of powerful foes, a tentacled torment flees.
