## Duplicate 

_From one, there are now two._ 

**Level:** 6 

**Component Cost:** 70 gp **Market Price:** 150 gp **Key Skill:** Arcana 

**Category:** Creation **Time:** 10 minutes **Duration:** Special 

Prior to performing this ritual, you fashion a crude clay replica of a small, inorganic object you possess that weighs no more than 2 pounds, such as a key. When you complete this ritual, the crude replica transforms into an exact copy of the original. It performs identically to the original in all mundane ways and is not recognizable as a fake, even by someone using the Arcana skill. A copy of a magic item is nonmagical. 

Once the duration expires, the object reverts to its original crude clay form.

The Feat of Strength ritual confers to a single willing subject a significant boost of strength, enabling the target to make a single Strength check with a bonus to the check determined by your Nature check result. ## Purify Spellscarred 

_Your healing touch expels the taint of the Spellplague from a living creature._ 

**Level:** 18 **Component Cost:** 7,000 gp **Category:** Restoration **Market Price:** 20,000 gp **Time:** 30 minutes **Key Skill:** Heal (no check) **Duration:** Instantaneous 

You remove all traces of the Spellplague from a willing spellscarred creature. A spellscarred creature reverts to its normal appearance and loses its spellscar as well as all beneficial or harmful effects the scar provided. A character who has gained the Student of the Spellplague feat, or any other feats that require a creature to be spellscarred, loses those feats and can choose others to replace them.
