## Character Motivations 

For better or for worse, the Vilhon Wilds is a region shaped by the Spellplague and its aftermath. Like other lawless parts of Faerûn, the Vilhon Wilds gives birth to more than its share of hardy individuals who fall naturally into lives of adventure. The region is unique in that most would-be adventurers are shaped one way or another by the proximity of the Plaguewrought Land and the mysteries it unleashes.
