# Torval Dunmore — Innkeeper, The Old Skull Inn

> [Back to NPC Roster](../roster.md)

- **Role:** Proprietor of Shadowdale's only inn; informal social center of the village
- **Affiliation:** None formal; loyal to the dale
- **Location:** The Old Skull Inn, central village
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A one-eyed veteran who has seen the dale at its worst and decided the best contribution he could make was to run a decent inn and keep his ears open.

**Appearance:** Late fifties. A big man run slightly to fat, with a close-cropped gray beard and a glass eye that doesn't quite track with the real one — he lost the original to a drow crossbow bolt when he was in the militia twenty years ago. Wears a permanent mild expression that makes it hard to tell if he's amused or just waiting for the conversation to become interesting. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Patient.
- *Public face:* Warm, slow to judge, generous with the first drink. A good listener.
- *Private self:* Observant and unsentimental. He has no illusions about the dale's dangers and has been watching the Old Skull situation with quiet dread. He knows more about what the militia scouts have been finding than most civilians because scouts drink.
- *Mannerism:* Wipes down the bar even when it's already clean. Has a way of hearing things without looking like he's listening.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* For the dale to stay safe. Specifically for Old Skull not to become what it was when he was young.
- *Fears:* That his grandchildren will grow up in a dale that's been overrun or absorbed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Secret
> The cellar of the Old Skull Inn has a sealed passage — part of the old tunnel network beneath the village. He cemented the hatch shut himself after his wife disappeared down it thirty years ago. He has never told Lady Ulphor. **(DM ADDITION)**


**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Professionally welcoming, privately curious. He'll serve them, hear their names and business, and say almost nothing revealing until they've been around long enough to be vouched for.
- *What he wants from the party:* Nothing directly — but if they're the kind of people who go into dark places and come back, he will eventually find a way to ask them about the cellar.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** What he won't say
> Anything about the sealed passage.


**Practical notes:** Rooms 4 cp/night, stabling available, meals are simple but filling. He brews his own dark ale ("Skull Stout") that locals swear by. **(DM ADDITION)**
