# Oberon, the Green Lord — Archfey of the Living Wood

> [Back to NPC Roster](../roster.md)
>
> *The fey lord styled in play (S4–S5) only as **the Thorn Lord / the Living Lord** — **named at last** for the Session 006 audience. The in-fiction reveal lands when he names himself at his own table; the identity itself is settled DM canon.*

- **Role:** Lord of the Living Wood and its Feywild seam; the power behind the **recall / slow harvest** that is emptying the wood (see [factions](../factions.md) §8)
- **True name:** **Oberon, the Green Lord** — an archfey of the wild green, lord of beasts and growing things. Styled **"the Thorn Lord / Living Lord"** by Shadowdalers who do not know his name
- **Affiliation:** His own court — the green-white-bone court at the bright center of the seam (prep name: the *Bright Court Beneath*)
- **Location:** ~~His court across the seam~~ — **scattered (S6).** The Bright Court Beneath is disrupted; where and when he reconstitutes is unknown
- **Status:** **Scattered — not dead (S6, played).** When Sela ripped the binding ribbon at his throne, Oberon **burst into a flock of butterflies** — his face wide with *surprise*, not rage. Archfey do not die from dispersal. His reconstitution, and his disposition toward the party and toward Sela when he returns, are open questions. *Still not a fightable foe* (no statblock by design; see below)
- **One-line summary:** The most gracious host imaginable and the predator behind the blight — an ancient archfey who does not *steward* the green so much as **gather** it, reeling whole living woods (and now people) into his eternal court to keep forever, smiling and still.

**Appearance:** A tall, beautiful, eternal fey king with bark-textured greenish skin and eyes like green-gold sunlight through leaves; crowned in living antler and white hawthorn blossom, robed in leaf, moss, and root, hung with the court's three ribbons of **green, white, and bone.** He smiles with a host's warmth that does not quite reach his too-old eyes. Portrait in [S6 player handouts](../../sessions/session%206/the-green-lords-court-3-player-handouts.md). **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:** Gracious, funny, generous, and **never not warm** — even when refused, even at the moment his harvest is broken. He does not rage or bluster; he does not lie. The horror of him is that he genuinely believes being gathered into his eternal court *is* a kindness. He loves a feast, loves a bargain more (a deal binds tighter than bread), and keeps perfect courtesy through everything.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* To **gather** — to draw the living green of the wood, and now the marked mortals, into his court and hold them forever. He thanked the party sincerely for sealing the drow cleft: they protected his harvest without knowing it.
- *Method — the slow harvest:* twelve years of Sela's ribbon-stitching let him reel the wood inward a thread at a time. Having gathered the *green,* he has begun gathering **people** (the mark, the summons, the audience).
- *Fears:* Little — but he can be **cheated of a catch.** The one lever in his hall is the **binding** itself, not his person.

**Relationship with the party:**
- *Played (Session 006):* Received the marked party at his court after Maelorn's gate trial, **named himself at his own table,** laid the feast, and made his offers — each one tailored and each one revealing how much he knows: **perpetual healing** for Nalith (he noticed **Quillip's berry** in her possession and honored her for not spending it), **every Harper secret** for Yinu (*"I read every letter, cipher, and dead drop that crosses my wood"* — including her cipher book), and **one undivided light** for Naan (he had read her private Lathander letter). **The party refused unanimously** — no one ate, no one promised, though no one formally insulted him either. He answered the kidnapping charge with parental framing (*"They're my kids. I feed them. Look how happy they are"*) and ignored Yinu untying ribbons and Naan freeing a thrall with Remove Curse. Then Sela ripped the binding: **he was cheated of the catch by his own courier,** scattering in open surprise. **A debt now runs in both directions** — and his knowledge of the Harpers, the letter, and the party did not necessarily scatter with him.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM — revealed in S6: the harvest was also a lid.** Sela's reading of the ribbons established that Oberon's message network was **suppressing the Vhaeraunian drow beneath Old Skull** — his words, carried through her, held the thralls of the deep in check. This was containment as a by-product of appetite, not benevolence: he was protecting his harvest. With the binding ripped and Oberon scattered, **the suppression is gone and the drow threat to Shadowdale is live.** Any future bargain with a reconstituted Oberon has an obvious price on the table: *put the lid back on.* **(DM ADDITION, established in play S6)**

> [!WARNING]
> **DM — do not stat or fight Oberon.** He is an archfey in the heart of his power; there is **no statblock by design.** The threat in his hall is the **Harvest Clock** (the contracting seam), not a boss fight. The only handle on the room is the **binding** Sela tied — and the only hand that can cut it is hers. If the party attacks, he does not deign to fight; the music plays on and the seam contracts a stage faster.

> [!NOTE]
> **Source notes**
> **Oberon, the Green Lord** is a canonical **D&D archfey**, used here as the wood-lord of the Living Wood. He is **not** named in this repo's Forgotten Realms reference extracts (`references/…/_raw/`), which are thin on named archfey — so treat the *naming of the Living Wood's lord as Oberon* as a **DM addition / DM canon decision** (confirmed by the DM), not a sourcebook citation. The harvest-court, the slow harvest, and the Feywild crossing are DM additions staged for Session 006. **(DM ADDITION)**
