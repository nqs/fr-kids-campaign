# Sela Wyndmere — Herbalist and Lay Healer

> [Back to NPC Roster](../roster.md)

- **Role:** The village's primary healer and herbalist; informal liaison with the fey of the Living Wood
- **Affiliation:** None formal; quietly aligned with Mielikki and the fey community
- **Location:** A cottage on the north edge of the village, where the settlement bleeds into the Living Wood
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A half-elf who has lived at the edge of the forest long enough that the fey treat her as a neighbor — which means she knows things no one else in the village does, and doesn't always share them.

**Appearance:** Apparent age late thirties (true age closer to sixty — half-elf). Slight, with silver-green eyes and dark hair shot through with a white streak she's had since she was a child. Her clothes always have burrs and leaf debris in them. Her cottage smells of drying herbs, river clay, and something that might be foxfire or might just be imagination. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Knowing.
- *Public face:* Gentle, unhurried, dependably competent. Shadowdalers bring her everything from fevers to grief and she takes it all with equal quiet attention.
- *Private self:* Deeply embedded in the fey community of the Living Wood in ways she doesn't fully explain. She attends things in the forest at night that she describes, if asked, as "consultations." She is not entirely sure whether the fey are her friends or her patrons, and the distinction worries her.
- *Mannerism:* Seldom gives a direct answer when an indirect one will do. Often phrases things as questions. Has a habit of pausing mid-conversation to listen to something no one else hears.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* The Living Wood to remain healthy and the fey within it to remain — she knows that if the drow threat drives the fey out, something much worse will move in.
- *Fears:* That the Feywild bleed-through is not entirely benevolent. She has been noticing strange behavior from some of the fey near Old Skull — things she can't explain as typical territorial behavior.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Secret
> A fey lord of the Living Wood, one of the older and more powerful ones, has been asking her to carry messages. She doesn't know who the messages are intended for, and she's been saying yes because she doesn't know how to say no to him. **(DM ADDITION)**


**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Friendly, quietly evaluating. She's seen enough adventurers to know they usually bring trouble for someone.
- *What she wants from the party:* If they're trustworthy, she may ask them to accompany her on one of her forest "consultations." Something has changed in the wood and she doesn't want to go alone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** What she won't say
> Who she's been carrying messages for, or to whom. (A second held card even she can't play: what the messages actually *said* — she never read them and doesn't know.)
