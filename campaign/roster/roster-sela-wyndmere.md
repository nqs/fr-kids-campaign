# Sela Wyndmere — Herbalist and Lay Healer

> [Back to NPC Roster](../roster.md)

- **Role:** The village's primary healer and herbalist; the wood-lord's unwitting courier of twelve years — now a penitent ally guiding the party into the Living Wood
- **Affiliation:** None formal; quietly aligned with Mielikki and the fey community. **As of S5 she has broken her pact with the wood-lord** (the Thorn Lord / Living Lord)
- **Location:** A cottage on the north edge of the village, where the settlement bleeds into the Living Wood; **as of S5, on the road into the wood with the party**
- **Status:** Alive — **breaking her fey pact, which will cost her the fey-granted healing powers** she has relied on for twelve years (timing/effect TBD)
- **One-line summary:** A half-elf healer who carried a fey lord's messages for twelve years without reading them — and, learning her ribbons were the stitching that bound the Feywild seam, broke the pact, gave up her powers, and turned to undoing the harm she helped enable.

**Appearance:** Apparent age late thirties; **true age ~150 (half-elf, established in S5).** Slight, with silver-green eyes and dark hair shot through with a white streak she's had since she was a child. Her clothes always have burrs and leaf debris in them. Her cottage smells of drying herbs, river clay, and something faint and silvery underneath — foxfire, or imagination. She has **delivered nearly every baby in Shadowdale** for decades. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Knowing.
- *Public face:* Gentle, unhurried, dependably competent. Shadowdalers bring her everything from fevers to grief and she takes it all with equal quiet attention.
- *Private self:* Deeply embedded in the fey community of the Living Wood in ways she doesn't fully explain. She attends things in the forest at night that she describes, if asked, as "consultations." She is not entirely sure whether the fey are her friends or her patrons, and the distinction worries her.
- *Mannerism:* Seldom gives a direct answer when an indirect one will do. Often phrases things as questions. Has a habit of pausing mid-conversation to listen to something no one else hears.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* The Living Wood to remain healthy and the fey within it to remain — she knows that if the drow threat drives the fey out, something much worse will move in.
- *Fears:* That the Feywild bleed-through is not entirely benevolent. She has been noticing strange behavior from some of the fey near Old Skull — things she can't explain as typical territorial behavior.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Secret — ~~held~~ **disclosed in S5**
> A fey lord of the Living Wood (the **Thorn Lord / Living Lord**), one of the older and more powerful ones, asked her to carry ribbon-knotted messages. **In S5 she confessed the whole arrangement to the party:** twelve years ago the wood granted her healing powers in exchange for small favors (reporting comings and goings, the town's winter stores, when Elminster was in residence), which over time became a slow harvest — the fey took children, took stores, and left droughts as "gifts." Her ribbons, she learned, were **stitching the Feywild seam** to the material plane. Breaking the pact to tell them **costs her the fey healing.** **(DM ADDITION)**


**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Friendly, quietly evaluating. She's seen enough adventurers to know they usually bring trouble for someone.
- *What she wants from the party:* If they're trustworthy, she may ask them to accompany her on one of her forest "consultations." Something has changed in the wood and she doesn't want to go alone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Still unknown (even to her)
> ~~Who she's been carrying messages for, or to whom.~~ (Disclosed in S5: the wood-lord.) **The one card even she can't play: what the messages actually *said* — she never read them and doesn't know.** This remains an open mystery into S6.

> [!NOTE]
> **Played — Session 004 (Greengrass Festival):** First on-screen contact with the party. Sela ran **the Greening** at the wood's edge: she judged Nalith's three rounds, praised her Round-1 poultice (*"This is excellent. You must be a skilled healer."*), and pinned the **gold ribbon** on her — the same moment Quillip invisibly slipped Nalith the fey berry. She also conjured the test breeze for Round 2 of the archery contest (the wind-read Kto failed). That is the full extent of played interaction: brief, warm, festival-judge contact, almost entirely with Nalith. **The rest of the party knows her only by sight and village reputation.**

> [!NOTE]
> **Played — Session 005 (*The Thorn Lord's Summons*):** Sela's arc opened in full. At the bonfire she was guarded with Dirk and Naan ("the lady said you'd come"; deflected questions about the Thorn Ward). At her cabin the next morning — caught packing a satchel "like a soldier breaking camp" — she **confessed the twelve-year pact and broke it,** knowing it would cost her the wood's healing. She **guided the party into the Living Wood,** explained the ribbon-code (two white ribbons for one message, several green for another) and was horrified to learn from Naan that the ribbons were binding the seam. She recalled having already chosen **Rellan Tessar's burial cloth** before the party brought him back alive — *"Whatever else you are, you're the kind of trouble I can work with."* She also told Fiorn the Fey primrose he named at the Greengrass contest "came straight from the Fey Realm — the Living Wood dropped that onto the table for you." **Now a penitent ally and the party's guide for the Living Wood arc.**

**Practical notes:** She can provide healing poultices, basic antitoxins, and herbal remedies. She won't charge people who genuinely can't pay. She'll also answer questions about the Living Wood — up to a point.
