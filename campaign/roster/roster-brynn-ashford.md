# Brynn Ashford — Shadowdale's Delegate to the Dales Council

> [Back to NPC Roster](../roster.md)

- **Role:** Shadowdale's working representative at the Dales Council in Ashabenford; Lady Ulphor's proxy
- **Affiliation:** Shadowdale; the Dales Council
- **Location:** Splits time between Shadowdale and Ashabenford (Mistledale). Currently in Shadowdale.
- **Status:** Alive
- **One-line summary:** A sharp-tongued young diplomat trying to punch above his weight at a table of older, better-resourced dales — and quietly resentful that he's been given the chair instead of the authority to do anything with it.

**Appearance:** Early thirties. Lean, angular face with ink-stained fingers — he writes everything down. Dresses carefully in Dalelands practical-formal: clean leather doublet, Shadowdale's thistle-and-ash badge pinned at the collar. Moves quickly and tends to perch at the edge of chairs rather than sit back in them. **(DM ADDITION)**

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Sharp.
- *Public face:* Composed, articulate, well-prepared. He knows Shadowdale's position on every agenda item before the meeting starts.
- *Private self:* Frustrated. He represents Shadowdale with genuine loyalty, but Lady Ulphor's instructions rarely extend his mandate far enough to make real commitments. He watches Archendale play both sides and Mistledale hoard its political capital, and he can't do much about either.
- *Mannerism:* Takes notes constantly — during conversations, at the tavern, apparently in his sleep. When he disagrees with something, he asks a clarifying question instead of stating the disagreement directly. This is both genuinely useful and occasionally maddening.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* To get Shadowdale taken seriously as a voice on the Council, not just a small dale that benefits from others' decisions. To eventually be trusted with a broader mandate from Lady Ulphor.
- *Fears:* That the Council will decide the free dales' best option is to seek Cormyrian protection — which would effectively end Shadowdale's independence.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Secret
> He has begun quietly building his own relationships with other delegates' staffers, gathering information Lady Ulphor didn't authorize him to gather. He tells himself it's for the dale's benefit. It might be. **(DM ADDITION)**


**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* Slightly guarded — he's used to evaluating people's usefulness before investing in them.
- *What he wants from the party:* If they have any standing with other factions (Myth Drannor, Harpers, Cormyr), he wants to know. Leverage is his primary currency.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** What he won't say
> The specific state of Council fractures he's observed — that information is his personal insurance.


**Plot hook:** If the party needs Council resources, Brynn can unlock them — but he'll extract something in return. And if his unauthorized information-gathering ever surfaces, it could embarrass Lady Ulphor badly at precisely the wrong moment. **(DM ADDITION)**
