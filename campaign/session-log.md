## Campaign Arc So Far

The party arrived in **Shadowdale** as freelance hands and, over four sessions, has become the unofficial drow-incursion task force for **Lady Addee Ulphor** and the Ashaba House. Session 001 saw them rescue militia scout **Rellan Tessar** from a Vhaeraunian cleft on Old Skull's northern flank, scarring the field officer **Zeldrazz T'orrl** and recovering a map fragment that pointed to two more staging sites. In Session 002 they reached **Site B (the Hawthorn Chimney)** only to find a **Lolthite raid party had already purged it** and collapsed the cleft from below; they killed the wounded drider left behind and confirmed two drow factions — Lolthite and Vhaeraunian — are at war beneath Old Skull. **Brynn Ashford** opened a Council-side back-channel, asking the party to bring intel to her *before* the Harpers see it.

**Session 003 closed the drow incursion arc.** The party reached **Site C** (the Half-Mask Shrine), where the Vhaeraunian field-priest **Khelziir Aun'velve** was mid-ritual to anchor a one-way surface portal. They struck from surprise, befriended the curious pixie **Quillip Burr-and-Whistle** (paid in an original song) for full intel, and **Nalith's Hold Person paralyzed both bosses at once.** Yinu's auto-critting Scorching Ray killed Khelziir; **Kto killed the paralyzed Zeldrazz with her greatsword — ending the nemesis arc.** The portal never opened. The party recovered a **portal-anchor pendant** from the priest.

**Session 004** brought them home for the **Greengrass Festival.** They debriefed **Brynn first** (at Orvyn Tal's bookshop) and then **Lady Ulphor** at the Ashaba House, surrendering the pendant — revealed to be one half of a paired **twin-stone**, its counterpart in the Underdark — to Ulphor's vault (200 gp to Naan, who held it; 600 gp group reward). At the festival the party swept the contests: **Fiorn** won the bardic showcase (drawing a touring offer from the famous elf-bard **Helbrin**), **Yinu** won the arcane-skills challenge (prompting **Orvyn Tal to openly reveal his Harper affiliation** and promise her a gift), **Nalith** won the Greening (and glimpsed **Quillip** again, who left her a fey token), **Naan** performed the sapling-blessing to bloom, and **Kto** dueled the now-recovered **Rellan Tessar** at archery. **Corwick Helm**, the former rival, was openly warm.

**Session 005 (*The Thorn Lord's Summons*)** opened on the deferred Greengrass bonfire and brought a new face: **Dirk** (Ari), a weathered, middle-aged orc fisherman living on a raft moored on the Ashaba, who joined the party. The bonfire was interrupted when **Maelorn, the Thorn Ward**, rode out of the treeline on a great stag and delivered a summons to the four *marked* PCs (Fiorn, Yinu, Naan, Nalith; **Kto absent**): the Living Wood's lord expects them at his court **in two days** — *"Do not disappoint me by dying."* The next morning **Lady Ulphor** confirmed the Thorn Ward's appearance is unprecedented, declined to pay (this is the party's own peril, a compulsion they cannot safely ignore — an Arcana read confirmed the mark is an **enchantment-school compulsion**), outfitted them with mundane gear, and handed **Naan** a private letter inviting her to study at the **Temple of Lathander in Highmoon**. **Yinu** collected the Harper gift alone at **Orvyn Tal's** shop — a cipher book, *The Cypher and Glyph Sign of the Northern Sword Coast*, framed as *"a key, not a recruitment."* The party then collected the healer **Sela Wyndmere**, who **broke her twelve-year pact** with the wood's lord: she confessed she had carried his ribbon-knotted messages for twelve years (never reading them) and that her ribbons were the **stitching binding the Feywild seam** to the material plane — losing her fey-granted healing powers by telling them. She guided the party into the **Living Wood**, where a skill challenge (no combat) revealed the cause of the blight: the Feywild seam is **collapsing inward toward the center of the wood**, recalling the fey like water down a drain — they are not fleeing the drow but being **recalled**. The session **cut before the party reached the lord's court**; the audience is deferred to Session 006.

The drow staging sites are all closed, but the **Vhaeraunians will retaliate** once they discover Site C fell. Open threads heading into Session 006: the **Thorn Lord's audience itself** (two days out; the party magically compelled to attend), **Sela's fate** now that she has broken her pact (and the unread contents of her twelve years of messages), **Naan's competing Lathander invitation** to Highmoon, **Yinu's Harper cipher book** (read before the next Harper meeting), **Helbrin's road-tour offer to Fiorn**, what **Quillip's fey token** does (still unused — Quillip did not appear in S5), and the magic Yinu sensed in Orvyn's back room. The Lolthites still do not know the party exists.
