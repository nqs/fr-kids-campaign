## Campaign Arc So Far

The party arrived in **Shadowdale** as freelance hands and, over four sessions, became the unofficial drow-incursion task force for **Lady Addee Ulphor** and the Ashaba House — closing all three Vhaeraunian staging sites and killing field-officer Zeldrazz T'orrl. A Greengrass festival interlude brought new alliances before **the Thorn Ward Maelorn** delivered a fey compulsion summons on behalf of the Living Wood's lord. Sessions 005–006 resolved the Feywild arc: the party refused Oberon's tailored paradises, and **Sela Wyndmere** ripped his binding ribbon, scattering him into butterflies and breaking the suppression layer over the Vhaeraunian drow beneath Old Skull — launching the party out of the Bright Court Beneath to an unknown destination.

**Session 007 opened a new arc in a new region.** Sela's portal ejected the party into a desolate salt marsh on a long-dried seabed, where an active lighthouse was luring victims to feed a **Brine Fiend** demon — its sentient compulsion beam showing each PC the thing they most desired. The party pushed through (Yinu dispelling Naan; Fiorn consumed/transported by the mechanism when he cast Fly), killed the summoner **Veyra** in the lantern room, and dissolved the Brine Fiend as a consequence. Freed lighthouse keeper **Tamsin Reed** extinguished the light; the party seized Veyra's ledger — naming New Sharshel nobles who funded the ritual — and agreed to escort the stranded Embergull survivors the nine miles to **New Sharshel**.

## Recent Session — Session 007 Quick Reference
**Title:** The Lighthouse at the Edge of the Salt · **Full log:** [session 7 - log](../sessions/session%207/session%207%20-%20log.md)

- **Players present:** Caitlin (Kto), Fox (Fiorn), Sev (Yinu), Piper (Nalith), Lilli (Naan). **Ari (Dirk) and Kaila (Tyvlex) absent.** Campaign has left the Dalelands per table decision; Shadowdale threads are geographically distant but unresolved.
- **The salt marsh:** Party arrived at night in a flat, dried seabed scattered with stranded ships. The ocean retreated *decades* ago — a pre-existing mystery, not tied to the party's arrival. No magic detectable (Yinu Arcana 21 — oddly empty). Nearest landmark: the active lighthouse ~1 mile west.
- **Lighthouse compulsion:** The rotating beam showed each PC the thing they most desire (Fiorn — his stolen soul; Yinu — Elminster's instruction; Naan — inner peace; Nalith — her dead mother; Kto — divine recognition) and compelled certainty that the desired thing was inside. Yinu Dispelled Naan successfully; Fiorn cast Fly and was consumed/transported by the sentient mechanism — fate unknown at session end.
- **The Embergull camp:** ~10 survivors camped at a stranded hull; keeper **Oren** warned the party the lighthouse kills people and confirmed **Tamsin Reed** was still alive inside. Party pressed on under compulsion.
- **Combat — lantern room:** Villain **Veyra** was using the beam to lure victims for a **Brine Fiend** emerging through a summoning circle. Kto (Cloud of Daggers, longbow) and Yinu (Lightning Bolt) were decisive; Veyra died round 2 and the Brine Fiend dissolved automatically. Yinu reached ~6 HP after the Fiend's bite and Veyra's Scorching Ray.
- **Aftermath:** Tamsin Reed (herself compelled to keep the light running) extinguished the lighthouse. Four chained prisoners freed. Yinu looted Veyra's **ledger** (naming New Sharshel noble patrons of the ritual) and ~150 gp. Compulsion lifted for all remaining PCs.
- **Session ended:** Party preparing to escort Embergull survivors ~9 miles to **New Sharshel**. Fiorn's fate, the ledger's implications, and the ocean-retreat mystery are the immediate open threads.
