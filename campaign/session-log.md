## Campaign Arc So Far

The party arrived in **Shadowdale** as freelance hands and, over four sessions, became the unofficial drow-incursion task force for **Lady Addee Ulphor** and the Ashaba House — closing all three Vhaeraunian staging sites and killing field-officer Zeldrazz T'orrl. A Greengrass festival interlude brought new alliances before **the Thorn Ward Maelorn** delivered a fey compulsion summons on behalf of the Living Wood's lord. Sessions 005–006 resolved the Feywild arc: the party refused Oberon's tailored paradises, and **Sela Wyndmere** ripped his binding ribbon, scattering him into butterflies and breaking the suppression layer over the Vhaeraunian drow beneath Old Skull — launching the party out of the Bright Court Beneath to an unknown destination.

**Session 007 opened a new arc in a new region.** The portal dropped the party on the dried-up bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars in Impiltur, where a lighthouse beam showed each PC what they most desired and compelled them toward it. Yinu tried to fly there and the light took her — she woke chained in the lantern room beside four prisoners. Nalith dispelled keeper **Tamsin Reed**'s compulsion at the door; in the lantern room the party killed the summoner **Veyra** (Yinu's *Lightning Bolt*), the **Brine Fiend** collapsed, and Tamsin shut the light off. They took Veyra's ledger — naming New Sarshel backers who paid for the summoning — and set out to escort the stranded *Embergull* survivors nine miles east to **New Sarshel**.

## Recent Session — Session 007 Quick Reference
**Title:** The Lighthouse on Dry Land · **Full log:** [session 7 - log](../sessions/session%207/session%207%20-%20log.md)

- **Players:** Lilli (Naan), Piper (Nalith), Kaitlyn (Kto), Sev (Yinu), Fox (Fiorn — left partway through). **Absent:** Ari (Dirk), Kaila (Tyvlex). **Juniper did not attend.** The campaign has left the Dalelands by table decision.
- **The landing:** night, a dry salt pan of stranded ships; the sea has been gone for decades (Nalith, Nature). No detectable magic. A working lighthouse ~1 mile off.
- **The compulsion:** the beam showed each PC their deepest want (Fiorn — his soul; Yinu — Elminster's teaching; Naan — a quiet white room; Nalith — Christmas with her late mother; Kto — her god's recognition) and compelled them toward the tower.
- **The *Embergull* camp:** keeper **Oren** warned them the light eats people and that **Tamsin Reed** still keeps it.
- **Yinu taken:** she cast *Fly*; the light took her and she woke **chained in the lantern room** beside four prisoners, then Misty Stepped free.
- **Tamsin:** Nalith's *Dispel Magic* freed the keeper at the door; she led them up and unlocked the lantern room.
- **The fight:** Kto's longbow, Naan's *Cloud of Daggers* and Yinu's Shadowspawn; **Yinu's *Lightning Bolt* killed Veyra** in round 2 and the Brine Fiend collapsed into salt water. No PC dropped.
- **Aftermath:** Tamsin shut the light off; compulsion ended. Four prisoners freed. Loot: Veyra's **ledger** (New Sarshel backers who funded the summoning — reason unknown) and ~15 coins.
- **Session ended:** setting out to escort the survivors nine miles east to **New Sarshel**. Kto wants a church there.
