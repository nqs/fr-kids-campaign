# World Overview

## Setting Name
The Forgotten Realms — Campaign based in **Shadowdale**, The Dalelands

## Elevator Pitch
The Dalelands are a patchwork of fiercely independent farming communities strung along
the River Ashaba and its tributaries, caught between the elf-realm of Myth Drannor to
the east, the expanding merchant empire of Sembia to the south, and the looming shadow
of Netheril to the north. Shadowdale — the "Fey Dale" — is a relative island of peace:
a small, unwalled village nestled beneath the granite dome of Old Skull where the North
Ride crosses the Ashaba. Fey have flooded its forests since Mystra's fall thinned the
border with the Feywild; drow are rumored to be stirring again beneath Old Skull; and
the great wizard Elminster sits at the old Silverhand farm, pipe in hand, very tired and
not much interested in helping anyone.

## Tone & Genre
- **Overall tone:** Grounded heroic fantasy with creeping darkness — the Realms are
  fraying at the edges, and even a quiet dale like Shadowdale has real threats underneath
  its pastoral surface.
- **Violence level:** Cinematic — consequences are real, but not gratuitously grim.
- **Magic feel:** Present but not mundane; the Spellplague's aftermath means magic
  sometimes behaves unexpectedly, and old enchantments carry scars.
- **Humor:** Present — Shadowdale's locals are wry, and Elminster is reliably
  unhelpful in an entertaining way.

## The Big Premise
Shadowdale is peaceful — on the surface. Beneath Old Skull, the old drow passages
are stirring again. The fey flooding the Living Wood bring their own politics and
dangers. And the Dales Council in Ashabenford is struggling to hold the remaining
free dales together against Sembians, Netherese, and each other. The party arrives
at the intersection of all of it.

## Cosmology

### The Planes
- **Material Plane:** Faerûn, specifically the Dalelands.
- **Feywild:** The border is unusually weak along the River Ashaba in Shadowdale —
  fey bleed through here, and the Living Wood has a distinctly otherworldly quality.
- **Shadowfell / Underdark:** Old Skull descends into the Underdark. Drow are
  whispered to be using the old passages again since Elminster's power has diminished.
- **Astral / Upper Planes:** Distant for now.

### Magic in the World
Magic functions but carries the scars of the Spellplague. Spellcasters sometimes
experience brief, unexpected surges or dead moments — nothing mechanically punishing,
but atmospheric. Wild magic zones exist near the Darkwatch and along the deepest
parts of Old Skull. The fey presence along the Ashaba makes divination and
illusion magic feel slightly more potent in the dale.

## History & Timeline

### Ancient Era
- Old Skull served for centuries as a drow gateway to the surface; Shadowdale was
  periodically controlled by drow raiders from the Underdark.
- The dale was shaped by legendary figures: Elminster settled here in ancient times;
  the Silverhand farm bears his old protégé's name.

### Recent History (last ~100 years)
- **Mystra's death / the Spellplague** devastated the weave of magic across Faerûn.
  Elminster, as one of her Chosen, lost enormous power — he remains in Shadowdale,
  immortal and formidably intelligent, but no longer the archmage who could reshape
  nations.
- **Lord Azalar Falconhand** led a wave of benevolent fey into Shadowdale, drawn by
  the Ashaba's unusual concentration of geomagical energy. Twenty years ago he
  resigned the lordship.
- **Lady Addee Ulphor** gained the Pendant of Ashaba and has ruled as Lady of
  Shadowdale since. Her tenure has been quiet but increasingly tense as drow
  activity beneath Old Skull is reported again.
- The southern Dales (Scardale, Featherdale, Tasseldale) have fallen to Sembia and
  its Netherese overlords. The free dales — Shadowdale among them — cling to
  independence through the Dales Council at Ashabenford.

### Current Year / Era
The Post-Spellplague era; the exact year is left to the DM to set. The "news of the
day" is: Netheril is still expanding, the Dales Council is fractious, and something
is wrong under Old Skull.

## Current State of the World

### Major Powers (relevant to Shadowdale)
- **Shadowdale** — independent, led by Lady Addee Ulphor; population ~1,000 in the
  village, ~10,000 across the dale.
- **Myth Drannor** — elven city-state to the east; an important ally and cultural
  anchor for the free Dales.
- **The Dales Council (Ashabenford)** — the governing body of the free Dales;
  increasingly desperate.
- **Sembia / Netheril** — southern and northern threats pressing on the free Dales.
- **The Harpers** — active in the Dalelands; Shadowdale has historically been
  a Harper-friendly town.

### Active Conflicts
- **The Old Skull problem** — drow movement in the tunnels beneath the promontory
  has resumed. Local militia have found evidence but no one in the village wants
  to say it out loud yet.
- **Fey politics in the Living Wood** — not all fey are benevolent; territorial
  disputes and glamour-mischief are becoming a nuisance for farmers on the forest edge.
- **Dales Council fractures** — pressure from Sembia is splitting loyalties;
  Archendale in particular is playing both sides.

> [!hook] The "News of the Day"
> Ordinary Shadowdalers are talking about: a family whose goats have gone missing
> near the forest edge (almost certainly fey mischief, possibly something worse);
> a merchant caravan from Mistledale that arrived two days late with a frightened
> driver who won't say what delayed them; and the fact that Elminster's smoke hasn't
> been seen rising from the Silverhand farm chimney in a week.

## Themes
1. **Independence vs. Safety** — the free dales are proud of their autonomy, but
   freedom is expensive when empires are pressing in from every direction.
2. **Old Powers, Diminished** — Elminster, the old Harper networks, Shadowdale's
   ancient magical defenses — the things that used to protect this place are less
   reliable than they were.
3. **The World Below** — the Underdark is never far from the surface in the Dalelands.
   Old Skull is a literal door. What's coming up?
