# Session 003 — The Half-Mask Shrine

*Tier 2 · 5 PCs at Level 5 (→ 6 on completion) · Old Skull's northern flank → Site C*

**Status:** ✅ Played

The party crossed the granite shoulder to **Site C**, the last open Vhaeraunian cleft, and befriended a curious pixie, **Quillip Burr-and-Whistle**, paying for her intel with one of Fiorn's original songs. They dropped through a ceiling gap into the shrine-camp, wiped out the antechamber scouts from surprise, and bypassed the spider pen entirely to reach the shrine where the field-priest **Khelziir Aun'velve** stood mid-ritual beside **Zeldrazz T'orrl**. Nalith's *Hold Person* paralyzed both bosses at once; Yinu's auto-critting *Scorching Ray* killed Khelziir, and **Kto finished the paralyzed Zeldrazz with her greatsword — ending the nemesis arc**. The ritual portal never opened, the party recovered the **portal-anchor pendant**, and the drow-incursion arc closed.
