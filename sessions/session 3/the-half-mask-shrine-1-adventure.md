## Adventure Summary

Site B is rubble. Site C is not. The party wakes at the collapsed Hawthorn Chimney with the smell of cold ash on their bedrolls and a four-hour traverse of granite shoulder ahead of them. At the cleft they meet two unexpected things — a drow watcher in a pine with a brass horn at her belt, and a curious pixie named **Quillip Burr-and-Whistle** who has been counting watch rotations from a knothole for two days and is willing to trade her notes for the right kind of fey-style payment.

Inside the cleft is the same shrine-camp from last session's intelligence: an antechamber with two scouts, a spider pen, and a shrine where **Khelziir Aun'velve** stands at the basalt altar mid-ritual, with **Zeldrazz T'orrl** flanking him. The ritual countdown is now **two rounds** — the priest has had an extra night to work. And somewhere on round four, if the party stalls, a **Lolthite raid party** comes up the Underdark passage and crashes the shrine from behind.

The session ends with the portal anchor either smashed, stolen for study, or — if the party is clever enough to leave it running — turned into a one-way Harper listening glyph. Then the party walks back to Shadowdale and reports to Brynn or Orvyn first, and Lady Ulphor tells them what it cost her to put Captain Helm back together.

## Scene 1 — Cold Camp at the Hawthorn Chimney

### Setup
Dawn. Pale light through the hawthorn boughs at Site B. The cleft is collapsed (the drider's death-throes from last session, plus the Lolthite charges that purged the camp), and a film of mist clings to the granite. The party slept here on the assumption that nobody would come looking for them in the wreckage of someone else's purge. They were right. Now it's morning.

> [!NOTE]
> **Read-aloud:** Read Aloud
> The hawthorn berries above you are pale as dead pearls. The cleft mouth is gone — twenty feet of granite collapsed in on itself, the chimney throat sealed. Three of your bedrolls are scattered around a fire pit that didn't get lit last night. The wind smells of cold stone and, faintly, of something that was burning two days ago and isn't anymore.

### What This Scene Is For
- An inventory beat after a long rest. **Yinu has all spell slots back.** Anyone who burned daily resources at Site B is fresh. Make sure that lands.
- **Brynn's letter.** Loric finds a folded paper tucked into his pack he doesn't remember packing. It is in Brynn Ashford's hand, sealed with the thistle-and-ash sigil of Shadowdale. *"Whatever you find at the third site, I want a sketch and a number before Orvyn does. I am not asking you to lie to him. I am asking you to choose the door you knock on first."* No signature. The letter is Loric's to share, sit on, or burn.
- **Decision beat.** Push for Site C now (4 hr overland) or scout the surrounding granite for any Lolthite track-sign. Either choice is fine; the Northern Traverse skill check in Scene 2 takes the choice into account.

### DM Notes — Cold Camp Tone
- Yinu's *Fireball* exhaustion from last session is fully recovered after the long rest. No mechanical penalty.
- **Aelinor Wains has retired** — the militia reserve is unavailable this session. Corwick said no, and Lady Ulphor agreed. The party is alone on the granite.
- Don't push the letter. If Loric pockets it without comment, that's also a story.

## Scene 2 — The Northern Traverse

### The Trek
Approximately four hours overland from the collapsed Site B to the cleft mouth on Old Skull's northern flank. The route skirts the granite shoulder above the Living Wood — windier, colder, and more exposed than the southern approach last session. No fey wards to navigate up here; the Living Wood thins out before the bare stone.

### Skill Check — Pathfinding
A single **Survival or Nature DC 13** check, made by the lead PC. (No skill challenge — this is a transition, not a setpiece.)
- **Success:** the party arrives at the cleft mouth in early afternoon, with daylight and time to scout.
- **Failure:** they arrive at dusk, footsore. Each PC starts the next encounter at half their hit-dice pool unless they short-rest at the cleft (which Khelziir's spider will hear).

### Optional — Lolthite Track-Sign
Anyone who *additionally* takes a **DC 14 Survival** check on the way up may notice three sets of overlapping scuff-marks in the lichen on a granite ledge — boot-edges, recent, moving *uphill* and *toward Site C from a different angle than the party*. This is the **only forewarning** of the Lolthite Crash later. If the party flags it, they may set up watchposts, leave a trip-wire, or simply mention it to Quillip. If they miss it, the Crash arrives with full surprise.

### DM Notes — Traverse Tone
- Quiet and exposed. No combat. Use this scene for character beats — Brynn's letter, Yinu's spellbook, Fiorn cracking up the marching order.
- If the party has a Druid, Nalith may notice the wood is *too* quiet on the northern shoulder. The fey have withdrawn here as well — confirm Sela Wyndmere's report.

## Scene 3 — The Watcher Above & The Knothole

### The Cleft Mouth
The same cleft mouth from last session's intelligence. A vertical fissure 6 ft. wide and 10 ft. tall in the granite shoulder, breathing cold spider-musk air. Spider-silk threads still cross the opening at ankle and chest height — Khelziir's tripwire chime.

### Encounter — The Watcher
A single **Drow Scout** is perched in a wind-stunted pine sixty feet above the cleft mouth, brass signal horn slung at her belt. **DC 17 Perception** to spot her before she spots the party. If she sees them first, she **blows the horn before being killed** — Khelziir's cell goes to alert and the antechamber loses its surprise round.

### The Pixie in the Knothole

Lower on the same pine, in a knothole the size of a thumb-tip, **Quillip Burr-and-Whistle** has been hiding for two days, taking pinecone-stack notes on Khelziir's cell. She is invisible by default. She **reveals herself the moment a PC says or does something interesting** — Druidic spoken aloud (Nalith), a shapechange (Fiorn), a true name, a fragment of a real song, an honest greeting addressed to the empty pine.

### Quillip's Bargain
Once visible, Quillip introduces herself in rhyming couplets and offers a deal. She'll trade **one** of the following for **one** fey-style payment:

| Offering | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Intel** | Watch rotations, the spider's alarm-shriek behavior, Khelziir's mid-ritual status, Zeldrazz's position. Removes the antechamber's surprise penalty even if the horn sounds. |
| **Diversion** | Quillip casts *phantasmal force* and lures the Watcher down the trunk to an empty hollow. The Watcher is removed from the encounter without a horn-blow. |
| **Banked Boon** | A single-use casting of **one** of *polymorph*, *confusion*, or *sleep* (Quillip's choice from the three named) on a target the party identifies within 24 hours. She arrives invisibly and casts on cue. |

### What She'll Accept as Payment
- A **true secret** about a PC, told plainly (the secret must be one Quillip couldn't easily learn elsewhere)
- A **song** never sung in the Living Wood before (Fiorn or Nalith — Performance check is roleplay, not a die roll)
- A **true name** offered freely — the giver's, or a known third party's
- **A page from Yinu's spellbook**, copied for her in fresh ink (one cantrip or one 1st-level spell — Quillip won't read magic, she just likes the shapes)
- A **binding promise** — *"Speak my name aloud the next time you stand under a hawthorn"* or similar fey-style geas; cost comes due exactly as stated
- A **token from far away** — anything from outside the Dales the party has carried with them

### Bargain Rules (DM)
- All bargains are honored fey-style. The boon arrives reliably; the cost comes due exactly as stated, no twist. **Quillip is not a hag.**
- If the party stiffs her, she returns in a later session demanding payment with interest. Do not skip this.
- If brushed off or threatened, she vanishes (Superior Invisibility). She will **not** tip off the Vhaeraunians — drow are distasteful to her — but the offered intel and boon are gone for this session.
- **She will go very still and leave the conversation** if asked directly about Sela Wyndmere's "unnamed fey lord." Don't volunteer this; only react if the party probes it.


### Approach Options to the Cleft (Once the Watcher Is Handled)
- **Frontal stealth** — group Stealth DC 13 to enter without triggering the silk
- **Cut the wires** — Sleight of Hand DC 12 to cut without ringing the chime
- **Climb above** — DC 15 Athletics, drops party into the antechamber from the ceiling (no silk, surprise round if quiet)

## Scene 4 — The Antechamber

### Chamber 1 — The Antechamber
A 30 × 30 ft. natural cavern with two bedroll piles, a cookfire smoldering in the center, and a weapon rack against the east wall holding three hand crossbows and a sheaf of bolts. **2× Drow Scouts** are on watch — one fletching, one dozing.

- If the party kept surprise (or bought it from Quillip), both can drop in the first round.
- If the horn sounded, both are crossbow-ready and one will sprint for the Shrine to warn Khelziir on initiative count 10.
- The cookfire is **lit and tended** — kicking it over scatters embers and opens line of sight from the antechamber into the Spider Pen's silk curtain (Scene 5).

### What the Antechamber Tells the Party
- The two scouts are wearing **standard Vhaeraun half-masks**. Khelziir's silver one is in the Shrine. The reinforcements that step through the portal (if it opens) wear plain black — they're not from this cell.
- One bedroll has a journal in Undercommon: a **two-day count of attempts to consecrate the anchor**. Yinu can read it (Arcana DC 12) and confirm: *the ritual is closer to completion than last session's intel suggested.* Hence the 2-round countdown.

## Scene 5 — The Spider Pen

### Chamber 2 — The Spider Pen
A 20 × 40 ft. side chamber separated from the antechamber by a curtain of spider-silk. **1× Giant Spider** is chained to the wall — Khelziir's pet sentinel.

- If the silk curtain is disturbed, the spider **shrieks** (a Vhaeraunian-trained alarm). Audible throughout the dungeon.
- Cutting the chain releases the spider; it does not distinguish friend from foe.
- The pen's far wall has a **second crawl-tunnel** sloping down into the dark — this is the route the Lolthite raid party will use in Scene 7. The party can spot it on **DC 14 Perception** while in the pen and may attempt to block, ward, or trap it (a trip-wire, *spike growth*, *entangle*, or simply Kto with a longbow on overwatch). **A blocked tunnel delays the Crash by one round but does not prevent it.**

## Scene 6 — The Shrine

### Chamber 3 — The Shrine
A 40 × 50 ft. chamber. A black basalt slab altar at the back, draped with a Vhaeraunian half-mask banner. A **partially-consecrated portal anchor** sits at the chamber's center: a circle of obsidian shards 8 ft. across, half-inscribed with Undercommon glyphs in luminescent silver ink. Khelziir stands at the altar, mid-ritual; Zeldrazz flanks him. Black candles burn on the altar with greenish flames.

> [!NOTE]
> **Read-aloud:** Read Aloud
> The chamber opens into a vault of cold black stone. At the back, an altar of polished basalt, and standing at it, a slim drow in robes the color of dried blood, his silver half-mask catching the green candle-flame as he turns. To his right, scarred across the cheek, a familiar face — Zeldrazz T'orrl, who sees you, and smiles like a man who has been waiting.

### The Boss Encounter

**Khelziir Aun'velve — Drow Priest of Vhaeraun.** Full statline on the combat tracker. He will not break formation from the altar — finishing the ritual matters more than his own survival. **R1:** *spirit guardians* + bonus-action *shield of faith*. **R2:** *silence* on the loudest caster (likely Yinu or Loric). **R3:** *spiritual weapon* (a black blade) and continues consecrating.

**Zeldrazz T'orrl — Returning Antagonist.** Drow Elite Warrior reskin. **He has personally targeted Kto** (the PC who delivered the killing-attempt blow last session, confirmed in S1) and will open with poisoned attacks against her. He is protective of Khelziir and will interpose if the priest is engaged in melee.

### The Ritual Countdown — 2 Rounds
The countdown is **2 rounds** when the party enters the Shrine (down from 3 last session — Khelziir has had an extra night). Each round Khelziir is alive, conscious, and not silenced or stunned, the countdown ticks down by one. If it reaches zero, the **portal anchor activates** and **4× Vhaeraunian Drow reinforcements** step through on the next initiative. The encounter escalates from Hard to Deadly.

The party can stop the countdown by:
- Killing Khelziir
- Knocking him unconscious
- Silencing him for a full round (he can step out — only delays)
- Destroying the half-finished anchor itself: **AC 12, 40 HP, vulnerable to bludgeoning** — but striking it provokes opportunity attacks from anyone in melee with the striker, and Khelziir treats it as an attack on himself

## Scene 7 — The Lolthite Crash

### Trigger
**Round 4 of the Shrine fight, OR if the party stalls at the cleft for more than two rounds without engaging.** Use whichever comes first. If the party blocked the crawl-tunnel in the Spider Pen, delay one round.

### What Happens
**3× Lolthite Drow Raiders** burst up out of the crawl-tunnel into the Spider Pen and from there into the antechamber. Spider-emblem shields, webbed cloaks, two curved blades each. They are not subtle. They are here to close another surface mouth.

### Faction Behavior
- **The Vhaeraunians prioritize the Lolthites over the party.** Khelziir will break ritual to *bane* or *spirit guardians* the Lolthites; Zeldrazz interposes between the Lolthite line and the altar. Any Vhaeraunian reinforcements that have already stepped through the portal turn on the Lolthites first.
- **The Lolthites prioritize the anchor over the party.** They are here to smash it. If the anchor is still intact when they arrive, they ignore the PCs to bullrush the altar.
- **The party can take advantage of the chaos** — slip out, finish off Khelziir while the Lolthites engage Zeldrazz, smash the anchor themselves under cover, or simply withdraw with whatever they've already grabbed.

### What This Means (DM-facing)
**The Lolthites learn the surface party exists.** Up until now, the Lolthites have only known about Vhaeraunians and dales militia. After the Crash, regardless of the encounter's outcome, the Lolthite enclave under Old Skull has a description of the party. This is a **major loose end** — flag it for the session log.

If the Crash *doesn't* trigger (party finishes the Shrine in three rounds), the Lolthites still come up the crawl-tunnel, but they arrive to find dead Vhaeraunians and no party. They do not yet know the surface party exists. **Reward speed.**

| 3× Lolthite Drow Raider | 1 | 600 |
| **Total** | — | **~600 (pressure-valve, not a fresh encounter)** |

## Scene 8 — Three Endings

How the party leaves the Shrine determines the political shape of the next two sessions.

### Ending 1 — Smash the Anchor (Loud Win)
The portal collapses inward in a thunderclap of cold air. Khelziir's cell is permanently shut down; the cleft is no longer usable as a Vhaeraunian staging site. Lady Ulphor is delighted; the militia get credit publicly; the Vhaeraunians know the surface knows.

- **Reputation:** +Lady Ulphor, +Corwick (publicly — see Scene 9), +militia
- **Loose end:** A furious Vhaeraunian retaliation arrives in 2–3 weeks. Form unknown to the party.

### Ending 2 — Steal the Anchor for Study (Quiet Win)
Requires a successful **DC 16 Arcana** check by Yinu or Loric to disrupt the consecration without triggering the portal. The anchor goes back to Lady Ulphor, who hands it to Orvyn Tal for Harper analysis.

- **Reputation:** +Harpers significantly, +Lady Ulphor privately, neutral publicly
- **Loose end:** Vhaeraunian retaliation still arrives in 2–3 weeks; they don't know the anchor was taken cleanly until they try to use it from the other side

### Ending 3 — Turn It Into a Feed (Clever Win)
If a PC succeeds on a **DC 18 Religion or Arcana** check after Khelziir falls, they realize the half-finished anchor can be left running as a one-way listening glyph into the Vhaeraunian command structure. Orvyn Tal pays handsomely (**+500 gp Harper bounty**) and offers Harper recruitment for this.

- **Reputation:** +Harpers heavily; the Vhaeraunians don't know they're being listened to — yet
- **Loose end:** Vhaeraunian retaliation still arrives in 2–3 weeks; the Harpers will hear it coming first

### Withdrawing Without an Ending
If the party flees mid-fight, the Shrine continues consecrating overnight and the portal opens fully. The Lolthite Crash (whether it triggered or not) will eventually purge the cell — but at the cost of Lolthites holding the surface anchor, which is **strictly worse** for Shadowdale than either faction holding it. Lady Ulphor will not say this out loud. She will pay the scouting fee and not the bonus.

## Scene 9 — Debrief at the Ashaba House

### Setup
Late afternoon, the day after the cleft. Lady Ulphor's working room. Brynn Ashford is present and taking notes; Orvyn Tal arrives later, summoned or not depending on the party's earlier choice.

### The "Who Reports First" Beat
- **If the party went to Brynn first** (per his letter): Brynn is satisfied. He shares one piece of Council intelligence in return — *"Mistledale is going to formally raise the question of Cormyrian protection at next month's Council. Lady Ulphor is going to vote against. I would like to know whether you think she's right."* This is a future-session hook; no answer required tonight.
- **If the party went to Orvyn first** (or both, transparently): Orvyn warms perceptibly. He drops his cover — admits Harper affiliation if not already known — and offers the party a standing dead-drop arrangement at the bookshop. Brynn's expression goes flat for a moment and recovers. He will remember.
- **If the party reported only to Lady Ulphor:** both Brynn and Orvyn will follow up privately within the week. The party can defer the choice — but Brynn's letter does not get easier the longer it sits.

### The Corwick Reconciliation

Lady Ulphor mentions, almost in passing, that **Captain Helm came to her two nights ago**. They spoke for an hour. He has accepted a private apology from Fiorn (which Fiorn did not actually offer — Lady Ulphor brokered it) and will continue as militia captain. He is **less hostile now**, but the party should understand: *"This cost me something to arrange. I would prefer not to have to do it again."*

She doesn't say what it cost her. **(DM: it cost her a private commitment to Brynn — she has authorized him to make a small Council commitment on Shadowdale's behalf without prior approval. Brynn knows. Fiorn doesn't.)**

### Payments
- **300 gp scouting payment** (the half Lady Ulphor would have paid regardless of outcome)
- **300 gp neutralize bonus** (paid only if Site C is closed, by any of the three endings — *not* paid if the party withdrew without resolving the anchor)
- Sela Wyndmere's herbal kit replenished (3× healing poultices, 2× antitoxin) for the next outing — already paid for
- Khelziir's silver half-mask: **Orvyn Tal 200 gp**, or **Lady Ulphor 100 gp** (party chooses the buyer)
- Anchor disposition payments per the chosen ending (see Scene 8 / Treasure section)

### Milestone
**Advance the party to Level 6 at session end.** Inform players before they leave the table — the level-up can be done between sessions.

### DM Notes — Debrief Tone
- Lady Ulphor is warmer than at the briefing. She sits when she speaks, instead of standing.
- Brynn is sharper than at the briefing. He is taking more notes than usual.
- Orvyn, if present, is happier than the party has ever seen him. He buys the round at the Old Skull Inn afterward.
