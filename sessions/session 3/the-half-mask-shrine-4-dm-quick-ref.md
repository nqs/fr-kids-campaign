## Ritual Countdown

**Starts at 2** when the party enters the Shrine. Ticks down by 1 each round Khelziir is alive, conscious, and **not** silenced or stunned.

| How to stop it | Caveat |
|---|---|
| Kill or KO Khelziir | — |
| Silence him for a **full round** | He can step out of the *silence* zone to delay (only delays) |
| Destroy the anchor (AC 12, HP 40, vuln. bludgeoning) | Provokes OAs; Khelziir treats it as an attack on himself |

**Countdown hits 0 →** 4× Vhaeraunian drow step through next initiative (init 10). Hard → Deadly.

- **Trigger:** Round 4 of the Shrine fight, OR party stalled at the cleft >2 rounds without engaging (whichever is first).
- **Delay:** Blocked Spider Pen tunnel → +1 round.

*One boon, one cost per encounter. Quillip picks the cost from the tier column.*

| Boon | Effect | Cost tier |

| Intel: Watcher schedule | Blind side, walk arc, horn placement | *Cheap* — a true secret (small), a verse of a real song, or a token from outside the Dales |
