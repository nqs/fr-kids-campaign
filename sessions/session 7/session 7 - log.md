## Session 007 — The Lighthouse at the Edge of the Salt
**Date played:**
**Location in-world:** Unknown salt marsh (dried seabed); the Embergull wreck camp; the lighthouse of Tamsin Reed; nearby road toward New Sharshel [spelling uncertain — transcript renders as "Sharshel" or similar; DM to confirm]
**Players present:** Caitlin (Kto), Fox (Fiorn), Sev (Yinu), Piper (Nalith), Lilli (Naan)
**Absent:** Ari (Dirk), Kaila (Tyvlex) [Tyvlex/Kaila not mentioned; Juniper — a prospective new player — did not attend]
**Session ended at:** Veyra dead, Brine Fiend dissolved, four prisoners freed, lighthouse light extinguished by Tamsin Reed; party preparing to escort survivors the ~9 miles to New Sharshel

### What Happened

**Arrival — the Salt Marsh**

- Party is ejected from Oberon's realm via the portal Sela opened. DM notes: per table feedback, the campaign is leaving the Dalelands; the portal dumps the party into a new region.
- They land at night in a flat, desolate salt marsh. Broken ships lie scattered across dried seabed. Ground is mushy, salty wet sand. Twilight. ~1 mile west: an active lighthouse. ~0.5 miles toward the lighthouse: a small campfire light near a ship hull.
- No water visible; the sea is miles away.
- **Nature check (Nalith/Piper — rolled 20, +2 = effectively 22):** The water has been gone from this area for *decades*. This was not a recent catastrophic event. Salt vegetation is just beginning to take hold. [DM confirms: ocean retreat is a long-standing mystery, not tied to the party's arrival.]
- **Arcana check (Yinu — 21):** No lingering magic detectable. DM notes this is oddly empty; magic is usually present everywhere.
- Party resources: no rest since S6. All missing HP/spell slots from S6 carry forward. [DM confirms; no combat occurred in S6 so damage is likely minimal — DM notes HP probably fine for most PCs.]
- Stars visible; Nalith can orient by them. Party is ~1 mile west of the lighthouse.
- Fiorn tastes the ground to confirm it is salt. DM: it tastes like ocean water. **Constitution check called for** [result not recorded — DM rules the ground really is salt].

**The Lighthouse Beam — Magical Compulsion**

- As party walks toward the campfire, the lighthouse beam sweeps over them. Each character experiences a vision of the thing they most desire:
  - **Fiorn:** a white sphere — his original soul (stolen by gods in his backstory).
  - **Yinu:** herself seated in Elminster's lab, being instructed in magic.
  - **Naan:** a white room [player frames it as "no longer being crazy" / inner peace; DM accepts].
  - **Nalith:** a living room with her dead mother, unwrapping a gift — reunion/Christmas memory.
  - **Kto:** recognition from her god; being known as the greatest by her deity.
- **Effect:** Each PC is placed under a magical compulsion. They *believe* the thing they most desire is inside the lighthouse. The compulsion is not frantic — no urgency to rush — but it is certain. Rational counter-arguments do not break it. Every subsequent sweep of the beam renews the feeling.
- Nalith begins walking toward the lighthouse immediately. Naan also walks. Kto runs (falls face-first into the wet salt — movement is a slog). Fiorn skips. Yinu walks.
- Yinu attempts **Misty Step** to cross the ~2,500 feet to the lighthouse. DM: Misty Step range is 30 feet (self); cannot cover the distance. Attempt abandoned.
- Fiorn attempts **Fly**. As soon as Fly is cast, the next sweep of the lighthouse beam causes Fiorn to **disappear** — consumed/transported by the compulsion's sentient source. [DM: "It's sentient, and it consumes people who try to make the trek."] Fiorn is gone from the scene.

**The Embergull Camp — NPC Encounter**

- Party arrives at the campfire. Ship name on the hull: **the Embergull** (a trading vessel, now stranded on dry seabed). ~10 people camped here. One person sits at the fire keeping a logbook; two are awake and staring into the dark; one sits ~50 feet away at the edge of the light, facing the lighthouse.
- All campers glance toward the lighthouse each time the beam sweeps over them — also under compulsion.
- Party approaches. Yinu/Nalith attempt conversation. Fiorn threatens the logbook-keeper for "his thing" [in-character: seeking his soul]; DM NPC deflects calmly.
- **NPC identified as Oren** [spelling uncertain — transcript unclear]: a keeper/de facto leader of the stranded camp. Gender unclear from transcript [referred to with "she" at points — possibly a woman; DM to confirm].
  - Oren explains: the water retreated decades ago (separate and prior event). People have been drawn to the lighthouse by its light. Some have gone; she has watched them die; she has buried them in the moss.
  - The lighthouse keeper is a woman named **Hanson Reed** [DM gives this name explicitly — later corrected in play to **Tamsin Reed**; see NPC section]. She still keeps the light. Oren has tried to get her to shut it off.
  - Oren: *"It's not going to give you what you want. It's going to try to eat you."*
  - Party does not fully believe her (compulsion active). Yinu and Nalith press on toward the lighthouse over Oren's warning.
- DM: **Dispel Magic** or **Remove Curse** can lift the compulsion. Yinu has Dispel Magic.
  - Yinu attempts **Dispel Magic on Naan**: DC 15; roll d20 + proficiency + Wisdom modifier (Wis +3, Prof +3 = +6). Roll: 18+6 [transcript says "18" then "19" — approximately 19 total]. **Success.** Naan's compulsion is lifted. She knows the thing she wanted is not in the lighthouse.
  - [No other PCs are dispelled before the party reaches the lighthouse — they continue under compulsion.]

**At the Lighthouse Door / Upper Room**

- Party arrives at the lighthouse. They go up the stairs.
- The lighthouse keeper opens/kicks open the door to the lantern room at the top.
- **Yinu is discovered chained to the wall** — she had Misty Stepped (verbal component only) out of her chains at some point and is present but was previously captured. [Transcript ambiguous — Fiorn who cast Fly disappeared; Yinu is the one found chained. It is possible Fiorn's "disappearance" and Yinu being chained are the same event displaced in narration. DM to clarify.] Four other humans are also chained to the wall.
- The lantern room is large and circular. At the center: the lighthouse light mechanism.
- **Veyra** — a woman, the villain of the scene — is present. She has been using the lighthouse beacon to lure victims to feed a demon. The **Brine Fiend** (a.k.a. Brine Demon / Salt Demon) is emerging from the floor within a summoning circle, reaching toward the four chained prisoners. Its feet are almost fully through.
- DM: Veyra is funding the summoning to bring the Brine Fiend fully into the material plane. She is being paid by nobles in nearby New Sharshel [names in ledger, not yet read].
- **Tamsin Reed**, the lighthouse keeper, is also present in the room — she is not hostile; she is being compelled to keep the light running and does not understand why.
- **Yinu Misty Steps** (verbal only, while chained) out of her chains. Equipment (staff) is leaning against the wall — not in hand.
- **Initiative called.**

### Key Decisions & Consequences

- **Table decision to leave the Dalelands** — per DM, player feedback drove the portal destination. The campaign is now in a new region. Shadowdale and its active threats (Vhaeraunian drow, Oberon's reconstitution, Sela's fate) are unresolved but geographically distant.
- **Compulsion not resisted — it worked on everyone** — only Naan was Dispelled before the lighthouse. The rest of the party walked in still under it. This did not prove fatal, but Yinu came close (~6 HP before taking Scorching Ray).
- **Fiorn's Fly attempt triggers disappearance** — Fox's character cast Fly and was consumed/transported by the lighthouse compulsion's sentient mechanism. Fate unknown at session end. [DM to establish: was Fiorn the fifth person chained in the lighthouse room? Transcript is ambiguous.]
- **Veyra's ledger seized** — party now holds evidence linking New Sharshel nobles to a demon-summoning ritual. This is an immediate political/criminal hook in the new city.
- **Tamsin Reed freed** — the lighthouse keeper was herself under a compulsion. She is now free. Her knowledge of the area and the ritual's backers is a potential resource.
- **Brine Fiend not banished or destroyed through direct effort** — it dissolved automatically when Veyra died. The party did not have to finish it. This means they don't know its full capabilities.
