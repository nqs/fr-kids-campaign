# Session 007 — The Lighthouse on Dry Land

*Tier 2 · Level 6 · the dry bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars, western Impiltur — the Salt Way, the Drywater, and the Sarshel Light*

**Status:** ✅ **Played** — the summary below records **what actually happened at the table.** Full record: [Session Log](session%207%20-%20log.md).

**Played summary.** Oberon's portal dropped the party at night on the dry bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars in western Impiltur — a salt flat a mile from a working lighthouse, where the turning beam showed each PC the thing they most wanted and compelled them to believe it was inside. At the stranded *Embergull*, camp keeper **Oren** fed them and warned them the light eats people. **Yinu cast *Fly* and the light took her** — she woke **chained in the lantern room beside four prisoners** and Misty Stepped free. At the tower door **Nalith dispelled keeper Tamsin Reed's compulsion**; Tamsin led them up and unlocked the lantern room. In a two-round fight Kto's longbow, **Naan's *Cloud of Daggers*** and Yinu's Shadowspawn wore down the summoner **Veyra** and the **Brine Fiend**, and **Yinu's *Lightning Bolt*** killed Veyra — the demon collapsed into salt water. Tamsin turned her key and shut the light off, ending the compulsion. The party freed the prisoners and took Veyra's **ledger naming New Sarshel backers who funded the summoning** (not why) and about fifteen coins; the session cut as they set out to escort the survivors nine miles east to New Sarshel. **Fox (Fiorn) played from the roll call and left partway through**, before the lantern-room fight; **Juniper did not attend**; **Kaila (Tyvlex) and Ari (Dirk) were absent.**

> [!NOTE]
> **Continuity:** starts straight out of Session 006's portal ejection — no return to Shadowdale, no questgiver, and no contract. Sela's fate, the drow threat beneath Old Skull, Oberon, and the party's other home threads are **left open on purpose.** **Tyvlex (Kaila) played in Session 006 and is not playing in Session 007:** she is **off-camera and safe**, her placement is **open pending the DM and her player**, and nothing in this session lands, captures, or kills any character not at the table. Canonical region: *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide* **p. 0145** (Impiltur · New Sarshel · the Fraternity of Tharos).
