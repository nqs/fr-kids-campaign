# Session 007 — The Lighthouse on Dry Land

*Tier 2 · Level 6 (five PCs, including Juniper's character) · the dry bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars, western Impiltur — the Salt Way, the Drywater, and the Sarshel Light*

**Status:** ⏳ **Not yet played** — the summary below records the **planned major beats**, not a record of play.

**Planned summary.** Nalith, Naan, Kto, and Yinu fall from Oberon’s portal onto an Impilturan road, where Juniper’s character witnesses their arrival and a lighthouse offers each traveler a different vision. A looping road and an answering camp lantern lead to survivors, four missing people, and evidence that the keeper was deceived into surrendering an old protective ward. The party can leave the physical boundary or follow the rescue trail through the keeper’s apron to a cult ritual due at true dawn. At the lantern, freeing captives and choosing whether to restore, destroy, or shutter the light determines the ward’s fate; survivors and evidence can then open voluntary leads into New Sarshel, nine miles east. Sela and the party’s former-region threads remain unresolved.

> [!NOTE]
> **Continuity:** starts straight out of Session 006's portal ejection — no return to Shadowdale, no questgiver, and no contract. Sela's fate, the drow threat beneath Old Skull, Oberon, and the party's other home threads are **left open on purpose.** **Tyvlex (Kaila) played in Session 006 and is not playing in Session 007:** she is **off-camera and safe**, her placement is **open pending the DM and her player**, and nothing in this session lands, captures, or kills any character not at the table. Canonical region: *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide* **p. 0145** (Impiltur · New Sarshel · the Fraternity of Tharos).
