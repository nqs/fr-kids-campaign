# Session 007 — The Lighthouse on Dry Land

*Tier 2 · Level 6 (five PCs, including Juniper's character) · the dry bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars, western Impiltur — the Salt Way, the Drywater, and the Sarshel Light*

**Status:** ✅ **Played** — the summary below records **what actually happened at the table.** Full record: [Session Log](session%207%20-%20log.md).

**Played summary.** The party came down out of Oberon's portal onto the dry bed of the Sea of Fallen Stars in western Impiltur — a cracked salt flat a mile west of a working lighthouse with no sea left to warn, where the turning beam put each PC in front of the thing their character most wanted. A campfire at a stranded trader's hull halfway to the light gave them soup, a warning, and the camp's account of travellers who walked to the light and never came back; the party learned that the beam imposes a magical compulsion and that *dispel magic* peels it off. At the tower, **keeper Tamsin Reed** told them not to go up and then asked them to help instead, led them to the top and opened the lantern room with her key — where **Juniper's new character was introduced as a prisoner chained to the wall with four other captives**, Misty Stepped free of her chains, and initiative was rolled. **Fox (Fiorn) was at the table only for the opening** — in the roll call and through Fiorn's vision — and sat out the session, with the DM explaining on the recording that he is starting a separate game for Fox and his friends; **Kaila (Tyvlex) and Ari (Dirk) were out.** The only fight was the lantern room: the party destroyed the **brine demon** (it came apart into a puddle of salt water) and killed the summoner, sustaining no losses, with Cloud of Daggers doing the heavy damage. **The ending chosen was to shutter the light — Tamsin turned her key and the light went out, ending the compulsion; nobody smashed and nobody restored the lens.** Four prisoners were freed, the summoner's **ledger naming New Sarshel nobles who funded her** was looted along with about fifteen coins, and the session cut as the party set out down the nine-mile road east to New Sarshel with the ship's survivors.

> [!NOTE]
> **Continuity:** starts straight out of Session 006's portal ejection — no return to Shadowdale, no questgiver, and no contract. Sela's fate, the drow threat beneath Old Skull, Oberon, and the party's other home threads are **left open on purpose.** **Tyvlex (Kaila) played in Session 006 and is not playing in Session 007:** she is **off-camera and safe**, her placement is **open pending the DM and her player**, and nothing in this session lands, captures, or kills any character not at the table. Canonical region: *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide* **p. 0145** (Impiltur · New Sarshel · the Fraternity of Tharos).
