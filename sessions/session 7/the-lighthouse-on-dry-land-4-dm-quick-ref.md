## Geography and snare

- Sphere radius **one mile**, centered on lamp. Landing at west edge, camp halfway to tower and 400 ft off road; New Sarshel **nine miles east of tower**, outside sphere.
- Away from lamp on road: five-minute loop to milestone. Toward lamp: ordinary route. Off-road: ordinary travel, but visions/false sky remain until boundary. Camp inside radius; residents can evacuate, but many stayed for missing people or hoped-for visions.
- No compelled movement. Comparing different visions exposes the trick without a roll. Sleep/time work normally; camp supplies shelter, not exclusive permission to rest.
- **Magic (homebrew targets, normal spell limits):** dispel magic automatically ends one creature’s 3rd-level personal glamour until new beam exposure; remove curse suppresses mark until dawn. Detect magic senses nearby magic, not a full map. Daylight illuminates; does not dispel. Actual flight/teleportation may cross boundary; fly can be available at Level 6.
- Shuttering, smashing, or restoring ends projected snare for everyone; only restoration preserves the ward.

## Time — one deadline

- True dawn **three hours after landing**. Orin’s hourglass and the ledger schedule establish it without a check. Walking to tower ~30 min plus chosen stops; one short rest costs one hour. Count fictional elapsed time, not discussion time.
- Arrival >1 hour before dawn: third pedestal inactive for encounter, fiend arrives round 2 and is half-manifested that round. Final hour: normal setup, fiend half-manifested round 1. Surprise uses ordinary stealth rules, not an automatic reward for avoiding rest.
- **Clock start:** Veyra starts at dawn or rushes the rite when confronted upstairs. Alarm readies her, does not start it. Start 0, or 1 for announced alarm (never stack). Unopposed at dawn: two advances per round, completion in three rounds. After that use breach aftermath, not a reset clock.
- Long rest misses dawn; warn before commitment. Short rest recovers only what actual character features allow. Depleted party: use lighter encounter option, not invented automatic slot recovery.

## Encounter 1 — Tamsin (Act II, Scene 2.1)

- Optional, nonlethal; CR 5 **provisional**, 1,800 XP. AC16, HP85, bloodied42; +6 boat-hook, drag DC14, lamp flare DC14. Her homebrew restraint leaves a PC at1 HP/prone instead of0.
- Evidence/watch-words can end confrontation without roll; remove curse ends homebrew hold; dispel magic DC15; persuasive use of missing people’s names DC13. Read stat card for details.
- Her standing order is guard exterior apron: she does not pursue past tideline or upstairs. Use normal movement/opportunity attacks to bypass, not disappearance.
- Keeper habits: checks wicks, insists on handrail, corrects nautical words. Once free, she explains the lamp and helps captives down stairs.

## Encounter 2 — objectives (Act II, Scene 2.2)

- **Veyra:** AC16 HP90; fire-iron, Demon-Name recharge5–6, spell DC15. Choose ritual advance OR attack/spell action. Reaction Counter-Ward forces WIS DC15 when an arm is completed; failure resets that arm/progress. One reaction total; resolve before testing final alignment.
- **Ward benefits:** three active pedestals = radiant resistance and advantage on spell saves; two = radiant resistance only; one/zero = neither.
- **Fiend:** AC15 HP90; bite+two claws; recharge Drowning Grasp; no ritual action. Lesser emissary, not the larger sealed prisoner. Killing it alone does not stop ritual. Intact misaligned lens sustains it, not pedestal count.
- **Lens:** AC15 HP30, damage threshold10. Smash sends emissary back and destroys ward. Restore three arms: per arm one action +DC13 relevant check OR two actions no roll; progress persists. Failed checks add time; reactions may undo progress. No accelerated mending.
- **Shutter:** key plus written code, one action. Ends snare, NEVER pauses ritual. Key lootable regardless of Tamsin’s fate.
- **Pedestals:** AC15 HP20 each; destroying active one reduces clock1, once. Third inactive in early encounter. No extra advancement per pedestal.
- **Captives:** release visible catch with one action, no roll, or destroy bracket AC12 HP10, or dispel its 3rd-level binding. Each rescued person reduces clock1 once. Carry unconscious people using normal movement. Freed people leave ritual targeting.
- **Veyra retreat:** below25 HP with fiend gone, tries gallery rope down west wall; use normal climbing movement and pursuit. Never automatic escape.

## Drawing — authoritative rules

- End round +1 if at least one bound captive AND one burning pedestal. Veyra’s action +1 under same conditions. **Maximum2 per round.** Fiend cannot advance.
- Captive freed or pedestal destroyed: −1, min0, once per target. Threshold events occur **once only** even on repeated crossings.
- No bound captive OR no burning pedestal stops counter; cannot restart with rescued captives. Stopping does not repair an already-cracked seal.

| Segment | First-crossing event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Longing vision, no forced response |
| 2 | Halen unconscious/stable if still bound; if already freed, no effect |
| 3 | Fiend heals15 or gets advantage on its next attack; once only |
| 4 | Merra unconscious/stable if still bound; if already freed, no effect |
| 5 | Floor fissure: difficult terrain; entering it DEX13 or2d6 acid/prone |
| 6 | Seal cracks, no instant deaths. Three further rounds to restore and reseal; stair remains usable for rescue/retreat |

- At6, smashing ends conduit but leaves cracked unwarded seal. After three-round recovery window, pressure stabilizes into a regional hazard; later lens resolution ends snare but cannot repair breach. No surprise explosion or additional automatic damage.
- Drained captives wake when lens is restored or smashed; otherwise remain stable, waking after an hour of care outside snare. Ordinary healing can wake them earlier. No automatic child death.

## NPC cues and evidence custody

- **Orin:** courier, Tamsin’s old friend; warm, mends boots, counts cups, ashamed of failed rescue. Holds her warning/copy, his tally, Andry’s stolen schedule. Wants friends saved, fears harming Tamsin.
- **Ilde:** competent camp organizer grieving son; sees him alive in light. Comparing visions can persuade her without roll; useful evacuation leader, not a penalty trigger.
- **Veyra:** scholar seeking recognition; fears returning empty-handed. Straightens cuffs when threatened. Bargains for life, notes, lens, or time—not for already-rescued people to be returned. Deal terms depend on current board.
- **Captives:** Halen counts steps/knots; Merra knows trade contacts; Bett mends nets and helps carry; Andry escaped briefly with schedule then recaptured. Their help is practical, not extra combat turns.
- Count: 15 travelers excluding Orin =9 camp+4 captive+2 earlier exposure deaths. Camp has10 living including Orin. Juniper/PCs not included in old tally.
- Original keeper log in tower; Orin has copy. Veyra’s full ledger contains city addresses, not the torn schedule. Leads depend on what party actually acquires.

## Outcomes / post-play notes

- Restore before or during recovery window: seal repaired, ward preserved, fiend banished without tooth. Survivors may keep waystation.
- Smash before6: seal intact but unwarded. After6: cracked and unwarded. Fiend returns, tooth if collected; evacuate or investigate later.
- Shutter only: snare ends, rite continues. Clock stopped by rescue/pedestals alone: seal safe if before6, but snare/lens still need resolution or the party can leave the area.
- Killing fiend or smashing lens leaves tooth; restoration does not. Veyra has15 gp; clothing is ordinary, resistances not transferable.
- Sky reveals actual time, not instant sunrise. Merra can offer introductions; no predetermined hostile reception in New Sarshel.
