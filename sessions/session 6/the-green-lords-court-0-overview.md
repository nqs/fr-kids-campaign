# Session 006 — The Green Lord's Court

*Tier 2 · Level 6 · the dying heart of the Living Wood → through the seam into the Green Lord's court*

**Status:** ✅ Played — the summary below records **what actually happened at the table.** Full record: [Session Log](session%206%20-%20log.md).

Session 006 delivered the audience in the two acts as planned — with the table writing its own path through both. **Act One, the gate:** at the standing stones, **Maelorn the Thorn Ward** emerged from the seam-doorway and ran his trial as a real fight — himself plus two giant-owl archers overhead — *"I will not aim to kill. So do not insult me by dying."* New PC **Tyvlex** (Kaila) debuted by going first and opening with a Sneak Attack; the party bloodied Maelorn and passed the measure (the berry shortcut went unused — Nalith kept Quillip's token). **Act Two, the table:** the lord named himself at last — **Oberon, the Green Lord** — laid his endless-spring feast, and offered each marked PC a tailored eternity (perpetual healing for Nalith, every Harper secret for Yinu, one undivided light for Naan). The party **refused unanimously** — no one ate, no one promised. Naan's *Remove Curse* freed a single thrall (who remembered nothing of her years in the hall); Yinu tracked the seam-thread contracting in real time and quietly began untying ribbons. The climax was **Sela Wyndmere's:** reading her own twelve years of knots at the foot of the throne, she discovered the messages she carried were the **suppression layer holding the Vhaeraunian drow beneath Old Skull in check** — and she **ripped the binding ribbon in half.** Oberon burst into a flock of butterflies; the party was rocketed out of the hall. *Session cut on the launch: landing location unknown, Sela's fate unknown, and Shadowdale now stands undefended against an unsuppressed drow threat. Where they land is Session 007.*

> [!NOTE]
> **Played outcome vs. prep.** The two-act structure held, but the table diverged from prep in the details: the gate-trial ran as a full combat (no berry shortcut), **Dirk was absent** (Ari out; the unmarked-companion beat went to Kto and the debuting Tyvlex), and Sela's sacrifice played as written **except her death is not confirmed** — she was last seen in the hall, fate unknown. Oberon was never fought, exactly as designed.
