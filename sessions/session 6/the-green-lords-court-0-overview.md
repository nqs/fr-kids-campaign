# Session 006 — The Green Lord's Court

*Tier 2 · Level 6 · the dying heart of the Living Wood → through the seam into the Green Lord's court*

**Status:** ⏳ Not yet played — the summary below reflects the **planned major beats,** not a record of play.

Session 006 delivers the audience Session 005 walked toward and cut before, in two acts. **Act One is the gate:** at the contracting seam in the wood's greying center, **Maelorn the Thorn Ward** runs the trial the Court owes every guest — a measuring, not a killing, that collapses into ceremony if **Nalith spends Quillip's hawthorn berry** and forces a choice on the unmarked **Dirk** — then escorts the party (and **Sela**) through the door. **Act Two is the table:** the lord of the wood is named at last — **Oberon, the Green Lord** — gracious, ancient, and generous, laying a feast and offering the marked an eternal place at his court; but the grace **is** the harvest, the seam reels the party in as it closes, and Shadowdale's "taken children" are already here, smiling and still. The session lands on **Sela Wyndmere's redemptive death:** the courier who tied twelve years of binding spends her fey-loaned life to **cut the line,** breaking Oberon's hold — and as she dies she reads her own knots for the first and last time, discovering that some of the messages she carried for twelve years were **drow intelligence:** Oberon's fey wardens were monitoring and containing the drow beneath Old Skull, and a deeper **"third cleft"** the party has never found was held by fey — and is now unheld. The party carries that knowledge with them as the collapsing seam hurls them **through into the raw Feywild,** Sela gone, the wood saved at their backs — and the door beneath Old Skull left wide open. *Where they land is Session 007.*

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope.** This is deliberately a **two-act** session — the gate and the table, nothing past the threshold. The tumble through is the closing image; the Feywild on the far side is held for Session 007. **Oberon is not a combat encounter** (no statblock, by design) — the only lever in his hall is the binding, and the only hand that can cut it is Sela's.
