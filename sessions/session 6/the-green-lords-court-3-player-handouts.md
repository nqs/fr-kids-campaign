# Where We Left Off

![The Greying Heart of the Living Wood](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/6f66ca63-5b54-4fd1-b63e-3e398eb9b4c7.png)

**You are deep in the Living Wood, and the Living Wood is dying around you.** A fey lord you have never met **marked four of you** at the Greengrass bonfire and summoned you to his court — *"You are expected in two days. Do not disappoint me by dying."* The mark is a real compulsion; you could not refuse it if you tried.

To find out who he is before you face him, you collected **Sela Wyndmere**, the village healer — who broke down and confessed she has been his courier for **twelve years.** She tied ribbon-knots and left them at a hollow hawthorn for a lord she never saw, never reading the messages, and learned only now that those ribbons were **stitching** — binding the Feywild to your world and letting him **reel the wood inward, a little every year.** Telling you cost her the healing he gave her.

She walked you in. The deeper you went, the more the forest **packed up and left** — color, then birdsong, then warmth — draining in a slow spiral toward one bright point ahead. **Yinu read the truth in it:** the fey are not fleeing the drow. They are being **recalled** — pulled down a drain toward the wood's center, where the lord is gathering them.

Now you stand at that center. Ahead: two great hawthorns arched over a doorway of standing light, the seam to the lord's court, **closing on a clock.** Sela has come as far as she's ever been allowed — and means, for the first time, to go further.

### What Now?

- **The door is the only way on** — and a gatekeeper is about to step out of it to find out whether you're worth his lord's table.
- **Do you still have Quillip's berry?** The pixie's little hawthorn token has never been spent. The gate might be exactly what it's for.
- **Dirk wasn't marked** — he wasn't at the bonfire. The gate will notice. Walking through is a choice he gets to make.
- **Whatever waits past the seam, Sela is walking into it with you** — and she is not telling you everything she means to do.

## The Hawthorn Gate

![The Hawthorn Gate — seam-doorway to the Green Lord's court](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/0da829e5-d6f0-4e38-9a3b-4b84c3f92c89.png)

Two ancient hawthorns have grown together into an arch, white with the last blossom in a grey and dying forest. Beneath them the air **stands up like water on its end** — a doorway of green-gold light, the seam to the lord's court, pulled tight and bright and breathing. Seven moss-furred standing stones ring the clearing, each hung with a knot of ribbon: green, white, bone. This is the door Sela carried twelve years of messages toward and was never once let through.

## The Green Lord's Court

![Title Page — The Green Lord's Court](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/81c3e360-cdad-40d1-a7a0-310a4968f38b.png)

Through the seam, the dying grey falls away and you stand inside **spring without end** — a hall woven from living hawthorn grown into vaulting arches, white with blossom, warm with a light that has no source, long tables laid with a steaming feast and music just past the edge of a song you almost know. On a throne of antler, root, and grey thorn, before the seam pulled to a single shining thread behind him, the lord of the wood waits — and rises to meet you like family he has wanted to seat for an age.

## Oberon, the Green Lord

![Oberon, the Green Lord — archfey of the Living Wood](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/c977505a-1050-4201-ad71-e3ba41bf5096.png)

The lord of the wood, named at last. **Oberon, the Green Lord** — an archfey of the wild green, ancient beyond the dale, crowned in living antler and white blossom, robed in leaf and moss and three trailing ribbons. He is the most gracious host you have ever met: warm, funny, generous, and genuinely glad you came. He thanks you for keeping the drow out of his wood. He learns your names and your gifts. And he offers you a place at his table — *forever, and no winter in it.*

> *"Stay. Be mine, and be honored, and never grow old or afraid again. Say yes. It is such an easy word."*

His smile never falters. That is the frightening part.

## Maelorn, the Thorn Ward

![Maelorn, the Thorn Ward — gatekeeper of the seam](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/2d4aabf0-802b-45e2-814a-a0116419af4a.png)

The knight who rode a stag into your festival and marked your friends — on foot now, keeping the door. A fey gatekeeper in bark and bronze, helmed in living silver branches, a single black thorn for a glaive-head. He is courteous and old and entirely unafraid, and he tests every guest the Court receives.

> *"I am Maelorn, Thorn Ward of this gate. My lord has marked you, and the marked are tested before they are met. I will not aim to kill — so do not insult me by dying."*

He is not here to kill you. He is here to find out what kind of guests you are — and then to open the door.

## Sela Wyndmere — The Last Ribbon

![Sela Wyndmere — the Last Ribbon](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/9f97e888-4286-4b78-9e05-d87c51acf318.png)

The healer who patched you up all winter, who buried enough scouts to have already chosen Rellan Tessar's burial cloth before you carried him home alive. For twelve years she tied the knots that sewed this wood into the Green Lord's hand — never reading them, telling herself that meant they were clean. At his throne, she reads them at last.

She is the one who tied the binding. She is the only one who can cut it. And the fey healing he loaned her is the thread she has to spend to do it.

> *"You have years left to grow old badly. I spent mine. Let me spend them well."*
