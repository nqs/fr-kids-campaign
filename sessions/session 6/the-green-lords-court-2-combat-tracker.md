# Combat Tracker — The Green Lord's Court

**One scripted encounter:** the gate-trial the Court owes every guest. It is a **ceremonial test that ends in parley at bloodied / Round 3** — and collapses to a single symbolic round if Nalith spends Quillip's hawthorn berry. **Do not kill the Thorn Ward; do not let the Thorn Ward kill a PC.** Act Two (the court of Oberon) has **no combat** — the Green Lord is an archfey in the heart of his power and is **not** a fightable foe; there is intentionally no statblock for him. See file 1, Scene 3, and file 4 for the Harvest Clock that replaces a boss fight.

## Encounter 1 — The Hawthorn Gate

*Scene 2 · Medium-to-Hard (raw 2,200 XP → ~3,300 adjusted) · ceremonial test, parley at bloodied/R3 · 6×L6*

### Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | The Hawthorn Cleft — the contracting Feywild **seam** at the dying center of the Living Wood, twin hawthorns arched over a doorway of green-gold light, ringed by seven standing stones. The seam is the door to the Green Lord's court. |
| **Light** | Twilight-bright fey glow under the arch; flat grey daylight at the dead clearing's edge. |
| **Terrain** | The **seam/ritual circle** at center (north) — the Thorn Ward's whole job is to keep the party off it until they're judged. **Standing stones** (cover) ring the clearing; two **toadstool rings** (difficult terrain); a **ribboned cairn** to the east (cover). Forest path enters from the south; **blossoming hedge** walls the north behind the arch. |
| **Antagonists** | 1× **Maelorn, the Thorn Ward** (CR 5, ⚗️) + 2× **Sprite Outrider** (CR 1, owl-mounted — the same two from the S4 bonfire and the S5 prep, for continuity). |
| **Party** | **6 PCs at Level 6** (Dirk is **not marked** — see *Triggers*; Kto joins if Caitlin is present). |
| **XP** | Raw 2,200 → ×1.5 multi-foe at 6 PCs → **~3,300 adjusted** (6×L6: Medium 3,600, Hard 5,400). **Medium-to-Hard** but scripted short — the Thorn Ward yields, he does not win. No rebalance needed; he was never trying to. With a 7th PC (Kto), it runs softer — fine. |
| **Duration** | 3 rounds max. The Thorn Ward **kneels and names the party worthy when bloodied (≤45) OR at end of Round 3** — whichever first. |
| **Goal (PCs)** | **Show the Thorn Ward something worth naming** — courage, restraint, cleverness, or mercy. Winning is not killing him; it is being judged worthy and crossing the door. |
| **Goal (Thorn Ward)** | Test, not kill. Measure these mortals, keep them off the seam, yield with honor when they've proven themselves, then escort them through. |
| **Berry shortcut** | If **Nalith spends Quillip's hawthorn berry** (shows/presses it to the arch), the Thorn Ward grounds his glaive — see *Triggers.* The fight becomes **one symbolic round,** no real combat. Covers Dirk. |

### Triggers & Countdowns

- **The Berry Shortcut (best outcome).** If **Nalith presents Quillip's hawthorn berry** at any point (action to show/press it to the arch), the Thorn Ward **immediately grounds his glaive.** Run **one symbolic round** — a single crossed-blades salute — then go straight through the door to Act Two. The Sprites stand down. *No real combat.* **The berry vouches for everyone at the arch — Dirk included** (*"vouched by the wood's own"*). **Even here, Maelorn still *reads* the party in that one round** — the gate exists to measure, and he carries a verdict (*berry-vouched,* plus however they've carried themselves) to Oberon's face in Scene 3. The berry buys past the *fight,* not the *measuring.*
- **The Unmarked Sixth (Dirk's beat — fires before or at initiative).** Dirk carries **no mark.** Maelorn says so aloud and offers the choice: *stand with the marked and be measured,* or *stand aside without quarrel.* Standing with the party = Dirk is tested normally and **counted through the door.** Standing aside = safe, but **no safe-conduct across the seam** (watches from the treeline). Let silverstrike1 answer — don't have another player answer for them. Full read-aloud in file 1, Scene 2.
- **Bloodied (≤45 HP)** — the Thorn Ward steps back, plants his glaive, and **kneels:** *"Enough. You are worth the table."* Combat ends.
- **End of Round 3** — even unbloodied, the Thorn Ward calls the test passed (*"You did not break, and you did not kill. Good."*) and yields. Combat ends.
- **Ceremonial Restraint (⚗️)** — the Thorn Ward **never drops a PC.** Any hit that would reduce a PC to 0 instead leaves them at **1 HP and prone** ("the flat of the thorn"). Narrate it.
- **Mercy carries over** — if the party *calmed* (rather than killed) the stranded animals in S5's wood-walk, Maelorn knows (the wood tells him) and **opens at advantage to yield** — bloodied threshold ≤55, yields a round early. If S5 ran without that mercy beat, hold to the strict bloodied/R3 triggers.
- **What he carries through the door** — note *how* the party fights (restraint, cleverness, mercy, swagger). **Maelorn walks in with them and reports it to Oberon's face** at the throne (see file 1, Scene 3 — *Maelorn's Verdict* — and file 4's verdict table); it sets the Green Lord's opening warmth. This applies on **every** outcome, the berry shortcut included — the gate always produces a spoken verdict.
- **Avoid: killing the Thorn Ward.** Oberon then receives them as **poachers, not guests** — the harvest in Act Two keeps its hooks but loses its grace (he need not be gracious to meat). Let it cost the tone of the whole second act.
- **Avoid: a PC death.** Ceremonial Restraint should prevent it. If dice get strange, have the Thorn Ward call the test over on the spot — he would rather yield than spill guest-blood.

### Tactics Summary

- **Maelorn the Thorn Ward** — R1 deliver the challenge (free), Thorn-Glaive ×2 against the boldest melee PC, pulling the blow. R2 *Bramble Snare* to control the line, glaive the snared. R3 yield (or earlier at bloodied). **Stays between the party and the seam** the entire fight — *Ward of the Gate* means no one crosses until he yields and stands aside.
- **Sprite Outrider 1 & 2** — R1 hover, *Heart-Sight Arrows* at the two most aggressive PCs; any sprite reading "resolve, not malice" shifts to non-lethal. R2 *Sleep-Tipped Arrows* at the highest-output caster (non-lethal). R3 salute and peel off when the Thorn Ward yields. They **do not recognize Dirk** — a Heart-Sight on the orc reads "river-water, no mark, no malice" and gets called down to Maelorn, feeding the Unmarked Sixth beat.

> [!WARNING]
> **Flag:** DM — This is the gate-test S5 set up and never reached
> Same courteous-fey shape as the S4 Herald crash and the prepped S5 trial: a knight who will not be embarrassed, two familiar sprites, a hard parley trigger, no PC deaths, no fey deaths. The *difference* is what's on the far side. Don't let this fight run long or go bloody — the night's weight is in **Act Two,** and you want the table's blood and dice-luck saved for caring about Sela, not for grinding a gatekeeper who was always going to kneel.

### Loot / Aftermath

- **No coin, no gear** — this is a rite, not a raid. On his yield, Maelorn **stands aside from the seam** and escorts the party through (Sela included — the door no longer holds her at her old limit).
- If the party **killed** him instead: no escort, no grace; they cross the seam over his body and Oberon greets *poachers* (see Scene 3 tone note). Strongly steer away from this.

### Maelorn, the Thorn Ward

> A fey knight of the Green Lord's court, armored in bark and bronze, helmed in a crown of living silver branches hung with white blossom. His glaive ends in a single black thorn. His voice is level and old. He has no intention of killing you — and every intention of finding out whether you are worth his lord's table before he opens the door to it.

**Fey, Lawful Neutral · CR 5 (1,800 XP) · ⚗️ homebrew**

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 17 (bark-and-bronze plate) |
| **HP** | **90** (12d8 + 36) · **Bloodied 45** |
| **Speed** | 30 ft. |
| **STR** | 16 (+3) |
| **DEX** | 18 (+4) |
| **CON** | 16 (+3) |
| **INT** | 13 (+1) |
| **WIS** | 16 (+3) |
| **CHA** | 17 (+3) |
| **Saves** | DEX +7, WIS +6, CHA +6 |
| **Skills** | Athletics +6, Insight +6, Perception +6 |
| **Damage Resistances** | Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical weapons not made of cold iron |
| **Condition Immunities** | Charmed, frightened |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16 |
| **Languages** | Common, Elvish, Sylvan |

**Traits.** *Magic Resistance:* advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects. *Fey Step (recharge 4–6):* bonus action, teleport up to 30 ft. *Ward of the Gate:* while the Thorn Ward is conscious and within 15 ft. of the seam, no creature can enter the seam — passing it requires dropping or moving him, or his leave. *Ceremonial Restraint (⚗️):* the Thorn Ward fights to test, not kill — any hit of his that would reduce a creature to 0 HP instead leaves it at **1 HP and prone.** He will not deliver a killing blow.

**Actions.** *Multiattack:* two Thorn-Glaive attacks. *Thorn-Glaive:* melee, +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., **1d10 + 4 slashing + 1d6 poison** (~13; the poison is a numbing fey toxin, never lethal). *Bramble Snare (recharge 5–6):* thorns erupt in a 15-ft. line; each creature in it makes a **DEX DC 14** save or is **restrained** (escape DC 14 STR, or 5 fire damage frees them).

**Bonus Actions.** *Fey Step* (see Traits).

**Reactions.** *Thorn Ward's Parry:* when hit by a melee attack, add **+3 AC** against that attack (may cause it to miss). Once per round.

**Round-by-Round Tactics.**
- **Round 1.** Free: deliver the challenge aloud (and the Unmarked-Sixth line to Dirk). Glaive ×2 against the boldest melee PC; pull the blow. Hold the seam.
- **Round 2.** *Bramble Snare* across the party's front to control the line. Glaive the snared PC. Parry the hardest incoming hit.
- **Round 3.** **Yield.** Ground the glaive, kneel, name the party worthy, stand aside from the seam. Combat ends. (Earlier if bloodied.)

**On yield.** Rises, stands aside, and **escorts the party through the seam** into the court — Sela with them. States nothing of what waits; that is his lord's to say. *Do not kill him* — see Triggers.

### Sprite Outrider (×2)

> Small fey mounted on fey-touched giant owls — pale gray feathers, starlight pupils. The same two outriders who circled the Greengrass bonfire and shadowed the wood-walk. Their toy-looking bows are not toys.

**Fey, Chaotic Neutral · CR 1 (200 XP each)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 15 (leather + fey grace) |
| **HP** | **22** (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 10 ft. ground, 60 ft. fly (80 ft. on owl mount) |
| **STR** | 8 (−1) |
| **DEX** | 18 (+4) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 14 (+2) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 13 (+1) |
| **Skills** | Perception +4, Stealth +6 |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14 |
| **Languages** | Common, Elvish, Sylvan |

**Features.** *Mounted (Giant Owl):* the owl provides a flying mount; the sprite uses the owl's movement. *Heart-Sight:* as an action, touch a creature OR succeed on a DC 13 ranged shot with a *Heart-Sight Arrow* to read its surface emotional state. If a sprite reads "resolve/curiosity, not malice," that sprite **shifts to non-lethal** for the rest of the encounter.

**Actions.** *Shortbow:* ranged 40/160, +6 to hit, **1d6 + 4 piercing.** *Sleep-Tipped Arrow* (1/encounter each): ranged 40/160, +6 to hit, **1d6 + 4 piercing**; target makes a **CON DC 12** save or is **incapacitated (drowsy) until the end of its next turn.** Non-lethal. *Heart-Sight Arrow* (1/encounter each): ranged touch 40/160, DC 13 vs AC, 1 piercing on hit, reads the target's surface intent.

**Round-by-Round Tactics.**
- **Round 1.** Hover 60 ft up. Heart-Sight Arrows at the two most aggressive PCs. Any sprite reading "resolve, not malice" goes non-lethal.
- **Round 2.** Sleep-Tipped Arrows at the loudest-output caster — aiming to *drowse,* not drop.
- **Round 3.** Salute the Thorn Ward's yield and peel off through the seam ahead of the party.

**On defeat.** A sprite dropped to 0 → the other breaks off and follows the Thorn Ward. They are not here to die for the gate; don't let the party farm them.
