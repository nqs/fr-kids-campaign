## The Harvest Clock *(replaces a boss fight in Act Two)*

The seam contracting to a point **is** the threat — not a statblock. Track it as a 3-stage clock; advance one stage per "scene beat" in the court (the welcome, the offer, the reveal), and **+1 extra stage if the party attacks Oberon** or tries to force the door.

| Stage | State | What the party feels / can do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seam wide; feast laid | Welcome. Can talk, look, refuse, bargain. Hooks set in the mark but slack |
| 2 | Seam half-closed | The line draws taut — the four marked feel the pull toward the throne. Tells become visible (the still guests, the children, the filaments) |
| 3 | Seam a thread | The line reels. Saying "yes" stops you resisting; doing nothing = gathered. **Only the binding can be cut now** |
| 0 | Seam closes | Whoever is inside is **gathered** forever — *unless the line is cut first* (Sela) |

- **How to stop it:** **not** combat, **not** the door. **Sela cuts the binding** (Scene 4). The marked cannot out-pull the line; steel does nothing to Oberon.
- **At zero without the cut:** the party is gathered (smiling, still, eternal). Avoid — but the *threat* of it is what makes Sela's choice land. If the table dithers, let Stage 3 squeeze, then have Sela move.

## Eat Nothing, Promise Nothing *(fey law cheat-sheet)*

- **Taking the lord's food by your own choice = consenting to be gathered.** Quillip hisses it if no one rolls **Survival/Nature DC 13** or fey-lore.
- **A careless promise is a contract.** Anything the party agrees to, Oberon will hold them to — write it down.
- **Refusing flat is allowed and safe-ish** — Oberon doesn't rage; the line just keeps reeling. Refusal alone does **not** stop the harvest; the cut does.

## Encounter 1 — The Hawthorn Gate *(Scene 2)*

**6×L6 · ~3,300 adj XP (Medium-to-Hard) · 3 rounds · parley at bloodied/R3 · NO kills either way**

**Maelorn, the Thorn Ward (CR 5, ⚗️)** — AC 17 · HP 90 (**bloodied 45**)
- Glaive ×2: +7, 1d10+4 + 1d6 poison (numbing, never lethal)
- Bramble Snare (rech 5–6): 15-ft line, DEX DC 14 or restrained
- Magic Resistance · Fey Step (rech 4–6) · Parry (+3 AC reaction) · **Ward of the Gate** (no one crosses the seam while he stands)
- **Ceremonial Restraint:** a hit that would drop a PC leaves them at **1 HP + prone** instead. He never kills.

**2× Sprite Outrider (CR 1)** — AC 15 · HP 22 *(the S4/S5 pair)*
- Shortbow +6, 1d6+4; Sleep-Tipped (CON DC 12 incap 1 rd, non-lethal); Heart-Sight (reads intent → "resolve not malice" = non-lethal)

| Round | Thorn Ward | Sprites |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Challenge + Dirk's choice (free); glaive ×2, pull the blow; hold the seam | Heart-Sight the 2 boldest |
| 2 | Bramble Snare the front line; glaive the snared; Parry | Sleep-Tipped the loudest caster |
| 3 | **Yield** — kneel, name them worthy, stand aside | Salute, peel through the seam |

### Ending the Gate

| Outcome | Trigger | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Berry shortcut** | Nalith presents Quillip's hawthorn berry | Glaive grounded → **1 symbolic round, no combat** → through the door. *Best outcome.* **Covers Dirk** |
| Worthy (bloodied) | HP ≤ 45 | Kneels, names them worthy → escorts through |
| Worthy (timer) | End of Round 3 | "You did not break, did not kill" → escorts through |
| Mercy bonus | Calmed the stranded beasts in S5 | Yields a round early / bloodied ≤55 |
| **Unmarked Sixth** | Maelorn stops at Dirk (pre-initiative) | Dirk's choice: **stand with the marked** (tested + counted through) or stand aside (safe, no crossing). Ari answers, nobody else |
| **Quillip's crossing** | After the gate resolves | She's afraid to cross; **if any PC invites her, she comes** (fey eyes inside the hall + S7 anchor). If not, she stays at the treeline and is gone when they fall. Let the players decide |
| **Killed Thorn Ward** | PC kill | **Bad.** No escort; Oberon greets *poachers* — Act Two keeps its hooks, loses its grace. Let it cost the tone |

> **The gate always produces a verdict — even on the berry shortcut.** Maelorn still *reads* the party in the one ceremonial round. Note the word: *merciful / clever-wary / berry-vouched / brave / poachers* — he speaks it to Oberon's face when they cross (Scene 3), and it sets the lord's opening warmth. The berry buys past the **fight,** not the **measuring.**

## Maelorn's Verdict → Oberon's Read *(spoken aloud, Scene 3)*

Maelorn walks in with the party and reports their measure to Oberon's face — the gate's payoff made tangible. Pick from how they carried themselves; it sets the welcome's warmth:

| Verdict | Maelorn says (paraphrase) | Oberon's welcome |
|---|---|---|
| Worthy / merciful | *"They did not break, did not kill; one stayed my blade with a word."* | **Warmest** — delighted, leans in |
| Clever / wary | *"Quick, careful, trusted nothing — not even the door."* | Amused, respectful |
| Berry-vouched | *"They bore your hawthorn's own berry, lord. Vouched by the wood."* | Pleased, indulgent (*"Of course."*) |
| Poachers (killed Maelorn) | a sprite/Oberon: *"They spilled my Ward's blood at my gate."* | **Thin and cold** — courtesy with the velvet off |

## Oberon — Behavior & The Offer

- **Never not gracious.** Warm, funny, generous, sincere. He is **not lying** — he genuinely thinks being gathered into his eternal court is a kindness. That's the horror.
- **Does not fight.** Attack him and he sighs; the music plays on; **the Harvest Clock jumps a stage.** No statblock — on purpose.
- **The offer:** an eternal place at his table — *"forever, and no winter in it."* Saying yes = consenting to the gather.
- **Per-PC temptation** — each offer-thread *trumps a live hook* (don't relitigate it, just dangle it):

| PC | Oberon trumps… |
|---|---|
| Fiorn | Helbrin's road-tour — *"an audience that never tires, never leaves"* |
| Yinu | the Harper cipher — *"read every letter that crosses my wood, from the inside"* |
| Naan | the Lathander/Highmoon letter — *"never choose between two lights again"* |
| Nalith | the thankless-healer grind — *"heal a hall where healing always wins"* |
| Dirk | the rootless raft — *"a river that never floods; no one asks where you came from"* |
| Kto *(if present)* | the closed spider-war — *"hunts that never end"* |

**The tells** (let the party find them):

| Tell | Check | Reveal |
|---|---|---|
| The still guests / the children | Insight DC 14 / Perception DC 15 | Shadowdale's "taken" are all here — smiling, still, never left |
| The thread behind the throne | Arcana DC 15 | The seam is hooked into the marks; closing reels them in, doesn't send them home |
| Oberon himself | Insight DC 17 | He means it. Refusal won't enrage him; the line is already set |

## Oberon's Bargain Matrix *(he loves a deal — every deal is a hook)*

| Boon | Effect | Cost tier |
|---|---|---|
| Safe passage out | He lets them leave | A name, a memory, a season, or a sworn return at the next turning |
| Release the taken children | They go free | **Someone takes their place at the table** (← the door Sela walks through) |
| A true answer | Who/what/why of the harvest | A secret, freely given |
| Give back the gathered years | — | **Refused.** The line is set; something pays it out |

*Write down anything the party promises — it's real fey contract law and follows them into the Feywild. Stiffing or threatening Oberon: he is unbothered and the clock keeps ticking.*

## What the Party CAN Change *(agency inside a fixed climax)*

The *ending* is fixed (Sela cuts, Sela dies, party falls). The *terms* are not — keep these on the table so it never feels like a cutscene:

| Lever | How | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Oberon's S7 disposition | refuse / bargain / threaten / play along + gate verdict | his stance next time the wood and party cross |
| The taken children go free | bargain for them, or hold the gap Sela's cut opens | their clean escape becomes **the party's** win, not automatic |
| What they owe (or refuse to owe) | every bargain = real fey contract | follows them into the Feywild and back |
| How complete the drow intel is | help Sela decode the knot (below) | full message vs. fragment |
| Who witnesses Sela | stand with her, a word, Fiorn's *Falling* | the heart of the night; the log remembers it |

**Say yes to clever non-combat plays** (cutting other ribbons, shielding Sela's work, talking a child loose, spending Quillip's banked boon): let them buy a round / free a captive / harden standing. Only **steel on Oberon** never works.

## Sela's Sacrifice *(the climax — Scene 4)*

- **Only Sela can cut the line.** She tied the binding; the fey healing he loaned her is the thread she spends to sever it. It **kills her.** She knows.
- **The party cannot save her AND stop the harvest** — say so (Insight / Quillip / Maelorn): *"The line needs a life. There is no third knot."* She refuses any substitution: *"Let me spend them well."*
- **Steer hard toward letting her have it.** If a player *insists* on a heroic swap, it's a table call — but the prepped, far stronger beat is **Sela's** death. Don't let the party "win" their way out of it.
- **What the party CAN do:** stand with her, guard her as the hall resists, **free the children** through the gap she opens, and *witness* her (a word, a hand, Fiorn's song). **Mark who stands with her** — for her, and for the log.
- **Help her read the knot (decode check).** Steady her hands = full read, no roll; or **Arcana/Investigation DC 13** (Yinu/Fiorn) for the *whole* message. Fail / no help = only the **fragment** (*"masked ones… spider-ones… a third cleft they never found—"*); the precise wording (and "river-stone / do not let them past") is theirs **only if a PC helps.**
- **Give Sela a goodbye to a *face,* not the party.** Aim it at **Nalith** (S4 Greening bond — *"You're the one this wood needed; I was only ever the courier"*); fall back to **Fiorn** (the *Falling* song) or whoever held her hand.
- **Sela's last message (drow-intelligence tie):** as she cuts, Sela reads her old knots for the first time. One message — four years old — is **Oberon's instruction to fey wardens** watching the drow beneath Old Skull. It names the **"masked ones"** (Vhaeraunians) and **"spider-ones"** (Lolthites), and a **"third cleft"** — a deeper passage below the three sites the party cleared (S1–S3) — that fey wardens were holding. The containment is now broken along with the binding. **The party carries this intelligence into the Feywild.** It's Sela's final gift — the one piece of her legacy not spent by her death. Full read-aloud in file 1, Scene 4.
- **Then:** the binding collapses → Oberon's hold breaks → the seam tears wide the wrong way → **the party is pulled through into the Feywild.** Sela is gone.

## Tone / Staging Beats

- Open **at the gate,** mid-stride from where S5 cut — no travel montage, the wood is already grey.
- The gate fight is a **question, not a slaughter** — Maelorn pulls every blow; save the table's blood for Act Two.
- The court should feel **wonderful first** — warmth, music, a feast, a host who loves them — *then* wrong. Don't tip the harvest early; let them find the children.
- **Oberon stays gracious through everything,** even the cut. *"The courier reads at last."* No villain rant.
- **Sela's death is the night.** Slow down. Let players stand with her. **Give her last words to a *face* (Nalith first).** Let it hurt.
- **Make agency visible.** The ending is fixed; the *terms* aren't — children, promises, the decoded message, who witnesses her. Say yes to clever non-combat plays.
- **End on the fall** — brightness, wrongness, together, falling. Cut to black. *"You'll find out when you land."*

## DM Don'ts

- **Don't** stat or fight Oberon. He's an archfey; the lever is the binding, not the lord.
- **Don't** kill Maelorn (and don't let him kill a PC — Ceremonial Restraint catches it).
- **Don't** let the party "save" Sela out of her sacrifice — steer hard; it's the heart of the night.
- **Don't** narrate where they land in the Feywild. End on the fall. S7 owns the cold open.
- **Don't** re-run Session 5's loose threads — Harper book, Lathander letter, drow clock all wait.
- **Don't** tip the harvest before the party finds the children — let the welcome be genuinely lovely first.
- **Don't** stage a level-up. Milestone is earned, applied between sessions.
