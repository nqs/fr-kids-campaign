## Adventure Summary

The heroes who put out the lighthouse walk into **New Sarshel** on the first day of its yearly **Tide Festival** ⚗️, and the whole city cheers them through the gate. Veyra's ledger, which looked so sinister in the lantern room, turns out to be a comedy. The people who paid her are the **Gilded Gull Club** ⚗️: three vain nobles whose seaside mansions now stand a mile from the water. Veyra sold them "**the Summoning**" — and they thought she meant *summoning the sea back to their doorsteps*. They were fooled, not wicked, and they had no idea about the prisoners.

The session is a day at the fair with a mystery running through it. The party fends off a fake toll booth on the road, gets a heroes' welcome, then spends festival day at the temple bells and the games while working out who the Gulls are and why they paid. At sunset, the club's leader panics and reads a leftover Veyra scroll from the club's parade float. The giant clockwork sea serpent comes alive and charges down the parade route. The party stops it, rescues the crowd, and ends the night under fireworks with rewards, a new job for Tamsin, and a ship ready to take them anywhere on the Sea of Fallen Stars.

**Canon used:** New Sarshel is the one Impilturan port that kept pace with the retreating Sea of Fallen Stars and won the region's trade; people from shrinking towns have moved there (*CG* p. 145, *PG* p. 107). **Everything else** — the festival, the Gull Club, the Bracks, the float, Tibbet, the Hall of Many Lights, the freed prisoners' names — is a **DM addition ⚗️**.

## Tone Rules — for every scene

- **Villains are silly, not scary.** The Brack Brothers and the Gilded Gulls surrender, apologise, blab, or run away comically. Nobody is killed, and nobody needs to be.
- **Hazards are slapstick.** Confetti, pickle barrels, water jets, flattened stalls. When something goes wrong for a bystander, they end up soggy, sticky, or sitting in a fountain — never hurt.
- **Every PC gets a win.** Each scene names a spotlight for a specific PC. Fiorn is not here (Fox has moved to his own game) — do not write him in.
- **The light lied; the festival gives back.** In Session 007 the beam showed each PC a false version of their heart's desire. Today each PC can get a small, *true* piece of it (see Scenes 1.2, 2.1, and 2.2). These are offers, not requirements.
- **No dark turns.** No kidnapping, ritual, or demon plot this session. Veyra's deeper ties stay an untouched mystery (see *Threads Left Open*).

## What's Really Going On (DM only)

- **The Gilded Gull Club** is three nobles from **Gull Row**, the old waterfront street of New Sarshel's first harbor. The sea kept retreating and the city built new docks to follow it — and Gull Row was left a mile inland, its piers standing over dry sand. The club wants its sea views back.
- **Veyra** found them a season ago and sold them "the Summoning": pay for her work at the old Sarshel Light and the tide will come home. They paid 400 gp apiece. They never went to the lighthouse and never asked questions. **They did not know about the prisoners or the demon.**
- **The ledger** lists their three names with the payments, each marked *"for the Summoning."* (At the table in Session 007 the party read it as payment to summon the demon — which is what Veyra meant. Both readings are true; that is the joke.)
- **Last night**, Gullwhistle's footman watched the lighthouse from Gull Row with a spyglass and saw the light go out. Gullwhistle panicked about his name being in whatever papers Veyra kept, and hired the **Brack Brothers** to stop anyone coming from the lighthouse and "collect" any books or papers as a "toll."
- **Veyra also gave the club a souvenir**: a *Scroll of the Rising Tide* ⚗️, "to be read when the sea returns." It is really a scroll that animates objects. Gullwhistle has it in his coat.

## Act I — The Road In

### Act I, Scene 1.1 — The Salt Road Parade

**Purpose / transition:** Pick up exactly where Session 007 ended, introduce the survivors as fun company, and give the table a silly, skippable fight that points straight at the city. The Bracks' blabbing hands over the first clue: *a fancy gent with a golden gull pin*.

#### Setting
- **Location:** The pale coast road across the dry seabed, from the dark Sarshel Light east toward New Sarshel. Session 007 cut as the column set out from the tower; the sun comes up on the road. The toll booth stands about six miles along, in the early morning.
- **See:** A white, cracked salt flat glittering in the sunrise; stranded old ships far off on the salt; behind the party, the lighthouse standing dark at last; ahead, the towers of a city on the horizon. Then, in the middle of the road: an old abandoned salt-collector's hut the Bracks moved into overnight, a striped gate pole across the road, and a hand-painted sign that reads **TOLL** (spelled wrong — "TOAL").
- **Hear:** Bram's sea shanties, the donkey's complaints, the creak of Dagna's pickle wagon, gulls. At the booth: someone shushing someone else very loudly.
- **Smell:** Salt, dust, dill pickles, breakfast smoke.
- **Sense:** **Insight DC 10** (or just watching them) — the "toll collectors" have never collected a toll in their lives. **Perception DC 12** — a big man is hiding badly behind the salt barrels, and something in the washtub is wearing a hat.

#### NPCs

##### The walkers (survivors and freed prisoners)
- **Disposition going in:** Grateful, chatty, and in awe of the party. Roughly fifteen people: **Oren** and the *Embergull* camp (ordinary folk with packs), **Tamsin Reed**, and the four freed prisoners, named here for the first time ⚗️:
  - **Bram Tolliver** — an old sea-shanty singer. Starts a song whenever anyone says "sea," "ship," or "water."
  - **Hettie Quillfeather** — a halfling mapmaker who has spent ten years mapping *where the sea went*. Her map shows Gull Row stranded a mile inland. She can supply this clue in 2.2.
  - **Dagna Stoutbarrel** — a dwarf pickle-maker who was hauling barrels to the festival's pickle contest when the light lured her. Her wagon was stuck at the camp; the party can help dig it out.
  - **Wren Ashby** — a young apprentice baker who has never seen a city. The party's number-one fan; asks for autographs.
- **Motivations:** Get to the city safely; thank the heroes; enter Dagna's pickles in the contest.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** If the party is kind to them on the road, they spread the story everywhere. **Wren runs ahead** from the toll booth to tell the city (she is fast and cannot keep a secret), which is why the gate cheers in 1.2. If **Sev says Yinu never grabbed her staff and pack in the lantern room**, Tamsin hands them over now — she brought them down.

##### The Brack Brothers ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Nervous bravado. **Mort** (skinny, crooked top hat, does the talking), **Ned** (short, worried, holds the toll bucket), and **Little Ollie** (enormous, gentle, apologises when he hits anyone), plus **Sir Pinchington**, Ollie's pet giant crab, who wears a tiny hat.
- **Motivations:** Earn the fat purse a "fancy gent" paid them last night. Their orders: *stop anyone coming from the lighthouse; the toll is one book or bundle of papers each.* They are hopeless at it.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **Pay the toll** (Mort asks 5 copper "hero discount" once they realise who the party is, or "one book" — the party can hand over *any* book) — they wave everyone through, then argue about whether that counted.
  - **Talk or trick past** (Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation DC 12; any good joke works) — same result.
  - **Fight** (**Encounter 1**, below) — they surrender the moment one brother drops or Sir Pinchington is threatened.
  - **However it ends**, a little kindness or pressure makes them **blab**: *"A fancy gent hired us in the night. Golden bird pin on his coat. Big feathery hat. Smelled like perfume and panic."* They do not know his name.

**Encounter:** **Encounter 1 — The Brack Brothers' Toll Booth** (File 2). Optional; Medium.

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If the Bracks were befriended, paid, or let go:** they turn up at the Grand Parade in 3.1 (Warden Plumm hires them as stewards) and clear one crowd group from the float's path on round 2.
- **If the Bracks were beaten:** the walkers tie them up and hand them to the watch at the gate in 1.2; by Tide Day they are in the stocks on Festival Green in 2.2, cheerfully selling "TOAL" souvenirs; they will repeat the golden-pin clue to anyone who buys one.
- **Either way:** the party knows **"golden bird pin, big feathery hat."** That is the thread to Scene 2.2.

### Act I, Scene 1.2 — Heroes at the Gate

**Purpose / transition:** Pay off the party's good deed with a big, warm welcome, house the survivors, explain the festival, and give everyone the long rest they badly need. The festival schedule becomes the session's map.

#### Setting
- **Location:** The **Landward Gate** of New Sarshel and the streets just inside. It is **Lantern Eve**, the festival's opening day.
- **See:** A crowd at the gate (Wren in front, jumping up and down); strings of bunting; paper lanterns going up on every balcony; workers hammering stalls on Festival Green; down at the harbor, the new docks crowded with ships. On the far side of the city, the long empty piers of **Gull Row** stand over dry sand.
- **Hear:** Cheering, a brass band warming up badly, a town crier announcing tomorrow's schedule, a fiddle somewhere.
- **Smell:** Fried dough, roasting nuts, sea wind from the new harbor.
- **Sense:** **History DC 12** or asking anyone — the Tide Festival celebrates the year New Sarshel "caught the sea": when the water drew back, the city built new docks to follow it, and it has prospered ever since.

#### NPCs

##### Warden Bettina Plumm ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Thrilled. A brisk halfling festival warden with an enormous clipboard and a whistle.
- **Motivations:** Run a perfect festival. Now she has *real heroes* for it.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Finds beds for every survivor and the party at **the Salty Pelican** inn, free. Hands the party the festival schedule (*Handout — The Tide Festival Schedule*, File 3) and invites them to ride at the front of tomorrow's Grand Parade. If shown the ledger now, she frowns: "Gullwhistle? *Lord* Gullwhistle? From Gull Row?" — but she has a festival to run and asks the party to bring proof, not gossip, ideally before the parade. She will point the three Gulls out to the party at the games tomorrow. **She is the person who can make the Gulls pay restitution in 3.2.**

##### Tamsin Reed
- **Disposition going in:** Warm, tired, glad to be free. She has kept a light for twenty years and does not know what to do with her hands.
- **Motivations:** Make sure the survivors are housed; find a use for herself.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Mentions the harbor's own light is badly kept ("a candle in a jar, that one"). Sets up her new job in 3.2.

**Encounter:** None

**Lantern Eve, at dusk (optional, gentle).** Everyone floats a paper lantern on the harbor for someone they miss or something they hope for. **Nalith's spotlight:** in Session 007 the light showed her Christmas with her late mother; here she can float a lantern for her mother. Keep it short and sweet, and only if Piper wants it.

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If the party showed Warden Plumm the ledger:** in 2.2 she finds them on Festival Green and points out the three Gulls by name — no searching needed.
- **If the party mentioned the golden-bird-pin clue to anyone in the city:** they hear that "the Gilded Gull Club wears gold gull pins — those Gull Row snobs."
- **Long rest** overnight at the Salty Pelican. The party starts Tide Day fully restored.

## Act II — The Festival

### Act II, Scene 2.1 — Bells and Blessings

**Purpose / transition:** A warm, quiet scene before the noise. The church thread from Session 007 pays off, each PC can get a gentle blessing, and a priest reads the ledger closely enough to name the club and the strange phrase *"for the Summoning."* That sends the party to the games to find the Gulls.

#### Setting
- **Location:** **The Hall of Many Lights** ⚗️ — New Sarshel's big, friendly temple, with an altar for every god anyone in the city has ever prayed to, and one small unmarked altar *"for the gods we haven't met yet."*
- **See:** Hundreds of candles; stained glass with every color of sea; families in festival clothes; a children's choir lined up and fidgeting.
- **Hear:** The **Noon Bells** — every bell in the city at once — then the choir.
- **Smell:** Beeswax, incense, flowers, festival bread blessed at the door.
- **Sense:** **Religion DC 10** — the unmarked altar welcomes any god, including ones no priest in the Hall knows by name (useful for Kto's god, Naan's patron Zophiel, or anyone else's).

#### NPCs

##### Brother Amsel Tidewell ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Kind, curious, and a bit nosy. A round, cheerful priest who keeps the unmarked altar.
- **Motivations:** Welcome every visitor; thank the heroes; see a good mystery solved.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **Mass / service:** The PC who has been missing mass (on the Session 007 tape this is most likely **Naan** — confirm with Lilli and Kaitlyn) gets the full service and a blessing. If they joke about being "fired," Amsel laughs: *"Nobody here is fired. Gods keep a long list of the people who showed up when it was hard."* **Naan's spotlight:** in the Hall's quiet, the voices go still for a while — the white room the light promised, for real.
  - **Kto's spotlight:** the light showed Kto recognition from her god. Amsel asks the heroes' names for the blessing and reads them out to the whole Hall; the crowd cheers, Kto's name first if she wants it.
  - **Reading the ledger:** he spots what the party never had time to notice: the three names are all **Gull Row** families, "G.G.C." is the **Gilded Gull Club**, and every payment says *"for the Summoning."* *"Summoning what, I wonder? The Gilded Gull Club are fools, not monsters. They'll all be at the games — they never miss a chance to be seen."*
  - **A gift:** two **potions of healing** from the Hall's stores, "for heroes who keep running toward trouble."
  - **Optional:** he offers to send a letter home to Shadowdale by the next ship, if the party wants to warn anyone about the drow.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If the party asked Amsel about Veyra's demon:** he says, gently, that it is over, the four prisoners are safe, and the Hall will look after them — he will not let it turn into a dark story today.
- **If the party skips the Hall:** Brother Amsel finds them on Festival Green in 2.2 handing out blessed bread, and reads the ledger there.
- **Either way:** the party knows **the Gilded Gull Club**, **Gull Row**, and the phrase **"for the Summoning."**

### Act II, Scene 2.2 — Festival Games and the Gilded Gulls

**Purpose / transition:** The fun centre of the session. Every PC gets a game to win while the party works out who the Gulls are and *why* they paid. What the party learns and who they befriend here changes the Grand Parade.

#### Setting
- **Location:** **Festival Green**, a big lawn by the old seawall, packed with stalls and games, with Gull Row's stranded piers in plain view.
- **See:** An archery range with straw gulls as targets; a sand track for crab races; a pickle-eating stage (Dagna's barrels on it); Tibbet's fireworks cart in a roped-off corner; and the three **Gilded Gulls** in absurd seabird finery and golden gull pins, holding court under a striped awning. Beyond it all, the club's parade float: a giant painted **clockwork sea serpent** being polished for tonight.
- **Hear:** Barkers, cheering, a crab announcer, Bram singing, and Lord Gullwhistle's loud laugh.
- **Smell:** Dill, sugar, fried fish, gunpowder near Tibbet's cart.
- **Sense:** **Detect Magic** — faint transmutation from a scroll in Gullwhistle's coat. **Arcana DC 13** on the float — it is full of clockwork, but there is nothing magic about it (yet).

#### The games (pick any; each is a quick roll or two)
- **Kto — the Archery Contest.** Three rounds against the city's best. Ranged attack rolls against AC 15, 17, 19; win two of three to take the Champion's Sash. Kto's +9 longbow is made for this.
- **Nalith — the Crab Races.** Pick a crab (Animal Handling DC 12 to choose a fast one; a druid may simply *ask* the crabs). Win and the crab, **Admiral Snips**, decides it lives with Nalith now.
- **Yinu — Tibbet's Fireworks.** Tibbet's finale rockets are mixed up. Arcana DC 13 (or any clever plan) sorts them. Tibbet teaches Yinu a fireworks trick — **the real lesson the light faked with Elminster** — and gives her a scroll (see *Loot*).
- **Everyone — the Pickle-Eating Contest.** Constitution saves, DC 10, then 12, then 14; last one standing wins Dagna's Golden Gherkin. **No pickled eggs** — a certain hero's religion forbids them.

#### NPCs

##### Lord Percival Gullwhistle ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Jolly, loud, and secretly terrified. Plump, ginger moustache, monocle, feathered tricorn hat with a golden gull, gold gull pin. He recognises the party as "the lighthouse heroes" and sweats.
- **Motivations:** Get the sea back to his doorstep; never be embarrassed in public; get that ledger.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** He tries to buy the ledger ("for my collection — 50 gold? 100?"). **Insight DC 12** — he is scared, not dangerous. He will not confess on his own; he blusters that the sea "will come home tonight, you'll see." He has Veyra's scroll in his coat (**Sleight of Hand DC 15** to lift it — if the party gets it, see *Conditional follow-through*).

##### Lady Marisol Brightwater ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Polite, a little sad. She misses swimming off her own pier.
- **Motivations:** The sea back; to not be a bad person.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** The weak link. **Persuasion DC 12**, a kind word, or ***Charm Person*** (which works fine out of combat) gets the whole story: Veyra promised to "summon the tide home"; they paid 400 gp each; they never went to the lighthouse. Told about the prisoners, she is horrified and **switches sides**. She knows the float's secret: **the big brass wind-key on its back turns it off.**

##### Sir Dunstan Pellow ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Blustering; waves a cane.
- **Motivations:** Sail his yacht from his front door again; never admit a mistake.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** **Insight DC 10** — he is just embarrassed. **Intimidation DC 12** — he blurts *"It was Percival's idea!"* within earshot of the crowd.

##### Tibbet Sparkwright ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Delighted by anyone who likes explosions.
- **Motivations:** A perfect finale.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Overheard Gullwhistle bragging: *"Tonight, at the parade, I read the Tide Scroll and the sea comes home!"* Gives the clue to anyone who helps with her rockets.

##### Hettie Quillfeather
- **Disposition going in:** Eager to show off her map.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Her map shows Gull Row a mile inland and the Gull Club's piers over dry sand — **the "why"** in one picture.

**Exposing the club.** The party **exposes** the Gulls if they make the story public with proof: show the ledger and Marisol's confession (or Dunstan's blurt, or Hettie's map plus the ledger) to Warden Plumm, or to the crowd from the pickle stage. There is no wrong way to do it.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If the party exposed the club:** Gullwhistle, cornered and red-faced, climbs onto the float at the parade to "prove the sea is coming" and reads the scroll in front of everyone. **3.1 happens.**
- **Otherwise:** Gullwhistle reads the scroll as the club's grand finale at the parade, exactly as Tibbet overheard. **3.1 happens either way.**
- **If Marisol switched sides:** in 3.1 she shouts the wind-key secret on round 1, and climbing onto the float is automatic for the first PC who tries.
- **If the party lifted the scroll from Gullwhistle:** he reads a *second* copy (Veyra sold him two — of course she did). The float still wakes, but Gullwhistle is not riding it — he is in the grandstand, waving his arms.
- **Spotlight wins carry forward:** Kto's sash, Admiral Snips, Tibbet's scroll, and the Golden Gherkin all appear in 3.2.

## Act III — The Grand Parade

### Act III, Scene 3.1 — The Runaway Float

**Purpose / transition:** The big, funny set-piece fight. The float wakes, the party saves the crowd and stops it, and the Gulls' scheme falls apart in front of the whole city — in a fountain.

#### Setting
- **Location:** **Harbor Way**, the parade route, from the top of the street down to the harbor plaza and its big fountain. The party is at the front of the parade (Warden Plumm insisted).
- **See:** Cheering crowds in the grandstands; a pickle stall; a puppet show for little kids by the fountain; confetti cannons on the corners; the **Grand Sea-Serpent Float**, teal and gold, with Gullwhistle standing on it holding up a scroll.
- **Hear:** A brass band, cheering, then Gullwhistle's voice: *"RISE, O SEA!"* — then a very large creak.
- **Smell:** Gunpowder, sugar, sea air, and soon, a great deal of pickle brine.
- **Sense:** **Arcana DC 12** once it wakes — the scroll animated the float; it isn't the sea, a demon, or anything dangerous beyond being a runaway parade float the size of a house. ***Dispel Magic*** can end it (File 2).

#### NPCs

##### Lord Percival Gullwhistle
- **Disposition going in:** Proud for exactly one second, then terrified. Clinging to the float's back and shouting for help — unless the party lifted his scroll in 2.2, in which case he is in the grandstand waving his arms.
- **Motivations:** Not fall off. Not be laughed at. (Too late for the second one.)
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Takes no actions in the fight. Any PC who climbs aboard can pluck him off as part of their move, and he is grateful forever. If he is still aboard when the float stops, he lands in the fountain with it.

**Encounter:** **Encounter 2 — The Grand Sea-Serpent Float** (File 2). Hard.

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If the Bracks were befriended in 1.1:** they show up as parade stewards and clear one crowd group on round 2.
- **If Marisol switched sides in 2.2:** she shouts the wind-key secret on round 1.
- **However the float stops** (smashed, dispelled, or wound down), it ends in the fountain with a sad whistle — and **Gullwhistle, if he was aboard, lands in the fountain with it.** Soaked and humbled, he confesses everything to the crowd in 3.2 whether or not the club was exposed earlier.
- **Crowd groups saved** (0–3) carry into 3.2's cheers and rewards.

### Act III, Scene 3.2 — Fireworks and Rewards

**Purpose / transition:** The happy ending. The Gulls apologise and make it right, everyone gets rewarded, Tamsin gets a new light, and a ship offers to take the party anywhere — the hook for the next session.

#### Setting
- **Location:** The harbor plaza, around the fountain, at nightfall.
- **See:** The float in pieces in the fountain; the crowd pressing in; Tibbet's finale rockets lighting the sky over the new harbor; ships' lanterns on the water.
- **Hear:** The biggest cheer of the day, Bram leading the whole plaza in a shanty, fireworks.
- **Smell:** Gunpowder, wet canvas, pickle brine, sea.

#### NPCs

##### Lord Gullwhistle and the Gilded Gull Club
- **Disposition going in:** Soggy, ashamed, relieved.
- **Motivations:** To stop being laughed at.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Gullwhistle apologises to the crowd; Marisol (and grudgingly Dunstan) too. Warden Plumm orders **restitution**: the club pays for **new homes in the city for every *Embergull* survivor and freed prisoner**, pays to restore the harbor light, and pays the party a **reward of 600 gp**. **If the party exposed the club in 2.2,** the club also hosts a free feast for the whole festival tomorrow, and Gullwhistle gives the party his ridiculous feathered hat as a trophy.

##### Warden Bettina Plumm
- **Disposition going in:** Overjoyed.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Names the party **Heroes of the Tide** in front of the whole city. For each crowd group saved in 3.1, the crowd gives a bigger cheer; **if all three were saved, every PC gains Heroic Inspiration.** She names **Tamsin Reed keeper of the harbor light**, and Tamsin, for the first time all session, looks like she knows exactly what to do with her hands.

##### Captain Ama Lorrow ⚗️
- **Disposition going in:** Impressed and grinning. A cheerful half-orc sea captain of the merchant ship ***Laughing Otter***.
- **Motivations:** Good company on a long voyage, and heroes are good luck.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:** Offers the party **free passage anywhere on the Sea of Fallen Stars**, sailing in three days. **This is the hook for Session 009:** ask the players where they want to go.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **The *Embergull* survivors and the four freed prisoners** settle in New Sarshel: Dagna's pickles win a stall, Hettie gets a job at the harbor office, Bram sings at the Salty Pelican, and Wren starts an apprenticeship at a city bakery (and keeps the party's autographs).
- **Tamsin** keeps the harbor light — a friend in New Sarshel if the party ever comes back.
- **The ledger** goes to Warden Plumm as evidence, unless the party wants to keep it.
- **Session 009:** wherever the *Laughing Otter* sails.
