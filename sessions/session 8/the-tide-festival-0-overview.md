# Session 008 — The Tide Festival

*Tier 2 · Level 6 · New Sarshel, Impiltur — the salt road, the Landward Gate, and the city's Tide Festival*

**Status:** ⏳ **Not yet played**

**Planned beats.** Picking up at dawn after Session 007, the party walks the *Embergull* survivors, the four freed prisoners and Tamsin Reed nine miles east and gets past the Brack Brothers' fake toll booth (pay, trick, or a silly fight). New Sarshel cheers them in on the opening day of its **Tide Festival**. On festival day the church thread pays off at the Noon Bells, a priest reads Veyra's ledger, and the party spends the games (archery, crab races, fireworks, pickle-eating) working out that the **Gilded Gull Club** — three vain nobles whose mansions the retreating sea left a mile inland — paid Veyra to "summon the tide" home, and never knew about the prisoners. At the Grand Parade, the club's leader reads a leftover Veyra scroll and their giant clockwork sea-serpent float comes alive; the party saves the crowd and stops it. The night ends with fireworks, restitution for the survivors, a new harbor light for Tamsin, and a ship offering passage anywhere on the Sea of Fallen Stars.

> [!NOTE]
> **Continuity:** starts straight out of Session 007's ending. **Four PCs at Level 6** — Naan (Lilli), Nalith (Piper), Kto (Kaitlyn), Yinu (Sev). Fiorn is not written in (Fox has moved to his own game); Dirk (Ari) and Tyvlex (Kaila) are not at this table. **Tone: happy and fun** — silly villains who give up, slapstick hazards, nobody dies. Veyra's deeper ties, Sela, Oberon, the drow under Old Skull, Yinu's cipher book and Naan's Lathander letter are **left open on purpose.** Canon: *FR Campaign Guide* p. 145 and *Player's Guide* p. 107 (New Sarshel and Impiltur); everything else is a DM addition ⚗️.
