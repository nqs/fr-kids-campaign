# Session 008 — The Tide Festival

*Tier 2 · Level 6 · New Sarshel, Impiltur — the salt road, the Landward Gate, and the city's Tide Festival*

**Status:** ⏳ **Not yet played**

**Planned beats.** Picking up at dawn after Session 007, the party walks the *Embergull* survivors, the four freed prisoners and Tamsin Reed nine miles east and gets past the Brack Brothers, salt-road wreckers hired to take Veyra's ledger (hand it over, buy them off, bluff, scare, or fight). New Sarshel cheers them in on the opening day of its **Tide Festival**. On festival day the church thread pays off at the Noon Bells, a priest reads the ledger, and the games (archery, crab races, fireworks, pickle-eating) bring the party face to face with the **Gilded Gull Club**, three Gull Row nobles who paid Veyra to bring the sea back to their stranded mansions. Their leader, the gracious Lord Gullwhistle, knew the light was fed with lives. He offers to buy the ledger, then threatens the party. At the Grand Parade he reads Veyra's last scroll: the club's clockwork sea serpent wakes with brass teeth, and his clockwork gulls dive for the ledger. The party saves the crowd, stops the float, and decides whether Gullwhistle faces the Grand Council or escapes across the harbor. The night ends with fireworks, new homes for the survivors, a harbor light for Tamsin, and a ship offering passage anywhere on the Sea of Fallen Stars.

> [!NOTE]
> **Continuity:** starts straight out of Session 007's ending. **Four PCs at Level 6** — Naan (Lilli), Nalith (Piper), Kto (Kaitlyn), Yinu (Sev). Normal death saves; nobody dies on screen. Veyra's deeper ties, Sela, Oberon, the drow under Old Skull, Yinu's cipher book and Naan's Lathander letter are **left open on purpose.** Canon: *FR Campaign Guide* p. 145 and *Player's Guide* p. 107 (New Sarshel and Impiltur); everything else is a DM addition ⚗️.
