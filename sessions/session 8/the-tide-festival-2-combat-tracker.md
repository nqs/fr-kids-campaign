# Combat Tracker — The Tide Festival

**Two silly fights.** Encounter 1 is **optional** — the Brack Brothers can be paid, tricked, or talked past — and they give up fast. Encounter 2 is the session's big set piece: a runaway parade float with a crowd to save and three ways to stop it. **Nobody dies in either fight** (see the non-lethal rule in each).

**Party: four PCs at Level 6.** 2014 DMG per-PC thresholds — **Easy 300 · Medium 600 · Hard 900 · Deadly 1,400** — so at **4 PCs**: **Easy 1,200 · Medium 2,400 · Hard 3,600 · Deadly 5,600.** Every stat block is **⚗️ homebrew** except Sir Pinchington (a *Monster Manual* giant crab in a hat); CRs are provisional budget ratings — run them loosely and for laughs.

### Triggers & Countdowns

- **Everyone surrenders** the moment **any one** of these happens: a Brother drops to 0 HP; two Brothers are Bloodied; **Sir Pinchington is attacked or Bloodied** (Ollie drops everything to protect him). They throw down their sticks: *"We give up! We GIVE UP! Mort, tell them we give up!"*
- **The gate pole.** Mort (or anyone next to the hut) can drop the striped pole as a bonus action: one creature on the road beside the hut makes a **DC 12 Dex save** or falls prone.
- **Salt barrels.** Anyone next to the barrel stack can tip one as an action: a 15-ft line of rolling barrel, **DC 12 Dex save** or prone.
- **The rope swing.** A creature next to the mast can swing up to 20 ft as part of its move without provoking opportunity attacks (**Acrobatics DC 10**, or land prone at the end — the table will love it either way).
- **The hut.** Full cover inside. Any single hit of 10+ bludgeoning or force damage on the hut knocks a wall flat, and whoever is inside is now standing in the open looking embarrassed.
- **After the surrender**, they blab: *"A fancy gent hired us in the night. Golden bird pin on his coat. Big feathery hat."* They hand over the purse he paid them as an apology (see *Loot*).

### Tactics Summary

- **Mort Brack** — R1: steps out of the hut and reads **The Fine Print** at the whole party. R2: whacks the nearest PC with his toll stick, shouting regulations. Surrenders first, loudly, on behalf of everyone.
- **Ned Brack** — Hides under the upturned rowboat and slings stones at whoever looks scariest. Uses his one **bucket** on the first PC to come at him.
- **Little Ollie Brack** — Steps out from behind the barrels (badly hidden) and **Big Hugs** the nearest PC, apologising. If Sir Pinchington is threatened, he stops fighting and runs to the tub.
- **Sir Pinchington** — Pinches ankles. Retreats to his tub at half HP.

### Loot / Aftermath

- **The fancy gent's purse: 25 gp**, handed over if the Bracks surrender or are befriended.
- **The toll bucket:** 3 cp, a button, and a very old biscuit.
- **The "TOAL" sign** — a souvenir. Worth nothing; priceless.
- **What happens to the Bracks:** see File 1, Scene 1.1 *Conditional follow-through*.

**Grid: 1 square = 5 ft. Field is 60 ft. × 60 ft. (12 × 12 squares), north up.** The party and the walkers come in from the **west edge** on the road; the walkers stop 60 ft. back, off the map.

- **The gate pole** lies across the road on the hut's east side.

- **Distances:** west edge → hut **25 ft.**; hut → barrels **20 ft.**; hut → rowboat **30 ft.**; hut → mast **25 ft.**

*Act III, Scene 3.1 · Hard (2,000 XP × 2 = 4,000 adjusted / Hard 3,600 – Deadly 5,600 at 4×L6)*

### Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | **Harbor Way** at sunset — a 30-ft-wide festival street running south to the harbor plaza and its big round fountain. Grandstands, a pickle stall, a puppet show, two confetti cannons. |
| **Light** | Bright — sunset plus lanterns everywhere. |

**Conditions:** Bln · Chr · Deaf · Frt · Grp · Inc · Inv · Prl · Pet · Pzn · Prn · Rst · Stn · Uns · Conc

### Triggers & Countdowns

- **Round 1 — Waking Up.** On its first turn the float only creaks, shudders, and sneezes confetti: **it does not move.** Every PC gets a turn to react before it rolls.
- **Runaway.** From round 2, at the start of each of its turns the float rolls up to 30 ft. toward the **nearest crowd group not yet saved or scattered**. **Distracted:** if any creature hit it since its last turn, it rolls toward the creature that hit it most recently instead, and attacks that creature. Hitting it is how you pull it away from the crowd. When no crowd groups are left, it chases whoever hit it last.
- **Crowd groups.** A PC within 10 ft. of a group can use an action to get them clear — shouting, herding, carrying kids, a clever spell. No roll for a sensible plan; DC 10 for a wild one. Mark it **saved**. If the float reaches a group that hasn't been saved, that group **scatters**: stalls flattened, people diving into hay bales and the fountain, nobody hurt — but it isn't saved.
- **Dispel Magic.** The *Scroll of the Rising Tide* ⚗️ is a **5th-level** effect: ***dispel magic*** cast with a 5th-level slot ends it automatically; with a lower slot, make a spellcasting ability check, **DC 15**. When it ends, the float rolls down into the fountain and stops.
- **The brass wind-key.** A creature next to the float can climb aboard as part of its move (**Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13**, costs 10 ft.; automatic for the first PC to try **if Marisol shouted the secret** in round 1). Aboard, it moves with the float; Chomp can't reach it, but Tail Swipe can — on a hit, the rider is also thrown 10 ft. off the float and lands prone. Turning the key is an action with a **DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** check; **two successes** wind it down and it coasts into the fountain.
- **Lord Gullwhistle.** Clinging to the float's back (9 HP, takes no actions). Any PC aboard can pluck him off and set him down as part of their move. An area spell aimed at the float's back will catch him — he drops **unconscious, not dying**, and lands in the fountain (warn the table first).
- **Area spells near crowds.** Before anyone casts *fireball*, *lightning bolt*, or another area spell, warn the table if the area touches an unsaved crowd group. If they cast anyway, the crowd dives clear comically — but that group counts as **scattered**.
- **Hazards anyone can use:** a **confetti cannon** (action, adjacent PC: 30-ft. line, **DC 13 Con save** or blinded until the end of the target's next turn — works on the float and the gulls); a **fountain water jet** (action, adjacent PC: 30-ft. line, **DC 13 Str save** or pushed 10 ft. and prone — Huge float is only pushed, never knocked prone); **pickle barrels** at stall B (action: 15-ft. line, **DC 12 Dex save** or prone, and everything smells of dill).
- **Allies who may arrive:** the **Brack Brothers** (if befriended in 1.1) clear one crowd group on round 2; **Lady Marisol** (if she switched sides in 2.2) shouts the wind-key secret on round 1.
- **When the float stops:** it ends in the fountain with a long sad whistle and a fountain-sized splash. The gulls drop out of the sky and lie in the confetti, ticking. Go to Scene 3.2.

### Tactics Summary

- **Grand Sea-Serpent Float** — R1: wakes up (no move). R2+: Runaway toward the nearest unsaved crowd, Chomp whatever is in front of it, Tail Swipe anyone behind or beside it. Uses **Confetti Breath** whenever it recharges and three or more creatures are in the cone.
- **Clockwork Gulls** — Dive-bomb the PC who is doing the most damage to the float. Snatch hats and snacks between attacks. Fly out of reach.

### Loot / Aftermath

- **The float's wreck**: brass gears, painted canvas, and one goofy wooden head that the festival keeps as a trophy forever.
- **Rewards** come in Scene 3.2 (restitution, the crowd's cheers, Heroic Inspiration if all three groups were saved) — see File 1 *Loot & Rewards Summary*.
