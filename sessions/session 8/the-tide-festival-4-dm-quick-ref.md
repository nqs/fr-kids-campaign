- **4 PCs at Level 6:** Naan (Lilli), Nalith (Piper), Kto (Kaitlyn), Yinu (Sev). Not playing: Fiorn (Fox — own game), Dirk (Ari), Tyvlex (Kaila).
- **Thresholds (4×L6):** Easy 1,200 · Medium 2,400 · Hard 3,600 · Deadly 5,600.

## The secret (DM only)

- The **Gilded Gull Club** (Gullwhistle, Brightwater, Pellow) paid Veyra **400 gp each** to "summon the tide" back to **Gull Row**, now a mile inland. The price included "feeding the light": travellers lured off the salt road.
- **Gullwhistle knew** and agreed. **Marisol** was fooled. **Dunstan** suspected and chose not to ask.
- Gullwhistle saw the light go out, hired the **Bracks**, and carries Veyra's ***Scroll of the Rising Tide*** (really an animate-objects scroll) and her letter: *"The light will need feeding until the tide turns. You agreed the price. — V."*

## Conditions that carry forward

- **Bracks bought off / scared off / bluffed / let go (1.1)** → they clear one crowd group on round 2 of Enc. 2.
- **Bracks taken in (1.1)** → in the stocks on Festival Green in 2.2; Mort shouts *"Oi! G! You owe us fifty!"* at Gullwhistle.
- **Bracks fled (1.1)** → gone.
- **Ledger handed to the Bracks (1.1)** → Amsel names the club from the gull purse; ledger is in Gullwhistle's coat in 3.1; gulls attack PCs instead.
- **Ledger or note shown to Plumm (1.2)** → in 2.2 she points out the three Gulls by name.
- **Decoy fooled the Bracks (1.1)** → Gullwhistle knows by 2.2, and is colder.
- **Club exposed (2.2)** → Gullwhistle rides the float's back and reads openly. **Not exposed** → he reads quietly from the grandstand. **3.1 happens either way.**
- **Marisol switched sides (2.2)** → she shouts the wind-key secret on round 1; first climb aboard is automatic.
- **Scroll and letter lifted (2.2)** → the letter is proof; he reads a second copy from the grandstand.
- **Crowd groups saved (3.1)** → bigger cheers; **all three saved = Heroic Inspiration for every PC**.
- **Gullwhistle taken (3.1)** → Council, estate seized, gold gull pin trophy. **Escaped** → wanted man; leave it open.

## Encounter 1 — Brack Brothers (Act I, Scene 1.1)

- **Mort** AC 15, HP 52, net · **Ned** AC 14, HP 44, sling + caltrops · **Ollie** AC 13, HP 67, grab + throw · **Clacker** AC 15, HP 13.
- **Avoid:** hand over the ledger; buy off (Persuasion DC 13 with 50+ gp); decoy (Deception DC 14); scare (Intimidation DC 15).
- **Surrender:** Mort drops, or two brothers at 0 HP.
- **Props:** gate pole (DC 12 Dex), salt barrels (DC 12 Dex, 1d6), rigging swing (Acrobatics DC 10), flimsy hut.
- **Normal rules:** PCs make death saves.

## Encounter 2 — The Float (Act III, Scene 3.1)

- **Float** AC 15, HP 136, speed 30 · Chomp +7 (3d8+5) · Tail +7 reach 15 (2d8+5, DC 15 Str or prone) · Steam Breath (DC 14 Dex, 4d6 fire, half on save).
- **Gullwhistle** AC 15, HP 52 · Rapier ×2 +5 (1d8+3) · Gull-Whistle · Sea-Glass Charm (teleport 30 ft., once) · Parry.
- **Gulls ×2** AC 13, HP 22, fly 50 · Peck +4 (1d6+2) · Snatch the ledger (DC 12 Dex).
- **Round 1:** scroll read; float wakes, doesn't move.
- **Runaway:** rolls at the nearest unsaved crowd; **hit it and it chases you instead.**
- **Save a crowd:** action within 10 ft., no roll for a sensible plan.
- **Stop it:** 0 HP · *dispel magic* (5th-level effect; DC 15 check below 5th) · wind-key: climb DC 13, then two DC 13 Athletics actions.
- **Gullwhistle escapes** off the south edge (rowboat); **surrenders** at 15 HP or fewer with no way out.
