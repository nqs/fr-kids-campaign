## The secret (DM only)

- The **Gilded Gull Club** (Gullwhistle, Brightwater, Pellow) paid Veyra **400 gp each** to "summon the tide" back to **Gull Row**, now a mile inland. They knew nothing about prisoners or demons.
- Gullwhistle saw the light go out, panicked, hired the **Bracks**, and carries Veyra's ***Scroll of the Rising Tide*** (really an animate-objects scroll).

## Conditions that carry forward

- **Bracks befriended / paid / let go (1.1)** → they clear one crowd group on round 2 of Enc. 2.
- **Bracks beaten (1.1)** → handed to the watch at the gate; in the stocks on Festival Green in 2.2, selling souvenirs, repeating the pin clue.
- **Ledger shown to Plumm (1.2)** → in 2.2 she points out the three Gulls by name.
- **Club exposed (2.2)** → Gullwhistle reads the scroll to save face; 3.2 adds the free feast and his hat. **Not exposed** → he reads it as the parade finale. **3.1 happens either way.**
- **Marisol switched sides (2.2)** → she shouts the wind-key secret on round 1; first climb aboard is automatic.
- **Scroll lifted (2.2)** → he reads a second copy from the grandstand; nobody is riding the float.
- **Crowd groups saved (3.1)** → bigger cheers; **all three saved = Heroic Inspiration for every PC**.

## Encounter 1 — Brack Brothers (Act I, Scene 1.1)

- **Mort** AC 13, HP 45 · **Ned** AC 14, HP 40 · **Ollie** AC 12, HP 60 · **Sir Pinchington** AC 15, HP 13.
- **Skip:** pay 5 cp or any book; DC 12 Persuasion / Deception / Intimidation; a good joke.
- **Surrender:** a Brother at 0 HP, two Bloodied, or **the crab threatened**.
- **Props:** gate pole (DC 12 Dex), salt barrels (DC 12 Dex), rope swing (Acrobatics DC 10), flimsy hut.
- **Non-lethal:** PCs drop to 1 HP and prone instead of 0.

## Encounter 2 — The Float (Act III, Scene 3.1)

- **Float** AC 15, HP 136, speed 30 · Chomp +7 (3d8+5) · Tail +7 reach 15 (2d8+5, DC 15 Str or prone) · Confetti Breath (DC 14 Con or blinded).
- **Gulls ×2** AC 13, HP 22, fly 50 · Peck +4 (1d6+2) · Dive-Bomb (DC 12 Str or prone).
- **Round 1:** float wakes up, doesn't move.
- **Runaway:** rolls at the nearest unsaved crowd; **hit it and it chases you instead.**
- **Save a crowd:** action within 10 ft., no roll for a sensible plan.
- **Stop it:** 0 HP · *dispel magic* (5th-level effect; DC 15 check below 5th) · wind-key: climb DC 13, then two DC 13 Athletics actions.
- **Gullwhistle** aboard (9 HP); area spells catch him — warn first. Anyone dropped is unconscious and stable, not dying.
