# Session 002 — The Second Cleft

*Tier 2 · 5 PCs at Level 5 · Shadowdale → the Living Wood → Site B (the Hawthorn Chimney)*

**Status:** ✅ Played

Lady Ulphor sent the party to scout the two remaining sites from the Vhaeraunian map; Captain Helm dismissed his missing watchers and stormed out after Fiorn's open contempt, while **Brynn Ashford** quietly asked the party to bring their findings to her *before* the Harpers. Five hours southwest through the Living Wood, they found Site B — the **Hawthorn Chimney** — already purged: a cold camp, a bloodied bedroll, and a half-burnt Lolthite scroll reading *"Close the surface mouths. The Vhaeraun is opening."* They killed the wounded drider and two giant spiders climbing the chimney, Yinu finishing the fight with a Fireball down the shaft. The cleft had been deliberately collapsed with drow magic, confirming two warring drow factions beneath Old Skull — **Lolthite and Vhaeraunian** — with **Site C still open and unscouted**.
