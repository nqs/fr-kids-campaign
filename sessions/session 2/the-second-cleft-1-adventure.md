# The Second Cleft
*Session 002 — A Shadowdale Adventure*

## Adventure Summary

The Vhaeraunian map fragment the party recovered last session is no longer just intelligence — it's a deadline. Lady Ulphor confirms her militia have corroborated the markings: the two unscouted sites are real, and the southern one (Site B, in the Living Wood) has gone dark in the last forty-eight hours. The party is dispatched to scout Site B for evidence of where the cell went, then proceed to Site C — the deeper cave on the northern flank — and either neutralize it or bring back enough information for Lady Ulphor to commit the militia to a coordinated strike.

Site B turns out to have been purged the night before by a rival drow faction. Site C is a half-finished Vhaeraunian shrine-camp, run by a priest of Vhaeraun named **Khelziir Aun'velve**, racing to consecrate a small surface-side portal anchor. **Zeldrazz T'orrl is here** — alive, healed, demoted, and furious. The session ends with the portal anchor either smashed, stolen for study, or — if the party is clever and lucky — turned into a Harper intelligence asset.

## Scene 1 — The Briefing at the Ashaba House

### Setup
Morning. Lady Addee Ulphor's working room at the Ashaba House. **Brynn Ashford** is present and taking notes; **Corwick Helm** arrives partway through, leather apron still on. Lady Ulphor has the Vhaeraunian map fragment unrolled on her desk, weighted with a tin mug and an inkwell.

![Brynn Ashford — Shadowdale's Delegate to the Dales Council](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/b9105155-7cff-46a0-899c-1707ae26dfd5.png)

![Corwick Helm — Blacksmith and Militia Captain](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/5459ec16-fec7-4d76-8f82-4886004cf84c.png)

### What Lady Ulphor Offers
- **250 gp** on completion of the scouting mission, with proof
- **+200 gp bonus** if the party can disable or destroy any drow staging site
- Sela Wyndmere's herbal kit — 3× healing poultices, 2× antitoxin (already paid for)
- The use of two militia at the trailhead as reserve: **Aelinor Wains** (Corwick's most reliable scout) and, if the party insists, **Rellan Tessar** — Corwick prefers Aelinor; Rellan is still Exhaustion 1

### New Information Lady Ulphor Shares
- **Site B** (southern marking, Living Wood) was watched by a militia scout for two days. As of yesterday morning, smoke from the chimney *stopped*. Either the cell broke camp or something killed them.
- **Site C** (northern marking, deeper in Old Skull's flank) is showing increased activity. Sela has reported fey withdrawing from a quarter-mile radius around it.
- The map fragment had a sigil in the corner the party didn't recognize. Lady Ulphor has identified it: a **Vhaeraunian priestly mark**. A *cleric* is on the surface. That changes the threat profile.

### Side Channel — Brynn Ashford
Brynn will, at some point during the briefing or after, find a moment to speak privately with one PC (GM's choice — likely the one with the highest CHA or already known to be politically aware). His ask: **Orvyn Tal asked him this morning whether the militia were planning to follow up on the map.** Brynn lied and said no. He'd like to know what the party finds before the Harpers do.

### DM Notes — Briefing Tone
- Lady Ulphor is composed but tighter than last session. She touches the Pendant of Ashaba twice while talking.
- Corwick volunteers Aelinor over Rellan with no explanation; the truth is he doesn't trust Rellan's nerves yet.
- If the party asks about Captain Helm's earlier cover-up, Lady Ulphor says simply: *"Captain Helm and I are speaking later this week. The matter is closed publicly. Privately it remains open."*

## Scene 2 — The Living Wood Approach (Site B)

![The Hawthorn Chimney — Site B Approach](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/69c86693-2c04-4e7a-b1f5-20bf9f691495.png)

### Travel
Approximately 5 hours from the village to the southern site, on Old Skull's southwestern flank, through the fey-dense edge of the Living Wood. Aelinor (and Rellan, if he came) holds the trailhead at a half-collapsed shepherd's bothy ~30 minutes from Site B. Beyond that, the party is on its own.

### Skill Challenge — "The Wood is Watching"
**4 successes before 3 failures.** DCs 13–15. Suggested checks (one per PC per round, no repeats):

- **Survival** or **Nature** to find Sela's old foraging trail
- **Perception** or **Investigation** to spot fey wards on bark — acorn-and-iron warnings to trespassers
- **Persuasion** or **Performance** to greet a watching dryad respectfully (Nalith and Fiorn shine here)
- **Arcana** to recognize a flickering Feywild seam and route around it

**Outcomes:**
- **Per failure:** a glamour-trick costs the party an hour, splits the marching order, or imposes one level of Exhaustion on the failing PC until next short rest.
- **On full success:** they arrive at Site B in late afternoon, with daylight still to work in.
- **On full failure:** they arrive at dusk, having lost their reserve at the bothy (Aelinor will hold but won't be able to find them again), and the encounter inside Site B happens in dim light.

### Arrival at Site B

#### Approaching the Hawthorn Chimney

> [!NOTE]
> **Read-aloud:** Read Aloud
> The hawthorn closes overhead in a low arch — pale berries, black thorns. Beneath the boughs, a fire pit lies cold under a film of ash, three bedrolls splayed around it like petals torn from a flower. One has a long dark stain along its length. The chimney mouth is a dark wound in the rock, and from inside, very faintly, comes the sound of something dragging itself upward.

![Tactical Map — Site B, The Hawthorn Chimney](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/c4b0920d-98a1-47b5-9939-8dfb7b4503c4.png)

### What the Party Finds (DC 13 Investigation)
- A natural rock chimney rising 20 ft. into a granite outcrop, surrounded by hawthorn
- Fire pit cold for ~30 hours. Three bedrolls; one slashed open and bloodstained
- **Drag marks** — humanoid, leading *into* the chimney shaft and *down*
- A **bone scroll case** with a half-burnt Undercommon order: *"…abandon Site B, reinforce the priest, the surface anchor must be set by the dark of the moon."*
- A **Vhaeraun half-mask**, smaller than Zeldrazz's, ceremonial, broken
- A **spider-silk thread** leading down the chimney's interior — too thick to be natural

## Encounter — What Came Up After

As soon as the party investigates the chimney shaft (or after ~3 in-fiction minutes if they hesitate), the wounded survivor of last night's purge climbs out to silence the witnesses.

![The Wounded Drider — Lolthite Enforcer at Site B](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/3123dcd6-db20-450c-a00a-b1202b2de8ea.png)

### Combatants

| Combatant | CR | XP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wounded Drider (Lolthite) | 6 | 2,300 | Below half HP at start; will fight to silence party |
| Giant Spider × 2 | 1 | 200 each | Climb out of the shaft on initiative count 10 of round 1 |

### Tactics
- The drider opens with **Web** on the party's heaviest melee (most likely Kto), then closes with the obsidian dagger and bite. It uses the chimney shaft as a fallback — if reduced below 1/4 HP, it will attempt to retreat downward.
- The two giant spiders flank — one stays in the chimney shaft (advantage on attacks vs. anyone leaning in), the other drops onto the closest light-armored caster (Yinu or Loric).
- **Encounter difficulty:** Hard for 5×L5 (~2,700 XP). Adjust by adding a third spider for Deadly.

### What This Means (DM-facing)
This is the first hard evidence that the **Vhaeraunian and Lolthite drow factions are at war beneath Shadowdale**. The drider is a Lolthite enforcer; the camp she purged was Vhaeraunian. The party can exploit this rivalry going forward — or get caught between it. The drider, if interrogated rather than killed (good luck), knows only that her matron was ordered to *"close the surface mouths Vhaeraun is opening."* She will not cooperate willingly.

### Loot from Site B
- **Bone scroll case** with the half-burnt Vhaeraunian order (intelligence — Lady Ulphor will pay 50 gp)
- **Broken ceremonial half-mask** (Orvyn Tal will pay 80 gp; Lady Ulphor 40 gp)
- **Drow poison ×2 vials** looted from the bedrolls (the spiders disturbed them but didn't take them)
- **~30 gp** in mixed coin and trade goods

## Scene 3 — Travel to Site C & The Watcher Above

![The Cleft Mouth — Site C Entrance](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/220830ea-d538-499e-a694-33d49b3d8ae9.png)

### The Trek
Approximately 4 hours overland from Site B to Site C, skirting the granite shoulder of Old Skull. If Aelinor and Rellan are with the party, they hold position at the shepherd's bothy — they will not enter the cleft regardless of how the conversation goes. Corwick was clear about that.

### Encounter — The Watcher
A single **Drow Scout (CR 1/2)** is perched in a wind-stunted pine 60 ft. above the cleft mouth, with a brass signal horn slung at her belt. **DC 17 Perception** to spot her before she spots the party. If she sees them first, she **blows the horn before being killed** — Khelziir's cell goes to alert status, and the party loses surprise on the antechamber fight in Scene 4.

### The Cleft Mouth
A vertical fissure 6 ft. wide, 10 ft. tall, breathing cold spider-musk air. Spider silk threads cross the opening at ankle and chest height — a tripwire warning system Khelziir installed last week. **DC 14 Perception** to spot the silk in the dim light at the cleft's lip. Anyone who walks into it without spotting it triggers a soft chime that echoes down the cleft — Chamber 1 goes to alert.

### Approach Options
- **Frontal stealth** — Stealth check (group, DC 13) to enter the cleft without triggering the silk
- **Cut the wires** — Sleight of Hand (DC 12) to cut without ringing the chime
- **Climb above** — the cleft is climbable along a side chimney; DC 15 Athletics, drops party into Chamber 1 from the ceiling (no silk, surprise round if quiet)

## Scene 4 — The Shrine-Camp (Site C)

A three-chamber dungeon carved into a natural cleft beneath Old Skull's northern face. The Vhaeraunians have been working here for roughly two weeks. Khelziir is in a hurry; the place is functional, not finished.

![Tactical Map — Site C, The Shrine-Camp](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/c1cfae4b-c9ae-40c7-bf4b-df76d5ff95cb.png)

### Chamber 1 — The Antechamber
A 30 × 30 ft. natural cavern with two bedroll piles, a cold cookfire in the center, and a weapon rack against the east wall holding three hand crossbows and a sheaf of bolts. **2× Drow Scouts (CR 1/4)** are on watch; one is fletching, one is dozing. If the party kept surprise, both can be dropped in the first round; if not, one will sprint for the Shrine to warn Khelziir.

### Chamber 2 — The Spider Pen
A 20 × 40 ft. side chamber separated from Chamber 1 by a curtain of spider-silk. **1× Giant Spider (CR 1)** is chained to the wall — Khelziir's pet sentinel. If the silk curtain is disturbed, the spider **shrieks** (a Vhaeraunian-trained alarm response), audible throughout the dungeon. Cutting the chain releases the spider; it will not distinguish friend from foe.

### Chamber 3 — The Shrine

![The Shrine Chamber — Site C Interior](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/3f79f172-3d3b-482a-b304-ea376ab93834.png)

A 40 × 50 ft. chamber. A black basalt slab altar at the back, draped with a Vhaeraunian half-mask banner. A **partially-consecrated portal anchor** sits at the chamber's center: a circle of obsidian shards 8 ft. across, half-inscribed with Undercommon glyphs in luminescent silver ink. Khelziir stands at the altar mid-ritual; Zeldrazz flanks him. Black candles burn on the altar with greenish flames.

## The Boss Encounter

![Khelziir Aun'velve — Drow Priest of Vhaeraun](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/c5e6d783-0e6b-4c49-b337-3341e387b92f.png)

### Khelziir Aun'velve — Drow Priest of Vhaeraun
**Medium humanoid (drow), neutral evil. CR 6.** AC 16 (chain shirt + shield of faith). HP 78 (12d8+24). Speed 30 ft. STR 11, DEX 14, CON 14, INT 13, WIS 17, CHA 14. Saves WIS +6, CHA +5. Skills Perception +6, Religion +4, Stealth +5. Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16. Languages Common, Elvish, Undercommon. **Innate Spellcasting:** faerie fire 1/day, darkness 1/day, levitate 1/day. **Spellcasting (cleric, save DC 14, +6 to hit):** cantrips — guidance, sacred flame, thaumaturgy; 1st (4) — bane, command, shield of faith; 2nd (3) — silence, spiritual weapon; 3rd (3) — dispel magic, spirit guardians; 4th (1) — banishment. **Sunlight Sensitivity.**

#### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Two shortsword attacks.
- **Shortsword:** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1d6+2 piercing plus 1d6 poison.
- **Hand Crossbow:** +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 1d6+2 piercing plus drow poison (Save DC 13 CON or unconscious 1 hour, ends if damaged).

#### Tactics
**Round 1:** casts *spirit guardians* (shadow-form skull spirits orbit him), then bonus-action *shield of faith* on himself. **Round 2:** *silence* centered on the loudest caster (likely Yinu or Loric — shut down the Wizard or the Sorcerer). **Round 3:** *spiritual weapon* (a black blade) and continues the consecration ritual. **Round 4+:** if the anchor is still intact, it activates and summons reinforcements (see below). Khelziir will not break formation from the altar — finishing the ritual matters more than his own survival.

![Zeldrazz T'orrl — Drow Elite Warrior, Demoted](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/c84c4f76-99f8-4d46-8dd0-a1ba14348fff.png)

### Zeldrazz T'orrl — Returning Antagonist
**Medium humanoid (drow), neutral evil. CR 5.** Use the *Drow Elite Warrior* stat block (MM p.128). Reskinned with: a plain steel scimitar in place of the drow-made one; a fresh scar across his left cheek; a chip on his shoulder the size of Old Skull. He has personally targeted whichever PC dealt the killing-attempt blow last session and will **open with poisoned attacks against them**.

### The Ritual Countdown
**3 rounds remaining** when the party enters the Shrine. Each round Khelziir is alive, conscious, and not silenced or stunned, the countdown ticks down by one. If it reaches zero, the portal anchor **activates**: a tear opens above the obsidian circle and **4× Vhaeraunian drow reinforcements** (use Drow stat block, MM p.128) step through on the next initiative. The encounter escalates to Deadly. The party can stop the countdown by: killing Khelziir, knocking him unconscious, silencing him for a full round (he can step out of *silence*, so this only delays), or destroying the half-finished anchor itself (**AC 12, 40 HP, vulnerable to bludgeoning** — but striking it provokes opportunity attacks from anyone in melee with the striker, and Khelziir treats it as an attack on himself).

## The Three Endings

**1) Smash the anchor** (loud win) — The portal collapses inward in a thunderclap of cold air. Khelziir's cell is permanently shut down; Lady Ulphor is delighted; the militia get credit publicly; the Vhaeraunians know the surface knows. *Reputation: +Lady Ulphor, +Corwick. Loose end: a furious response from below within 2–3 weeks.*

**2) Steal the anchor for study** (quiet win) — Requires a successful **DC 16 Arcana** check by Yinu or Loric to disrupt the consecration without triggering the portal. The anchor goes back to Lady Ulphor, who hands it to Orvyn Tal for Harper analysis. *Reputation: +Harpers significantly, +Lady Ulphor privately, neutral publicly.*

**3) Turn it into a feed** (clever win) — If a PC succeeds on a **DC 18 Religion or Arcana** check after Khelziir falls, they realize the half-finished anchor can be left running as a one-way listening glyph into the Vhaeraunian command structure. Orvyn Tal will pay handsomely (**+500 gp**) and offer Harper recruitment for this. *Reputation: +Harpers heavily; the Vhaeraunians don't know they're being listened to — yet.*
