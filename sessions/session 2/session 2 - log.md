## Session 002 — The Hawthorn Chimney
**Date played:**
**Location in-world:** Shadowdale — Ashaba House, the Living Wood (southwestern flank of Old Skull), Site B (the Hawthorn Chimney)
**Players present:** Caitlin (Kto), Fox (Fiorn), Sev (Yinu), Lily (Loric T.), Piper (Nalith)

### What Happened
- Party recalled to Ashaba House. Lady Ulphor reported that the militia watchers she'd posted on the two remaining map sites had returned mixed news: the **Site B** pair had stopped reporting in the day before, and the **Site C** pair had been forced back by fey withdrawing a quarter-mile around the cleft.
- **Corwick Helm** arrived mid-briefing and pushed back — dismissed his missing men as "drinking by the river" and refused to commit until tomorrow. Fiorn was openly contemptuous (the now-canonical "eats another piece of corn" bit). Corwick stormed out warning he'd go himself if they weren't found.
- Yinu identified the corner sigil on the recovered Vhaeraunian map as a **drow priestly mark** (Religion 20). Confirmed a drow priestess is on the surface and that drider/driker escorts are a real possibility.
- **Brynn Ashford** pulled **Loric T.** aside privately and disclosed that **Orvyn Tal is a Harper**, that Brynn distrusts their motives, and asked the party to bring findings to him *before* the Harpers — with a vague offer of "incentivity." Loric was noncommittal. **Nalith eavesdropped from under a chair**, undetected; the rest of the party was looped in afterward.
- Party departed Shadowdale and traveled ~5 hours southwest through the Living Wood to **Site B (the Hawthorn Chimney)**. No militia escort taken.
- Found the camp recently abandoned: cold fire pit, three bedrolls (one heavily bloodstained), a half-burnt **bone scroll case** in the fireplace. Yinu hit a 28 Investigation lying under the chimney and spotted a hairy, bladed limb on the smoke shelf — yelled a warning, party gained surprise on initiative.
- Fought a wounded **drider (Lolthite enforcer)** and **two giant spiders** climbing up from below. Fiorn raged and reckless-attacked for repeated heavy hits (one for 22 finished a spider). Yinu's Firebolt for 17 exposed the drider's still-beating heart. Loric chipped in with Firebolts; one spider caught fire and burned over time. **Yinu finished the encounter with Fireball into the chimney shaft**, incinerating the last spider in the enclosed space (and catching herself in the blast).
- **Scroll recovered from the fireplace** read aloud: *"Close the surface mouths. The Vhaeraun is opening."* — signed in **Lolthite** script. Loric's Religion check (17+) confirmed the writers were **Lolthites**, distinct from the **Vhaeraunians** whose map the party recovered last session.
- Inspection of the cleft itself: the gap had been deliberately **collapsed**. Yinu's Arcana check confirmed **drow magic** was used to seal it. A Lolthite raiding party purged this Vhaeraunian camp the night before and shut the underdark connection here behind them. **Site C remains open and unscouted.** Session ended at the cleft, with the party preparing to travel to Site C.

### Key Decisions & Consequences
- **Loric did not commit to Brynn's "first call" arrangement.** The Council/Harper wedge is now an active, unanswered thread.
- **Nalith's eavesdropping was clean** — Brynn doesn't know the rest of the party heard the exchange.
- **Public rift with Corwick Helm widened.** Fiorn's open contempt at the briefing has put the Captain on personal bad terms with the party.
- **Site B triaged, not exhausted.** No interrogation (drider killed); the collapsed shaft was not investigated further; no attempt to follow the Lolthite trail downward.
- **Two-faction drow war is now confirmed party knowledge** — Lolthites sealing surface anchors that Vhaeraunians are opening.

### NPC Interactions
- **Lady Addee Ulphor** — composed; touched the Pendant of Ashaba twice. Confirmed the priestess-sigil identification.
- **Corwick Helm** — combative; relationship with the party degraded after Fiorn's contempt at the briefing. Stormed out.
- **Brynn Ashford** — first significant on-stage appearance. Revealed Orvyn Tal's Harper affiliation. Pulled Loric aside, pitched the "first call" arrangement; Loric noncommittal.
- **Orvyn Tal** — off-stage. Now confirmed Harper to the party.

### Combat & Encounters
- **The Hawthorn Chimney:** wounded drider (CR 6) + 2 × giant spiders (CR 1). Party had surprise. All defeated. No PCs dropped. ~2,700 XP — Hard for 5 × level 5.
