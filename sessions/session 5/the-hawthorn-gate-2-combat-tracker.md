# Combat Tracker — The Hawthorn Gate

Two scripted, short encounters. **Encounter 1** can end without a kill (calm the beasts). **Encounter 2** is a ceremonial gate-test that ends in parley at bloodied / round 3 — and collapses to a single symbolic round if Nalith spends Quillip's hawthorn berry. **Do not kill the Thornward; do not let the Thornward kill a PC.**

---

## Encounter 1 — The Frightened Wood
*Scene 3 · Medium-light (raw 1,500 XP → ~3,000 adjusted) · 3-round scripted, mercy off-ramp every round · 6×L6*

### Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | A misty clearing at the edge of the Living Wood, near Goodman Harrow's lost goats. The contracting Feywild seam is ~200 ft further in (the animals are trying to reach it). |
| **Light** | Late-morning daylight, grey and flat under the thinning canopy. |
| **Terrain** | A **broken livestock fence** and **overturned cart** (half cover) on the west; a **shallow stream** cutting diagonally (**difficult terrain**, the beasts ignore it); **boulders and fallen logs** (cover) in the center. Deer-path exits north toward the seam. |
| **Antagonists** | 1× **Fey-Touched Great Stag** (CR 3, ⚗️) + 4× **Fey-Touched Wolf** (CR 1, ⚗️). |
| **Party** | **6 PCs at Level 6** (Loric rejoins). |
| **XP** | Raw 1,500 → ×2 multi-foe → **~3,000 adjusted** (Medium for 6×L6 is 3,600; Easy 1,800). **Medium-light** — built to be *survivable and winnable by kindness,* not a grind. |
| **Duration** | 3 rounds max. Ends early the instant the **stag is calmed** (see below) — the wolves break with it. |
| **Goal (PCs)** | Survive — and, ideally, **realize the animals are terrified, not hostile, and calm them.** |
| **Goal (beasts)** | Reach the closing seam. They attack only what stands between them and the door. |
| **Civilians** | **Sela** holds at the treeline; she will *aid* an Animal Handling check (advantage) but will not fight the wood. The goats cower; not in play. |

### Triggers & Countdowns

- **Stag calmed** — a creature within 30 ft uses an action: **Wisdom (Animal Handling) DC 14**, *or* casts `speak with animals` / `calm emotions`, *or* a druid's beast-empathy, *or* spends **Healing Light / Cure Wounds on the stag.** On success the stag is calmed (drops out of the fight). **Each wolf then makes WIS DC 12 or loses heart and bolts for the seam.** This is the intended ending — *flag it big.*
- **Sela aids** — any PC making the Animal Handling check within 30 ft of Sela has **advantage** (she talks them through it).
- **Bloodied beast whimpers** — when the stag or any wolf is first bloodied, a WIS-attuned PC (Nalith, Naan, Kto) *feels the fear, not malice.* Free tell toward mercy.
- **End of Round 3** — if not yet resolved, the seam constricts past reach; the surviving beasts make one last lunge for the door and are *gone* through it next round whether the party stops fighting or not. Combat ends.
- **Avoid: killing the stag.** Allowed, not punished mechanically — but Sela takes the shed antler and the Thornward's *"do not insult me by dying"* lands colder in Encounter 2. Let mercy be the better story.

### Tactics Summary

- **Great Stag** — R1 Charge the nearest armored PC between it and the deer-path (Kto), gore + knockdown. R2 thrash free of melee, Blink toward the seam, gore anyone in the gap. R3 all-out bolt for the door. **Calmed at any point → it stops and the fight unwinds.**
- **Fey-Touched Wolves (×4)** — R1 Pack Tactics onto whoever blocks the path; Blink past front-liners toward soft targets only if cornered. R2 harry, knock prone, peel toward the seam as it shrinks. R3 break for the door. **Lose heart and scatter if the stag is calmed (WIS DC 12).**

> [!WARNING]
> **Flag:** DM — This is a mercy puzzle wearing a fight's clothes
> The party has Nalith (Animal Handling +6, Druid), Naan (Skilled: Animal Handling; Healing Light works on the stag), and Kto (Animal Handling +5). Any *one* success on the stag ends it. Don't hide the off-ramp — telegraph the fear in the beasts' eyes from initiative. If the table chooses violence, run it straight, but make every bloodied beast *flinch,* not snarl.

### Loot / Aftermath

- **No coin, no gear** — these are stranded animals. If the stag is **killed**, it sheds a single white antler crowned in cold blossom (Sela keeps it; minor fey-iron token, sentiment not sale).
- If the stag is **calmed**, it follows the party to the treeline in Scene 5 and will not cross — a living thank-you and a small fey boon (DM discretion: advantage on the party's first Animal Handling/Survival check in the Living Wood next session).

### Fey-Touched Great Stag

> A plow-horse-sized stag half-stepped out of the Feywild — antlers hung with wilting white blossom, hide rippling with ghostly green light, eyes wide and fey-bright with **fear.** It is not hunting. It is trapped on the wrong side of a closing door.

**Traits.** *Fey Shimmer:* once per turn when it moves, the stag can **Blink (teleport up to 10 ft.)** to an unoccupied space it can see, leaving a scatter of white blossom — does not provoke. *Charge:* if it moves ≥20 ft. straight toward a target and then gores on the same turn, the target takes an extra **2d6** and must make a **STR DC 14** save or be knocked **prone.** *Frightened Heart (⚗️):* the stag is panicked, not hostile — it has **advantage on saves vs. being frightened or charmed by a hostile source,** but is **Calm-able** (see Actions/Trigger).

**Actions.** *Antlers:* melee, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., **2d6 + 4 bludgeoning** (~11). *Hooves:* melee, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., **1d8 + 4 bludgeoning** (~8), used against a prone or adjacent foe.

**Calm-able (not an attack — the win condition).** A creature within 30 ft. can take an action to soothe the stag: **Wisdom (Animal Handling) DC 14**, or `speak with animals`, `calm emotions`, druid beast-empathy, or healing magic spent on it. On success the stag is **calmed** — it stops attacking, lowers its head, and the encounter unwinds (wolves save WIS DC 12 or scatter).

**Round-by-Round Tactics.** R1: Charge the armored PC blocking the deer-path. R2: Blink toward the seam, gore anyone in the gap. R3: full bolt for the closing door. **Calmed at any point → fight ends.**

**On defeat.** Killing it is the *worse* outcome. Sheds an antler; Sela grieves; the Thornward notices. Steer toward calming.

## Encounter 2 — The Hawthorn Gate
*Scene 4 · Hard-leaning (raw 2,200 XP → ~4,400 adjusted) · ceremonial test, parley at bloodied/R3 · 6×L6*

### Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | The Hawthorn Cleft — a fey clearing where twin hawthorns arch over a glowing Feywild **seam**, ringed by seven standing stones. The seam is the door to the Bright Court Beneath. |
| **Light** | Twilight-bright fey glow under the arch; ordinary daylight at the clearing's edge. |
| **Terrain** | The **seam/ritual circle** at center (entering it is the Thornward's whole job to prevent). **Standing stones** (cover) ring the clearing. Two **toadstool rings** (difficult terrain). **Ribboned cairn** to the east (cover). Forest path enters from the south; **blossoming hedge** walls the north. |
| **Antagonists** | 1× **Maelorn, the Thornward** (CR 5, ⚗️) + 2× **Sprite Outrider** (CR 1, owl-mounted — reused from S4 for continuity). |
| **Party** | **6 PCs at Level 6.** |
| **XP** | Raw 2,200 → ×2 multi-foe → **~4,400 adjusted** (Hard for 6×L6 is 5,400; Medium 3,600). **Hard-leaning** but scripted short — the Thornward yields, he does not win. |
| **Duration** | 3 rounds max. Thornward **kneels and names the party worthy when bloodied (≤45) OR at end of Round 3** — whichever first. |
| **Goal (PCs)** | **Show the Thornward something worth naming** — courage, restraint, cleverness, or mercy. Winning is not killing him; it's being judged worthy. |
| **Goal (Thornward)** | Test, not kill. Measure these mortals, keep them off the seam, yield with honor when they've proven themselves. |
| **Berry shortcut** | If **Nalith spends Quillip's hawthorn berry** (shows/presses it to the arch), the Thornward lowers his glaive — see *Triggers.* The fight becomes **one symbolic round,** no real combat. |

### Triggers & Countdowns

- **The Berry Shortcut (best outcome).** If **Nalith presents Quillip's hawthorn berry** at any point (action to show/press it to the arch), the Thornward **immediately grounds his glaive.** Run **one symbolic round** — a single crossed-blades salute — then go straight to *Scene 5* terms. The Sprites stand down. *No real combat.* Flag it as the reward for keeping the token.
- **Bloodied (≤45 HP)** — the Thornward steps back, plants his glaive, and **kneels:** *"Enough. You are worth the table."* Combat ends. Standard worthy-verdict.
- **End of Round 3** — even unbloodied, the Thornward calls the test passed (*"You did not break, and you did not kill. Good."*) and yields. Combat ends.
- **Ceremonial Restraint (⚗️)** — the Thornward **never drops a PC.** Any hit that would reduce a PC to 0 instead leaves them at **1 HP and prone** ("the flat of the thorn"). Narrate it.
- **Mercy carries over** — if the party *calmed* the beasts in Encounter 1, the Thornward knows (the wood tells him) and **opens at advantage to yield** — treat his bloodied threshold as **≤55** and let him yield a round early. If they *killed* the stag, he is colder and holds to the strict bloodied/R3 triggers.
- **Avoid: killing the Thornward.** Audience off; the lord's interest sours into a grievance; move to an S6+ problem and let it cost.
- **Avoid: a PC death.** Ceremonial Restraint should prevent it. If dice get strange, have the Thornward call the test over on the spot — he would rather yield than spill guest-blood.

### Tactics Summary

- **Maelorn the Thornward** — R1 deliver the challenge (free), Thorn-Glaive ×2 against the boldest melee PC, pulling the blow. R2 *Bramble Snare* to control the line, glaive the snared. R3 yield (or earlier at bloodied). **Stays between the party and the seam** the entire fight.
- **Sprite Outrider 1 & 2** — R1 hover, *Heart-Sight Arrows* at the two most aggressive PCs; any sprite reading "resolve, not malice" shifts to non-lethal for the fight. R2 *Sleep-Tipped Arrows* at the highest-output caster (non-lethal). R3 salute and peel off when the Thornward yields. **They mirror the S4 outriders exactly** — players should recognize them.

> [!WARNING]
> **Flag:** DM — This is the Herald fight's sequel, by design
> Same shape as S4's crash: a courteous fey who will not be embarrassed, two familiar sprites, a hard parley trigger, no PC deaths, no fey deaths. The *difference* is the question it asks. Watch what the party does and write it down — restraint, cleverness, mercy, or swagger all give the Court a different read for the full-moon audience (S6).

### Maelorn, the Thornward

> A fey knight of the Bright Court, armored in bark and bronze, helmed in a crown of living silver branches hung with white blossom. His glaive ends in a single black thorn. His voice is level and old. He has no intention of killing you — and every intention of finding out whether you are worth his lord's table.

**Fey, Lawful Neutral · CR 5 (1,800 XP) · ⚗️ homebrew**

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 17 (bark-and-bronze plate) |
| **HP** | **90** (12d8 + 36) · **Bloodied 45** |
| **Speed** | 30 ft. |
| **STR** | 16 (+3) |
| **DEX** | 18 (+4) |
| **CON** | 16 (+3) |
| **INT** | 13 (+1) |
| **WIS** | 16 (+3) |
| **CHA** | 17 (+3) |
| **Saves** | DEX +7, WIS +6, CHA +6 |
| **Skills** | Athletics +6, Insight +6, Perception +6 |
| **Damage Resistances** | Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical weapons not made of cold iron |
| **Condition Immunities** | Charmed, frightened |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16 |
| **Languages** | Common, Elvish, Sylvan |

**Traits.** *Magic Resistance:* advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects. *Fey Step (recharge 4–6):* bonus action, teleport up to 30 ft. *Ward of the Gate:* while the Thornward is conscious and within 15 ft. of the seam, no creature can enter the seam — passing it requires dropping or moving him. *Ceremonial Restraint (⚗️):* the Thornward fights to test, not kill — any hit of his that would reduce a creature to 0 HP instead leaves it at **1 HP and prone.** He will not deliver a killing blow.

**Actions.** *Multiattack:* two Thorn-Glaive attacks. *Thorn-Glaive:* melee, +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., **1d10 + 4 slashing + 1d6 poison** (~13; the poison is a numbing fey toxin, never lethal). *Bramble Snare (recharge 5–6):* thorns erupt in a 15-ft. line; each creature in it makes a **DEX DC 14** save or is **restrained** (escape DC 14 STR, or 5 fire damage frees them).

**Bonus Actions.** *Fey Step* (see Traits).

**Reactions.** *Thornward's Parry:* when hit by a melee attack, add **+3 AC** against that attack (may cause it to miss). Once per round.

**Round-by-Round Tactics.**
- **Round 1.** Free: deliver the challenge aloud. Glaive ×2 against the boldest melee PC; pull the blow. Hold the seam.
- **Round 2.** *Bramble Snare* across the party's front to control the line. Glaive the snared PC. Parry the hardest incoming hit.
- **Round 3.** **Yield.** Ground the glaive, kneel, name the party worthy. Combat ends. (Earlier if bloodied.)

**On yield.** Rises, gives the **guest-token of iron-hawthorn,** states the full-moon audience terms (see file 1, Scene 5). *Do not kill him* — see Triggers.

### Sprite Outrider (×2)

> Small fey mounted on fey-touched giant owls — pale gray feathers, starlight pupils. The same two outriders who circled the Greengrass bonfire. Their toy-looking bows are not toys.

**Fey, Chaotic Neutral · CR 1 (200 XP each)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 15 (leather + fey grace) |
| **HP** | **22** (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 10 ft. ground, 60 ft. fly (80 ft. on owl mount) |
| **STR** | 8 (−1) |
| **DEX** | 18 (+4) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 14 (+2) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 13 (+1) |
| **Skills** | Perception +4, Stealth +6 |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14 |
| **Languages** | Common, Elvish, Sylvan |

**Features.** *Mounted (Giant Owl):* the owl provides a flying mount; the sprite uses the owl's movement. *Heart-Sight:* as an action, touch a creature OR succeed on a DC 13 ranged shot with a *Heart-Sight Arrow* to read its surface emotional state. If a sprite reads "resolve/curiosity, not malice," that sprite **shifts to non-lethal** for the rest of the encounter.

**Actions.** *Shortbow:* ranged 40/160, +6 to hit, **1d6 + 4 piercing.** *Sleep-Tipped Arrow* (1/encounter each): ranged 40/160, +6 to hit, **1d6 + 4 piercing**; target makes a **CON DC 12** save or is **incapacitated (drowsy) until the end of its next turn.** Non-lethal. *Heart-Sight Arrow* (1/encounter each): ranged touch 40/160, DC 13 vs AC, 1 piercing on hit, reads the target's surface intent.

**Round-by-Round Tactics.**
- **Round 1.** Hover 60 ft up. Heart-Sight Arrows at the two most aggressive PCs. Any sprite reading "resolve, not malice" goes non-lethal.
- **Round 2.** Sleep-Tipped Arrows at the loudest-output caster — aiming to *drowse,* not drop.
- **Round 3.** Salute the Thornward's yield and peel off into the seam.

**On defeat.** A sprite dropped to 0 → the other breaks off and follows the Thornward. They are not here to die for the gate; don't let the party farm them.
