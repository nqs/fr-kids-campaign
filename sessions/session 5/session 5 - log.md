## Session 005 — The Thorn Lord's Summons
**Date played:**
**Location in-world:** Shadowdale — Greengrass Festival grounds (bonfire, riverside); Old Skull Inn (overnight); Ashaba House (morning meeting); Orvyn Tal's bookshop; Sela Wyndmere's cabin; Living Wood (approach to center)
**Players present:** Fox (Fiorn), Sev (Yinu), Ari (Dirk), Lily (Naan), Piper (Nalith)
**Absent:** Caitlin (Kto)
**Session ended at:** Party in the Living Wood, mid-approach to the center of the blight; scene cut before reaching the Thorn Lord's court

---

### What Happened

**Bonfire — end of Greengrass Festival**

- **Dirk introduced:** New PC, Dirk (Ari), an orc fisherman described as middle-aged, 6'9", pipe-smoking, weathered — visual reference: Quint from *Jaws* with orc features. Currently living on a small raft/boat moored on the River Ashaba at the edge of town. Joins the party at the festival.
- Lady Ulphor presides over the bonfire, thanks attendees, and congratulates ribbon-winners.
- Nalith catches a squirrel (Animal Handling — success; DM notes she could simply call it to her as a druid). The squirrel, named Jared by the players, is carried in Nalith's bag for the rest of the session.
- Dirk speaks with a woman by the river — **Sela Wyndmere** — who is watching the crowd. He asks if she is okay. She gives her name: *"Sela. I am the healer of the town."* She notes she lives on the outskirts and is familiar with the fey of the Living Wood, and that the summons is *"very concerning."* When Dirk asks how she knows the figure, she abruptly claims she forgot something in her oven and leaves. Dirk reports this to the party.
- **Maelorn the Thorn Ward arrives:** As the bonfire is underway, gasps from the crowd precede a fey knight on a massive stag emerging from the treeline. The figure is visually confirmed as Maelorn the Thorn Ward (DM presented miniature/card). Music stops at his entrance. He rides to the center of the gathering, his voice booms, the smell of fresh flowers fills the air, and he points individually at each of the four PCs present (Fiorn, Yinu, Naan, Nalith; Kto absent). He states: *"The Living Wood calls you. Those of you that sealed the cleft of darkness are summoned to his court. You are expected in two days. Do not disappoint me by dying."* He turns and rides back into the treeline, which closes behind him. His wave of acknowledgment toward Fiorn's parting comment is noted.
- **Sela observed:** One PC (Naan) notices Sela sitting by the river, watching the Thorn Lord's appearance with obvious recognition, wringing her hands anxiously. When Naan approaches and asks if she is okay, Sela deflects ("you're new to town, aren't you?") and again withdraws. Naan reports to party.
- Party returns to the **Old Skull Inn** for the night. Dirk sleeps on his boat.

**Morning — Ashaba House**

- Butler admits the party. They are shown to the solarium; tea and snacks provided as usual.
- **Lady Ulphor debrief:** States the town council has been concerned about fey withdrawal — the fey had pulled back when the drow showed up and have not returned. She notes: *"The Thorn Ward came to our town. That has never happened before. You could ignore it at yours and our peril."* She offers the party whatever mundane supplies they need (backpacks, grappling hooks, etc.; explicitly not large bulk goods). She recommends they speak to **Sela Wyndmere** before entering the wood, notes she has it on good authority Sela is back at her cabin this morning despite disappearing the night before. She mentions **Brynn Ashford** also asked to see them.
- **Mark confirmed:** Arcana check called to examine the fey mark on the four PCs. [Roll unclear — result given as 24, speaker ambiguous but appears to be Yinu/Sev.] Result: all four are enchanted with a fey mark, enchantment school, more powerful than immediately apparent; the summons carries a compulsion — ignoring it would have consequences of unknown type.
- **Dirk introduced to Lady Ulphor:** First meeting. Lady Ulphor notes orcs have a fearsome historical reputation among humans, dwarves, elves, and halflings; she personally reserves judgment but advises Dirk that most will be frightened of him. She does not pay the party for this mission; frames it as acting in their own interest against the compulsion.
- **Letter to Naan:** On the way out, Lady Ulphor hands Naan a letter. Contents (Naan reads it; Dirk successfully peeks — Stealth check, result ~19 [roll 9 + modifier corrected to ~10, but DM accepted ~19 after sheet error clarification]): an introduction and invitation to the **Temple of Lathander** in **Highmoon** (described as the nearby regional capital), inviting Naan to study with the clergy or use their resources. Naan tucks it away without announcing it to the party.

**Orvyn Tal's Bookshop — Harper gift**

- Yinu goes alone (the rest of the party is sent ahead or tied off [unclear — played for laughs; Fiorn literally produces rope to stop others following]).
- Orvyn greets Yinu warmly: *"We have been watching you for quite some time."* He hands her a book: **"The Cypher and Glyph Sign of the Northern Sword Coast."** He tells her plainly: *"The Harpers have been waiting for you. This is not a recruitment — this is a key. Read this book before we next meet."* He explains the Harpers briefly: a covert network working against forces like the drow, the Red Wizards of Thay (Szass Tam's Thay; DM references the D&D movie *Honor Among Thieves*), and other threats to the region.
- Fiorn notes his character will stay up all night reading the book given other demands on the day.

**Sela Wyndmere's Cabin**

- DM reads a prepared scene description: *"The healer's cottage smells of dry herbs, river clay, and something faint and silvery underneath that might be foxfire or might be your imagination. Bundles of green hang from every beam. A workbench runs the length of one wall — mortars, knives, clean linen, and three spools of ribbon set out in plain sight. The half-elf is packing a leather satchel like a soldier breaking camp. A white streak bright in her dark hair, fur still on her sleeves from somewhere she has already been this morning."* She looks up without surprise: *"The lady said you'd come. Sit. I'm nearly done."* Somewhere in the garden the birdsong stops; she pauses, head tilted, then continues packing without explanation.
- **Sela's confession:** When asked directly if she knows the Thorn Ward, Sela confirms it and reveals her history: many years ago (twelve years, established later) when she first came to Shadowdale, the wood opened itself to her and granted her healing powers. In exchange, the wood asked small favors — reporting who had come and gone, the town's winter stores, when Elminster was in residence. *"Some of these favors were simple. And when it asked favors of me, I didn't really question it."* Over time it became apparent the fey court did not always act in the best interests of Shadowdale — the fey took children, took stores, and left droughts as gifts. *"I know that by telling you this, I am breaking a pact, that will lose me the power that I've been given. But it is time that I become honest with myself and my people, and stop aiding those that have come before us."*
- Sela notes she remembers carrying Rellan Tessar (referred to as "Raelon" [DM's pronunciation]) down off of Old Skull — she had already prepared his burial cloth. *"Whatever else you are, you're the kind of trouble I can work with."*
- **Ribbons identified:** Naan (Religion check, ~19) notices weathered ribbon knots on tree trunks at intervals through the edge of the wood. Sela confirms she was asked to place them as a code — two white ribbons for one message, several green for another. Naan explains to Sela that the ribbons were likely functioning as stitching — binding the Feywild seam to the material plane, keeping the wood half-enchanted. Sela is horrified: *"Are you telling me that if I didn't supply the ribbons, they may have been able to rip back into their world?"* She acknowledges she is responsible, in part. Naan offers that she did not know, and that *"there are things not even God can know."*
- Sela agrees to guide the party into the Living Wood: *"If you'll have me, I'll walk with you."*
- Sela hums Fiorn's woodwind competition song as they walk; when Fiorn notes it, she says: *"You're quite the skilled musician. The primrose you named at the competition — that came straight from the Fey Realm. The Living Wood dropped that onto the table for you that day. Have you ever spoken to them at all, that you're aware of?"* Fiorn: No. Sela: *"Interesting."*

**Entering the Living Wood — Skill Challenge**

- Party walks into the forest with Sela. DM describes the progression: first half mile is ordinary spring (birdsong, blackthorn in bloom, smell of green things); then birdsong fades, color bleaches out, blossoms go from white to gray. *"The deeper you go, the more it feels that the forest is packing up and leaving, and you are walking the wrong way, down the road, out of town."*
- DM runs a skill challenge (multiple PCs roll their specialties):

  - **Nalith — Animal Handling:** Two terrified goats discovered huddled in a thicket (linked to a missing-goats rumor from a farmer outside town). Nalith calls to them; they calm and bound back toward their farm. [DC/roll not stated; DM ruled success.]

  - **Naan — Religion (~19):** Notices weathered ribbon knots on trunks at intervals. Identifies them as Feywild binding markers — the court's stitching to the material plane (result feeds into Sela's confession above).

  - **Dirk — Climb + Perception (14):** Climbs a several-hundred-foot tree to the top of the canopy. At the summit, finds a single bloom — the last living flower seen in the wood. Looking outward: the forest is green a few miles back toward town, but for several miles in every other direction the forest is gray. A circle of blight centered on where they are heading. Yells down to the party: *"It's dying! The forest is dying!"*

  - **Yinu — Arcana (30: roll 19 + 11):** Makes the key discovery of the challenge. Identifies a **Feywild seam** that keeps this wood half-enchanted — the tie between the material plane and the Feywild here is contracted and pulling away. It is not that the fey are voluntarily leaving; **they are being recalled.** The seam is collapsing inward toward the center of the blight. Sela's ribbons were the stitching; without them, the seam has been retracting. DM notes: *"Think of it like water going down a drain — the Feywild is being pulled toward the center."*

  - **Fiorn — Performance (26):** Inspired by the decaying forest, composes and performs a mournful piece he names *Falling* on a conjured violin. The performance makes the party viscerally feel the sensation of being pulled down a drain — the blight is circling inward toward the center of the wood.

- Sela adds context toward the end of the walk: twelve years ago, her garden would not take, the bees were gone, flowers dropping dead. *"A voice in the Hawthorn said: carry my words and I'll hold the green."* She carried messages for the court for twelve years. *"I never did read them myself. And I told myself that meant they were just words I was delivering."* She never discovered what they said.

- **Session cut:** Party reaches (or approaches) the center of the Living Wood. DM calls the session before any confrontation with the court. Scene deferred to Session 006.

### Key Decisions & Consequences

- **Dirk joins the party** — new PC integrates at the festival; invited along to the fey court by inclusion (was not marked, but accompanies).
- **Sela Wyndmere breaks her pact** — she has voluntarily confessed her twelve-year arrangement with the Living Lord's court, breaking her oath. This will cost her the healing powers the wood granted her. Timing and effects TBD.
- **Yinu accepts Harper recruitment** — she takes the cipher book from Orvyn Tal. Not formally recruited yet; Orvyn frames it as "a key, not a recruitment," but the expectation to return after reading is clear. First active step toward Harper affiliation.
- **Naan's Temple of Lathander letter** — received and read privately; not disclosed to party. Hook is live but unresolved.
- **Feywild seam collapse identified** — the party now understands the blight is caused by the contraction of the seam, accelerated by the removal of Sela's ribbon-stitching. The fey are not leaving voluntarily; something is recalling them. Implications for the Living Lord's court summons are unknown.
- **Fey mark confirmed as compulsion** — ignoring the summons carries undefined but serious magical consequences; party is effectively compelled to attend.

### NPC Interactions

**Lady Ulphor** — Morning meeting at Ashaba House. Confirms the Thorn Ward's appearance is unprecedented and alarming. Provides mundane supply support; will not pay for this mission (frames it as self-preservation). Delivers Naan's Temple of Lathander letter personally. Directs party to Sela and to Brynn. Shrugs off Naan's mildly provocative comments without alarm — noted by DM that the town hosts extremely powerful individuals (Elminster named); she is not easily rattled.

**Orvyn Tal** — Delivers the Harper gift to Yinu: *The Cypher and Glyph Sign of the Northern Sword Coast.* First direct and explicit statement of Harper affiliation and intent. Confirms the Harpers have been watching Yinu. Asks her to read the book and return. No other PCs present.

**Sela Wyndmere** — Major scene. Half-elf healer, approximately 150 years old; has delivered nearly every baby in Shadowdale for decades. Breaking a twelve-year pact with the Living Lord's court, losing her fey-granted healing powers in the process. Guided the party into the wood and disclosed the ribbon-marking system. Key NPC for navigation of the Living Wood. She is clearly frightened but resolved. Noted that she had already chosen Rellan Tessar's burial cloth before the party brought him back — this earns her trust in the party.

**Maelorn the Thorn Ward** — Arrived dramatically at the bonfire on a massive stag. Delivered the summons to the four marked PCs. *"Do not disappoint me by dying."* Departed without further interaction. Confirms the summons is from the Living Lord's court; his title is the Thorn Ward. No further dialogue.

**Dirk (new PC)** — Introduced. Former sailor/fisherman, lives on a raft on the River Ashaba. Low charisma; blunt. First NPC contacts: Sela (brief, she fled), Lady Ulphor (brief; received a lecture on orc reputation), party (warming up; Fiorn has already nicknamed him "Dirch"). Has no sense of direction. Carries a harpoon, fishing rod, daggers, and a large drawstring bag of beers. Acquires no notable gear this session.
