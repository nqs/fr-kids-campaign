# Where We Left Off

![Morning After the Greengrass Bonfire — the Shadowdale village green](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/d7d8236a-5bee-4e14-99e6-6b52a480ca2b.png)

**You won Greengrass — and then the wood walked out of the trees to look at you.** At dusk, as Lady Ulphor lifted the firebrand, the **Antlered Herald** rode a blossom-crowned stag onto the river-stones and told you a lord you have never met — of the **Bright Court Beneath** — has **marked** you. He left an open invitation: an audience at the **next full moon**, on neutral ground, at a hawthorn-cleft of the lord's choosing. Then he was gone into the Living Wood.

After the fight, **Sela Wyndmere** — the village healer who's patched you up all winter — came to you shaking and said the worst thing she knows: *"He has been using me. I knew. I didn't know how to say it."* She has been carrying ribbon-knotted messages for that fey lord for years, and never reading them.

You are **Level 6** now, ribbons pinned, the bonfire finally lit behind you. It is the morning after, the embers are still smoking, and you have a fey lord's claim on you and eleven nights to figure out what that means.

### What Now?

- **Lady Ulphor wants everything** — and she will not let you walk into a fey court blind. *Find out who this lord is before the full moon.*
- **Sela is the road into the wood.** She can read the Living Wood when no one else can — but she's frightened, and she isn't telling you everything yet.
- **Orvyn has a gift for Yinu** at the bookshop. **Naan has a sealed letter** she hasn't opened. Both can wait — or can't.
- **The wood is emptying.** The fey are leaving. Animals are going missing at the forest's edge. Something is pulling the green *in.*

## The Living Wood, Leaving

![Title Page — The Hawthorn Gate](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/d597c8e3-787a-418e-813f-57e137d5d3d1.png)

Half a mile into the Living Wood the spring runs out. The blossoms go from white to grey, the birdsong thins, and the color drains from the trees like a painting left in the sun. Sela walks it the way you'd walk a sick-room. The deeper you go, the more it feels like the forest is **packing up and leaving** — and you are walking the wrong way down the road out of town.

## The Hawthorn Cleft

![The Hawthorn Cleft — seam to the Bright Court Beneath](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/3db5005e-5078-4445-8b9c-d3b47a2c994a.png)

Two ancient hawthorns have grown together into an arch, white with the last blossom in the forest. Beneath them the air **stands up like water on its end** — a doorway of green-gold light, the seam to the Bright Court Beneath pulled tight to a single shining point. Seven standing stones ring the clearing, each one hung with a knot of ribbon: green, white, bone. **This is the hawthorn-cleft the Herald named.** This is where the audience will be.

## The Frightened Wood

![Fey-Touched Great Stag and Wolves of the Frightened Wood](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/60c3df1f-0866-4338-b36e-a5901ec3408f.png)

A plow-horse-sized stag, antlers hung with wilting blossom, hide rippling with ghost-green light — and its eyes are wide with **fear,** not fury. Grey wolves flicker around its legs like guttering candles. They are not hunting you. They have been stranded on the wrong side of a closing door, and you are standing in the only way home. *They can be calmed, if you understand what you're looking at.*

## Maelorn, the Thornward

![The Bright Court Gatekeeper — Antler-Knight of the Living Wood](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/4bf897f3-5f05-4013-b8bf-fe13669c4526.png)

The keeper of the gate. A fey knight in bark and bronze, helmed in living silver branches, a single black thorn for a glaive-head. He is courteous and old and entirely unafraid.

> *"I am the Thornward of this gate. My lord has marked you, and the marked are tested before they are met. Show me you are worth my lord's table. I will not aim to kill. **Do not insult me by dying.**"*

He is not here to kill you. He is here to find out what kind of guests you are.

## Sela Wyndmere

![Sela Wyndmere — Shadowdale healer and unwilling fey courier](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/db2dc54c-e594-4a2c-986f-03262373b20b.png)

The village healer — and, it turns out, the only Shadowdaler the deep fey still speak to. For twelve years she has tied ribbon-knots and left them at a hollow hawthorn for a lord she's never seen, never reading the messages, telling herself that meant they were clean.

She is the one who can walk you into the wood. She is also frightened of what she's done — and somewhere on the road, if you've been kind to her, she'll tell you why: *"I wasn't keeping the wood alive. I was helping him gather it up."*

## Quillip Burr-and-Whistle

![Quillip Burr-and-Whistle — Pixie ally of the Living Wood](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/f0be0843-81b5-4ed2-851d-a66dca504923.png)

Your pixie go-between, turning up where the fey business is thickest. She left Nalith a dried hawthorn berry at the Greening — and at the gate, she'll want to know if you still have it. *"You still have my berry? Now would be the time, sweetling."*

She also has a riddle she won't drop: *"Full moon's his. But the quarter-moon's mine — hawthorn, midnight, bring three friends, not four. There's a thing he doesn't want you to see, and I do."*

## The Sprite Outriders

![Sprite Outriders on fey-touched giant owls](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/0a8a1646-979f-475b-875b-73fda652afe6.png)

The same two who circled the Greengrass bonfire — small fey on fey-touched owls, starlight in their pupils, little bows that look like toys. They ride for the Bright Court, and they guard its gate alongside the Thornward. They were watching you that night. They're watching you now.
