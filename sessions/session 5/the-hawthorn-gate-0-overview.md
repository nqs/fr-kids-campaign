# Session 005 — The Hawthorn Gate

*Tier 2 · 6 PCs at Level 5 · the Greengrass bonfire → the Living Wood → the Hawthorn Cleft*

**Status:** ⏳ Not yet played — the summary below reflects the planned major beats, not a record of play.

The session opens on the beat Session 004 never reached: **the Greengrass bonfire.** As Lady Ulphor lifts the firebrand, the **Antlered Herald** rides out of the Living Wood, *marks* the party for his unnamed lord, and names a full-moon audience — the session's first fight, built to end in parley and a scripted escape. In the aftermath the healer **Sela Wyndmere** confesses she has carried the lord's messages for years, and a stranger in the crowd — **Dirk** (silverstrike1's in-fiction debut), an orc deckhand stranded when his barge fled a river gone silent — is attached to the party the next morning. With the Living Wood emptying, they collect Sela at her cottage and walk into the wood to find the hawthorn-cleft the invitation named, passing the **Frightened Wood** (a no-initiative mercy beat: fey-touched animals that can be calmed rather than killed) before reaching **the Hawthorn Gate**, where the Bright Court's gatekeeper — the **Thornward** — tests the party, pausing over the **unmarked sixth** before a challenge that collapses into ceremony if Nalith still carries Quillip's hawthorn berry. The session is built to end with the **audience terms sealed** for the full moon — six named in the guest-right — Sela's arc opened, and hooks lit for Session 006 and beyond.
