# Where We Left Off

![Title Page — The Greengrass Greengage Affair](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/43198d6d-e1d7-46c9-b12e-3195c0c463bf.png)

**You are standing over Khelziir Aun'velve's body in the Shrine.** The drow priest is dead — Sev's Scorching Ray took him while Piper held him with *Hold Person.* Caitlin took down Zeldrazz T'orrl with her greatsword in the same round; the man who came back to finish her got finished instead. The portal anchor is still humming on the basalt altar, half-finished. *It is waiting for someone to finish.*

The Lolthites never came. The fight ended before the ritual hit zero. Both surface drow are dead. **Site C is closed.**

Loric walks in through the antechamber as the dust settles. Nema is at the cleft mouth on overwatch and steps in now. **You are five again.**

### What Now?

- **The anchor.** Smash it, take it, leave it running with a Harper listening glyph slipped in — or walk away. Yinu's Arcana can read enough of the inner ring to know there is something **fey** built into the heart of it that the drow never noticed.
- **The walk back.** Half a day overland down the southern face of the Old Skull, then home.
- **Brynn Ashford's letter** is still in Kto's belt-pouch. The party still doesn't know it exists — unless someone tells them.
- **The Ashaba House is waiting.** Lady Ulphor is hosting Greengrass tomorrow. She has not yet asked you to stay. She will.

## The Greengrass Festival

![The Greengrass Procession — Shadowdale on the morning of the festival](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/e124fecd-aec7-4a73-af0b-435f7617cb7d.png)

Greengrass is the first night of Mirtul — the cusp between winter and spring on the Dalelands calendar. Flowers, peace, and the first open river-crossings of the year. The Living Wood's druids walk the Greening at the wood's edge. The village green is bonfired. Old grudges are set aside for one night. The fey, by old tradition, walk the surface openly.

Shadowdale's festival is small but stubbornly proud — flower crowns on every fence, a procession from the Twilight Tower to the river-stones, an evening bonfire that Lady Ulphor lights herself, and five stations on the green that the kids of Shadowdale have been training all winter to win.

The Ashaba House is your bed and your beer for the next twenty-four hours. *"You will not be charged for the Ashaba House while you are mine."*

---

## The Five Stations

- **The Riverbank Archery Range** — floral-bobber targets in the Ashaba current. **Captain Helm** judges. **Rellan Tessar** is shooting in the same heat.
- **The Bardic Showcase** — under the Twilight Tower banner. The headliner is **Halbren Vass**, a touring half-elf with too much pomade.
- **Orvyn Tal's "Wonders of Spring" Arcane Stall** — three mystery items donated by villagers, identified live for the crowd. **Orvyn** quietly judging.
- **The Dragonchess Open** — back room of the Old Skull Inn. **Brynn Ashford** running the bracket and playing in it.
- **The Greening of the Beasts** — at the Living Wood's edge. **Sela Wyndmere** assisting. Flowers, blessings, a skittish doe.

*(If your sheet is still settling, the **Festival Footrace** along the south bank of the Ashaba is the floor option.)*

## The Bonfire at Dusk

![The Crash at the Bonfire — Lady Ulphor with the firebrand, the Antlered Herald emerging from the Living Wood at dusk](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/f1a020e6-d58f-4b93-ac73-372677397369.png)

When the sun goes down, the procession reforms on the river-stones. Ribbons get pinned. Lady Ulphor lifts the firebrand. The village holds its breath.

**Behind her, at the edge of the Living Wood — a stag steps out of the trees.** It is the size of a draft horse. Its antlers are crowned with white blossom. And there is a man riding it.

## The Antlered Herald

![Lorindar Greenmantle, the Antlered Herald — fey emissary of the Bright Court Beneath](https://storage.gemini.mcp.nqs.io/images/b7db5a83-2e57-4aa6-b858-12c3a397d03f.png)

> *"Honored guests of the Lady of the Ashaba — and you five who walked into our borders and walked out again — I am **Lorindar Greenmantle**, **Herald of the Bright Court Beneath**, and my lord has marked you. He sends greeting on this night of his choosing. He asks..."*

He carries a standard — a white branch crowned in iron antler, hung with three ribbons of green, white, and bone. He is courteous. He has not raised his voice.

He is also visibly armed. A long fey-iron glaive is across his saddle.

**If you look at his ribbons — and then at Sela's belt — they are the same knot.**
