# Session 004 — The Greengrass Greengage Affair

*Tier 2 · 5 PCs at Level 5 (→ 6 at the debrief) · Shadowdale (the Greengrass Festival)*

**Status:** ✅ Played

Home for the **Greengrass Festival**, the party debriefed Brynn first at Orvyn Tal's bookshop and then **Lady Ulphor** at the Ashaba House, surrendering the portal-anchor pendant — revealed to be half of a paired **twin-stone** whose counterpart lies in the Underdark — to Ulphor's vault. They swept the festival contests: Fiorn won the bardic showcase (drawing a road-tour offer from the touring elf-bard **Helbrin**), Yinu won the arcane challenge (prompting **Orvyn Tal to reveal his Harper tie** and promise her a gift), Nalith won the Greening and glimpsed Quillip again, and Kto dueled a recovered Rellan Tessar at archery. The night turned when the **Antlered Herald** rode out of the Living Wood: an unnamed fey lord of the **Bright Court Beneath** had *marked* the party and named a full-moon audience, and the healer **Sela Wyndmere** confessed she had been carrying his messages unread. The festival's closing bonfire beat was deferred to Session 005.
