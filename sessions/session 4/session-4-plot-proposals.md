# Session 004 — Plot Proposals

The party left Session 003 standing over Khelziir's body in the Shrine. Both bosses are dead, the Lolthites never crashed, and the surface drow incursion just had its head taken off. Session 004 opens with the **leftover business** — anchor disposition, the walk back, and the Ashaba House debrief — and after that the table needs something **lighter**. The kids just survived a Hard/Deadly fight; they've earned a breather. The party also hits **Level 6** at debrief, so the back half is a natural place to celebrate it.

This document proposes **three different plots** for the after-debrief portion of Session 004. All three are low-stakes, comedic, character-driven, and kid-table appropriate. Pick one (or mash two together).

---

## Shared Opening — "Cleaning Up" (≈45–60 min, fixed)

Before any of the three plots below, the session has to clear the Session 003 backlog:

- **Anchor decision** — Smash / Steal / Feed / Withdraw. The party is standing in the Shrine with two dead drow at their feet. Yinu has the ritual journal. Nalith can spot the residual fey-wrong of the half-finished glyph. Loric (back at the table) walks in via the antechamber having heard the fight end — easy retcon for Lily's return. Nema gets her in-fiction arrival here too: she was already at the cold camp, was on overwatch outside, and steps in now.
- **Walk back to Shadowdale** — a half-day overland beat. Use it to hand out loot, surface Brynn's letter (Kto is still carrying it), and let the table breathe.
- **Ashaba House debrief** — Lady Ulphor warmer, Brynn sharper, Orvyn (if present) buying drinks. Pay the 600 gp baseline + ending-dependent bonus. **Milestone: Level 6.**

Then the chosen plot begins. Each option below assumes 2–2.5 hours of play.

---

## Plot A — "The Greengrass Greengage Affair"

**Tone:** Festival comedy. Pony races, pie crusts, embarrassed militia. Public and social.
**Hook:** The party walks into Shadowdale to find the town already half-decorated. **Greengrass** — the FR spring holiday marking the end of winter — is in three days. Lady Ulphor's debrief is rushed because she's also hosting the festival opening, and she politely "asks" the party to stick around as honored guests of the Ashaba House.

### Setup
- Shadowdale celebrates Greengrass with the standard Dalelands beats: a flower-crown procession from the Twilight Tower down to the riverbank, pony races along the Ashaba's south bank, a pie & preserves contest judged at the Old Skull Inn, and an evening bonfire with a bardic showcase.
- Lady Ulphor is officiating. Brynn is running the pie judging (he insisted). Orvyn Tal is "browsing" the bookstall but actually watching the crowd. **Captain Helm** has been ordered by Lady Ulphor to share the judges' table with Fiorn for the bardic showcase, as part of the "reconciliation" she paid for at the debrief.
- **Rellan Tessar** is back on his feet enough to ride; he's entering the pony race and would like Kto to ride alongside him as her introduction to the Shadowdale militia social circuit.

### Possible scenes
- **The Pie Crisis.** Three contest pies have gone missing from the Old Skull Inn pantry overnight. No forced entry. No crumbs. Torval Dunmore (innkeeper) quietly asks the party to find them before judging starts in two hours. *(Culprit option: a hungry awakened badger Sela has been nursing back to health and forgot to mention; or a Quillip prank; or Fiorn's "Jester god" being literal.)*
- **The Bardic Showcase.** Fiorn vs. a smug rival entertainer named **Halbren Vass**, a touring half-elf minstrel who's been the Dalelands' Greengrass headliner for six years running. Performance-off. Captain Helm has to publicly score Fiorn. Heroic Inspiration is on the line.
- **The Pony Race.** Kto + Rellan vs. four other riders. Athletics / Animal Handling / Acrobatics challenges. The course crosses a stream where someone (Loric? Nalith?) can set up a "totally legal" buff zone.
- **Quillip Crashes the Party.** She's never seen a human festival, she's invisible, and she wants to see what a "pie" is. Low-stakes mischief — she swaps two flower crowns, makes a beard whistle in tune, takes one bite out of every pastry on the dessert table. No bargain owed; she's just curious.

### What it unlocks
- **+Goodwill in Shadowdale.** The town stops treating the party as "those out-of-town freelancers." NPCs greet them by name from now on.
- **Halbren Vass** becomes a minor recurring rival NPC for Fiorn — *(DM ADDITION, flag for roster).*
- **Rellan Tessar** moves from "owes them his life" to "actual friend." Adds a personal stake when the Vhaeraunian retaliation arrives in 2–3 weeks.
- Optional: a Greengrass prize (a silver-leaf brooch, a bottle of Twilight Tower wine, a young pony) — pick one, hand it to whichever PC wins their event.

### Why this one
Maximum NPC density. Lets every player do a thing — Fiorn performs, Kto races, Yinu judges arcana-relevant entries, Loric runs the kissing booth (or whatever), Nalith handles animals, Nema gets introduced to the whole town in one afternoon. Burns no plot capital. Resets table tone after three combat-heavy sessions.

## Plot B — "Quillip's Picnic at the Hawthorn"

**Tone:** Whimsical fey hijinks. Small group, big imagination. Standalone-feeling.
**Hook:** A week after the debrief, Nalith finds a folded acorn-leaf on her bedroll with a single dried hawthorn berry inside. *"You stand under a hawthorn at dusk on the next quarter-moon and I'll show you something nice. Bring four friends, not five. — Q."* (Quillip is being cute — she means "all of you," but she's a pixie.)

### Setup
- Quillip is hosting a small, very informal fey gathering on the granite shoulder above the Living Wood — neutral ground, no court politics, no oaths. Maybe six fey total: a hob with opinions about cheese, a pair of sprites running a leaf-boat race, a quiet dryad who answers questions in seasons rather than yes/no, and a sleepy badger Sela rehabilitated last spring who is here as a guest of honor.
- Quillip wants to **thank** the party for killing the watcher and giving her two days of her life back. The original-song bargain is settled — this is gratitude on top. **No cost.** *(Hold firm on this; the kid table will get suspicious otherwise.)*
- The gathering is small enough that the kids' characters are the main entertainment. Quillip wants stories. The hob wants to argue. The dryad wants to be left alone politely. The sprites want a fifth boat.

### Possible scenes
- **The Leaf-Boat Race.** A miniature regatta on a runoff puddle the size of a dining table. PCs build boats from on-hand materials (a hand crossbow bolt? a torn page from Yinu's spellbook? Fiorn's hat?). DC 10 Sleight of Hand / Nature / Arcana to launch a viable craft. Prize: a single dried hawthorn berry that will, planted in the right place, grow into a hawthorn that always blooms two weeks early. Cosmetic, but charming.
- **The Hob's Cheese.** The hob (**Brom Tussock-Knee**, *DM ADDITION*) wants the party to settle whether his cheese is the finest in the Dales or whether Sela's goat cheese is. Comedy tasting. Whoever picks his side gets a wheel of pungent gray cheese that grants Heroic Inspiration the first time it's eaten and only the first time.
- **The Dryad's Question.** The dryad (**Sister Asha-of-the-Long-Shade**, *DM ADDITION*) will answer **one** honest question put to her by the party — but only in the form of a season. ("Spring." "Late autumn.") The DM picks the answer ahead of time for one prepared question from the loose-ends list (e.g., *"When does the Vhaeraunian retaliation come?"* → "The end of next month, after the second rain."). If they don't ask anything she's prepared for, she answers "Today" and refuses elaboration.
- **The Sleeping Badger.** The awakened badger (**Margrim Bristle**, *DM ADDITION*) sleeps through the whole party but, on a successful Animal Handling DC 12, will wake up just long enough to tell **one** PC something true about themselves. Use for any character whose backstory sheet is blank — Kto, Yinu, Loric, Nalith all qualify.

### What it unlocks
- **Quillip becomes a friend, not a vendor.** Future bargains shift one tier of cost downward when the party invokes "the night under the hawthorn" — *(flag for roster).*
- **Sister Asha** is a recurring fey contact for low-cost soft prophecy. Use sparingly. *(DM ADDITION.)*
- The sleepy badger answer can be the start of a personal-arc seed for whichever PC engages with him — particularly useful for the four blank backstories.
- Plants a seed about the **unnamed fey lord** Quillip won't name — Sister Asha may glance toward the south once, briefly, before she catches herself. Don't pay this off yet; it's foreshadowing.

### Why this one
Small, weird, intimate. Lets shy players have a moment. Lowest-prep option — one location, ~five NPCs, no combat. Lots of room for Nalith (Druidic, Animal Handling) and Fiorn (Performance, Insight) to shine. Excellent if the table is post-combat tired and wants to roleplay over a snack table for two hours. Also a clean place to introduce Nema if Plot A didn't do it.

## Plot C — "Rellan's Welcome-Home Feast"

**Tone:** Living-room comedy. Awkward toasts, broken furniture, character beats.
**Hook:** The morning after the debrief, the party gets five hand-delivered invitations from **Mistress Holda Tessar**, Rellan's mother. *"Tomorrow evening, the third bell, our home above the millrace. We owe you our son. Please come hungry."* It's a six-course Dalelands family dinner. Half of Shadowdale is also coming.

### Setup
- Rellan is back from extended bedrest. His mother Holda has been planning this dinner since the day he came home — she has invited Lady Ulphor (politely declined, but sent a basket), Brynn (came; awkward), Orvyn (came; brought a wrapped book), Sela Wyndmere (came; brought poultices), Captain Helm (came under duress; will leave early), Torval Dunmore (catering), and twelve of Rellan's cousins.
- The Tessar millhouse is one room, sixteen guests, and not built for it. Bench seating. The party is at the head of the table whether they want to be or not.
- **The toast goes around the table.** Holda asks each guest to say one thing about Rellan. Then she asks each member of the party to say one thing about the rescue. *Roleplay challenge for the kids.* DM gives advance warning before going around.

### Possible scenes
- **The Toast.** Each PC gets a moment in front of an audience. No die roll. Players who freeze get an out — "Fiorn talks over you, smoothly" — but every kid who tries gets Heroic Inspiration. Captain Helm's toast is one sentence and pointed: *"I am glad I was wrong about him."* Kto can read whatever weight she wants into that.
- **The Mystery of the Vanishing Pies.** Holda baked three pies for dessert. By the time dessert comes around, only one is left. **Investigation DC 12** — Perception around the kitchen, Insight on the cousins, Animal Handling if anyone notices the back door is cracked. Solution can be Quillip again (recurring gag if Plot A also ran), one of Rellan's cousins, or **Margrim Bristle** the awakened badger (*DM ADDITION; usable across all three plots*) who wandered up from the riverbank. Pick whichever lands for table.
- **Captain Helm's Apology.** Helm asks to step outside with **Fiorn** specifically. Off the porch, no audience. He says — in his own words, not Lady Ulphor's — that he was wrong, and that he is going to be wrong again, and that he would like Fiorn to not gloat the next time. He extends his hand. Fiorn can take it, refuse it, or do something Fiorn-shaped. **This is the closure of the S2 Helm rift.** Whichever way it goes, mark it.
- **Orvyn's Book.** The wrapped book Orvyn brought is for **Yinu**. It is a slim Harper primer on *Cypher and Glyph-Sign in the Northern Sword Coast.* No price tag. No strings spoken aloud. *(Subtle Harper recruitment of Yinu specifically — flag for roster.)*
- **Brynn pulls Kto aside.** He saw her shift the letter in her belt-pouch at the debrief. He doesn't ask what she chose to do with it. He just says: *"Whenever you decide. I'll be at the bookshop Thursday afternoons."* And then he goes back inside.

### What it unlocks
- **Closes the Helm rift** as an active hostile thread (he may still be sour, but the open hostility ends here).
- **Opens Yinu's Harper recruitment** quietly. Companion thread to Plot A's bardic-showcase or Plot B's dryad-question.
- **Brynn's letter** stops being a buried secret and starts being a deliberate Kto-choice. Even if Kto still says nothing, the rest of the table now knows Brynn knows.
- Locks in **Rellan + Holda Tessar** as a "home base" NPC family for the party in Shadowdale. *(Holda is a DM ADDITION — flag for roster.)*

### Why this one
Best option for character work. Pays off the **three biggest social loose ends in one evening**: Helm rift, Brynn's letter, and Orvyn's quiet courtship. No combat, no map, no statblocks. Highest "roleplay-y" demand from the kids — if the table likes voice and improv, this is the one. If they don't, lean on Fiorn (player Fox is the Entertainer and will carry the room).

## Comparison Cheat Sheet

| | Plot A — Greengrass | Plot B — Pixie Picnic | Plot C — Rellan's Feast |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scale** | Whole town | Five fey on a hill | One dining room |
| **NPC density** | High (10+) | Medium (5 fey) | High (10+) |
| **Combat** | None (optional pony chase) | None | None |
| **Loose ends paid off** | Helm-thaw (public) | Quillip-debt + soft prophecy seed | Helm rift CLOSED + Brynn's letter + Yinu/Harpers |
| **DM prep** | Medium — 4 set-pieces | Low — one location, 5 NPCs | Low–medium — one room, scripted toast |
| **Best for** | Loud table, all five PCs active | Quiet table, character beats | Roleplay-strong table, big payoffs |
| **Risk** | Can run long if all four set-pieces fire | Whimsy may not land with action-oriented kids | Demands kids speak in-character on the spot |
| **Nema in-fiction intro** | Easy — public event | Easy — Quillip invites "four friends" | Requires a small retcon |
| **Loric T. (Lily) re-entry** | Easy — she rejoins for the festival | Easy — Quillip invites the whole party | Easy — she's at the feast |

---

## Recommendation

**Default to Plot C** if the table has the appetite for roleplay — it pays off the most lingering threads and the kids have shown (Session 003's Fiorn / Quillip exchange) that they'll engage when the social scene has stakes. **Fall back to Plot A** if energy is high and the table wants action variety — it's the most "stuff to do per minute." **Reserve Plot B** as the floor option if half the kids are tired and you need something gentle that still feels like a session.

You can also **chain B → A or B → C** across two short sessions later if you don't run Plot B now — Quillip's picnic doesn't expire and works as a future cooldown after the Vhaeraunian retaliation hits.

## DM Additions Flagged for Canonization

If any of these plots are picked, draft roster/world entries for:

- **Halbren Vass** — half-elf touring minstrel, Fiorn's recurring rival *(Plot A)*
- **Brom Tussock-Knee** — opinionated hob, cheese partisan *(Plot B)*
- **Sister Asha-of-the-Long-Shade** — quiet dryad, soft-prophecy contact *(Plot B)*
- **Margrim Bristle** — awakened badger, Sela's rehab patient *(any plot — recurring gag)*
- **Holda Tessar** — Rellan's mother, Shadowdale millhouse matriarch *(Plot C)*

None of these contradict FR canon for Shadowdale or the Dalelands; they sit comfortably alongside Lady Ulphor, Torval Dunmore, and Sela Wyndmere as small-town named-NPC color.
