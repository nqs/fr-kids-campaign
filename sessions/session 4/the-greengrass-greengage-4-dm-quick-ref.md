## The Crash — One-Page Combat

### Setup
**When:** Lady Ulphor raises the firebrand at dusk.
**Trigger:** A weapon comes out before Lorindar finishes his sentence — Sela's ribbon recognized OR a PC moves first.
**Round budget:** 3 rounds max. Herald escapes when bloodied OR end of Round 3.

### Antagonists

**Lorindar Greenmantle, the Antlered Herald (CR 5)**
- AC **16** · HP **85** (bloodied **42**) · Speed 30 (40 mounted on stag)
- **Glaive:** +7, **1d10+4 slash + 1d8 force** (~14)
- **Bow:** +7, **1d8+4 pierce + 1d6 radiant** (~12), 150/600
- **Fey Step:** 30 ft., bonus, recharge 4–6 (at-will on planned exit)
- **Reproof of the Court:** disadvantage on 1 melee hit/round (reaction)
- **Standard-Bearer's Word:** free, 1/encounter — speak 1 sentence, all hear it within 60 ft.

**Sprite Outrider × 2 (CR 1 each)**
- AC **15** · HP **22** · Speed 80 fly (owl)
- **Shortbow:** +6, **1d6+4 pierce**, 40/160
- **Sleep-Tipped Arrow** (1/encounter): CON **DC 12** or incapacitated 1 round
- **Heart-Sight Arrow** (1/encounter): reads target intent; if "fear" not "malice," sprite holds fire

### Round-by-Round Cheat Sheet

| Round | Lorindar | Sprites |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Speak invitation (free). Mount-charge to ~30 ft. of the first-aggressor PC. 2× glaive. | Heart-Sight on the 2 most aggressive PCs. Hold if "fear." |
| **2** | Continue glaive on same target. Reproof the second hit. | Sleep-Tipped Arrow on the loudest caster. |
| **3** | **Scripted Exit.** Fey Step + stag bolt 60 ft. into Living Wood. | Cover fire, then peel off. |

### Ending The Fight

| Outcome | When | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Round 3 timer | Always available | Herald escapes; standard delivered |
| Bloodied | HP ≤ 42 | Herald escapes early; same delivery |
| Sela intervenes | PC about to drop OR fight too lethal | Sela steps in; PCs hold; Herald bows out |
| Audience accepted | PCs hold weapons through speech | Best outcome; mark for S5+ |
| Lorindar killed | PC kill before Round 3 | **Bad.** Cost the table; war begins |

### Civilian Screen
**Helm + 4 reservists + Rellan + Sela** hold the line. **PCs do NOT have to triage civilians.** They are behind the militia.

## DM Don'ts

- **Don't** kill Lorindar. (See file 2 — let it cost if it happens.)
- **Don't** put a civilian in danger. Helm screens. The kids do not have to triage.
- **Don't** force the letter or the Harpers. If kids walk past them, that's fine; both threads survive to next session.
- **Don't** push Naan to spend Healing Light dice at the Blessing station — let Lilli decide. Track exact dice remaining before the Crash.
- **Don't** explain the signal-stone or numbing draught. **Plant** them.
- **Don't** narrate Quillip dropping the berry — *let Nalith notice it.*
