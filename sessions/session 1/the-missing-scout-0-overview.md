# Session 001 — The Missing Scout

*Tier 2 · 5 PCs at Level 5 · Shadowdale → Old Skull's northern flank*

**Status:** ✅ Played

The party arrived in Shadowdale as freelance hands and took on Torval Dunmore's worry: militia scout **Rellan Tessar** was four days overdue, and Captain Corwick Helm had written him off as a deserter. **Lady Addee Ulphor** hired them, and they tracked Tessar's trail through the fey-touched Living Wood to a drow cleft on Old Skull's northern flank, taking the camp by surprise. They cut down the drow elite **Zeldrazz T'orrl**, his scouts, and a giant spider, freed Tessar alive, and carried back a Vhaeraunian map fragment marking two more staging sites. Lady Ulphor received the intelligence with quiet dread — and that evening the bookseller **Orvyn Tal** slid a Dalelands map across their table, putting the Harpers on the board.
