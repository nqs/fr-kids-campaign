## 1. The Dales Council

**Type:** Regional governing body  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good (though fractious)  
**Seat:** Ashabenford, Mistledale  
**Status:** Active — the primary political voice of the free Dales  

### Overview
The Dales Council is the closest thing the Dalelands has to a central government, though it claims no sovereignty over individual dales and never has. Each free dale sends representatives; debate is loud, votes are contested, and consensus is rare — but in the face of Sembian conquest and Netherese pressure, the Council has grown "real teeth." *[CG]*

The ancient **Dales Compact** — the pact between the human settlers of the Dales and the elves of Cormanthor — was reaffirmed with Myth Drannor in the Year of Seven Sisters (1425 DR), and the **Standing Stone** that marks that agreement (destroyed by drow decades earlier) was restored. *[CG]*

### Current Membership
The free dales in council are: **Archendale, Battledale, Daggerdale, Deepingdale, Harrowdale, High Dale, Mistledale, Shadowdale.** Featherdale, Scardale, and Tasseldale are under Sembian/Netherese control and have no current representation.

### Goals
- Maintain the independence of the free dales against Netheril and Sembia
- Enforce the Dales Compact and preserve the alliance with Myth Drannor
- Coordinate mutual defense, trade roads, and diplomatic relations with Cormyr

### Tensions
- Archendale is the most aggressive and militaristic dale; it chafes at Council consensus
- Mistledale (where the Council sits) is nervous about Cormyrian expansion through Tilver's Gap
- The loss of three dales to Sembia has created a bloc of exile voices pushing for military liberation

### Adventure Hooks
- Council diplomatic missions to Myth Drannor or Cormyr
- Rival dale representatives with conflicting agendas
- An exiled Tasseldale or Scardale figure seeking Council support for a liberation movement

## 2. The Eladrin of Myth Drannor

**Type:** Sovereign eladrin kingdom  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Seat:** Myth Drannor, within the Cormanthor Forest  
**Ruler:** Coronal Ilsevele (per sourcebooks) — update if your campaign has changed this  
**Status:** Active — powerful ally of the free dales  

### Overview
Myth Drannor is the refounded eladrin realm within the great Cormanthor Forest, restored after the Spellplague by Coronal Ilsevele. It is the most significant military and magical power friendly to the Dalelands, and the alliance between Myth Drannor and the free dales is ancient — codified in the Dales Compact at the Standing Stone. *[CG, PG]*

Myth Drannor has an **embassy in New Velar** (Harrowdale) and has financed the construction of that city's defensive wall and shipyards. Its armathors — elite guards of the Coronal — are an elite fighting force. *[CG]*

### Goals
- Protect Cormanthor and the surrounding dales from Netheril's shadow
- Advance the renewal of eladrin culture in the eastern Heartlands
- Counter drow incursions into the Underdark beneath Cormanthor

### Tensions
- Myth Drannor's sovereignty over the ancient Cormanthor forest occasionally conflicts with Deepingdale's half-elven settlers
- Hillsfar to the north resents Myth Drannor's blocking of forest expansion
- The alliance with Archendale is strained following the unmasking of Sembian-aligned rulers there

### Notable Assets
- The **armathors** (elite eladrin warriors)
- Deep reserves of arcane knowledge
- Embassy and trade mission in New Velar

### Adventure Hooks
- Escort a Myth Drannor ambassador through increasingly dangerous territory
- Recover an eladrin artifact looted from Cormanthor before it reaches Netheril
- Discover a drow portal beneath Cormanthor that threatens the Coronal's grip on the forest

## 3. The Empire of Netheril (the Shade)

**Type:** Hostile empire  
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
**Seat:** Anauroch (restored Netherese cities)  
**Rulers:** The Twelve Princes (shadow-touched archmages)  
**Status:** Active — primary existential threat to the Dalelands  

### Overview
The ancient empire of Netheril has been restored. The **Shadovar** — the shades of Netheril — emerged from their refuge in the Shadowfell and reestablished dominion over the desert of Anauroch. They then systematically absorbed Sembia as a vassal state, destroyed **Zhentil Keep**, and have been pressing against the free dales ever since. *[CG]*

The Twelve Princes rule from the floating shadow-cities, practicing a form of magic that blends the arcane with shadow. Their agents — the Shadovar — operate throughout the Heartlands, infiltrating trade routes, subverting local rulers, and preparing the ground for annexation.

Netheril views the Dalelands as an unfinished project. Tarkhaldale has already been quietly seized; several other dales came close. The alliance between Myth Drannor and Cormyr is the primary thing holding Netherese expansion in check along the eastern frontier. *[CG]*

### Goals
- Annex the remaining free dales (Daggerdale, Mistledale, Archendale are the next logical targets)
- Destroy or co-opt Myth Drannor — the greatest magical obstacle to Netherese dominance
- Subvert Cormyr as they subverted Sembia — through merchant infiltration and political manipulation
- Ultimately, restore Netheril's ancient dominance over all of Faerûn

### Methods
The Shadovar prefer subversion to direct conquest when possible: corrupt local leaders, buy up merchant houses, sponsor humanoid raiders to destabilize regions, and strike militarily only when the ground has been prepared. Their Cyric-aligned Zhentarim proxies and the drow they manipulate serve as deniable instruments.

### Presence in the Dales
- **Tarkhaldale** is already under Netherese control — its saurial inhabitants driven underground *[CG]*
- **Daggerdale** serves as an unwilling buffer against Netherese expansion from the north *[CG]*
- Shadow agents and paid informants operate in Archenbridge and along the Moonsea Ride

### Adventure Hooks
- Identify and expose a Shadovar agent embedded in the Dales Council
- Race Netherese treasure-hunters to a buried ruin that contains something they must not have
- Investigate why humanoid raids from the Desertsmouth Mountains seem suspiciously coordinated

## 4. Sembia — Netherese Client State

**Type:** Hostile vassal state  
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil  
**Seat:** (Former capital Ordulin destroyed; administration fragmented across controlled cities)  
**Status:** Active — occupying Featherdale, Scardale, and Tasseldale  

### Overview
Sembia was once the richest merchant kingdom in the eastern Heartlands. Over the past century, Netheril's Shadovar gradually subverted it — first through merchant investment, then through installed administrators, and finally through the appearance of mercenary armies. Sembia is now a puppet state, though its merchant lords retain local authority so long as they serve Netherese interests. *[CG, PG]*

Within the Dalelands, Sembia controls three former dales: **Featherdale** (bought out and taken bloodlessly), **Scardale** (seized through a series of betrayals), and **Tasseldale** (conquered militarily despite fierce resistance led by Grand Mairshar Erich Inshiland). *[CG]*

Sembian merchants still trade with the free dales — particularly through Archendale — and the Swords of Archendale were unmasked as Sembian agents as recently as 1440 DR, illustrating how deep Sembian commercial infiltration runs.

### Goals (as Netherese proxy)
- Maintain control of the conquered dales as a revenue base and southern staging area
- Continue commercial infiltration of Archendale and other free dales
- Suppress resistance movements in Tasseldale and Scardale

### Local Character
Featherdale is quiet; its people live under light Sembian administration with little to do with Myth Drannor. Tasseldale is restless; the locals "don't care to be governed by outsiders." Scardale Town has become a den of intrigue where Thayan scarlet ships dock without comment and Moonsea warlords do business. *[CG]*

### Adventure Hooks
- Uncover a Sembian commercial espionage ring operating in Archenbridge
- Aid a Tassadran resistance cell that is one betrayal away from collapse
- Pursue a Sembian tax collector who has stolen something that doesn't belong to him

## 5. The Zhentarim (Remnant)

**Type:** Criminal/mercenary organization (shattered, partially reconstituted)  
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil to Chaotic Evil  
**Former Seat:** Zhentil Keep (destroyed by Netheril); Darkhold (Far Hills, west of Cormyr)  
**Status:** Broken as a major power — scattered remnants active  

### Overview
Once the dominant criminal and mercenary power of the northern Dalelands and Moonsea, the Zhentarim — the **Black Network** — was shattered when Netheril's Shadovar razed Zhentil Keep and the Citadel of the Raven. The Black Network's grip on the northern dales is "a distant memory," but remnants persist. *[CG]*

**Darkhold**, a massive fortress in the Far Hills west of Cormyr, was taken by the Zhentarim and remains so — staffed by Zhent mercenaries available for any task, "no matter the perfidy required." *[CG]* This is the closest significant Zhent base to the Dalelands.

The **Church of Cyric** has long been intertwined with the Zhentarim. Since Cyric's imprisonment, the Cyricist faction has lost some of its sway, but its agents within the remnant organization continue to push the Zhentarim toward destructive behavior in hopes of freeing their god. *[CG]*

**Mulmaster** is considered the "ideological heir to the Zhentarim" — a Bane-worshipping city-state on the Moonsea where the House of the Black Lord has significant political control. *[CG]*

### Presence in the Dales
Daggerdale was occupied by the Zhentarim within living memory; locals still harbor deep suspicion of anyone showing Zhent colors. The **Zhent Headless Horseman** — actually a lich wearing Zhentarim regalia — haunts the forest crossings of Battledale's northern reaches, a literal ghost of the Black Network's former dominion. *[CG]*

### Streetwise
Zhent sellswords are most commonly found in the northern dales, near the Moonsea. Hobgoblin mercenaries frequently work under Zhentarim colors. Any capable humanoid is welcome — doppelgangers, tieflings, even vampire spawn serve as agents. *[CG]*

### Adventure Hooks
- Hired swords with Zhent colors have appeared on the road from Dagger Falls — scouting, or something worse?
- Someone is recruiting from Darkhold for a job that requires "discretion" deep in the Dalelands
- Discover what the Headless Horseman (the Zhent lich) is actually guarding, or hunting

## 6. Cormyr — The Forest Kingdom

**Type:** Friendly allied monarchy  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Seat:** Suzail  
**Status:** Active ally — particularly close with High Dale; watched warily by Mistledale  

### Overview
Cormyr is a prosperous, well-governed human kingdom to the west of the Dalelands, ruled by the Obarskyr dynasty. It has long been a trading partner and ally of the free dales, and when Sembia's threat became acute, Cormyr sent **Purple Dragon Knights** to garrison High Dale. *[CG]*

Relations are warm overall, but not unconditional. Mistledale's Dales Council worries about **Cormyrian expansion through Tilver's Gap**, and Cormyr's annexation of Proskur and its ongoing pressure along the Dragon Coast raises questions about the kingdom's long-term intentions. *[CG, PG]*

Cormyr is also a target: Netheril would love to absorb it the same way it absorbed Sembia, and Cormyr has resisted multiple infiltration attempts. That shared threat is currently the strongest glue in the Cormyr-Dalelands alliance.

### Notable for the Dales
- **Purple Dragon Knights** garrison High Dale
- Cormyr maintains trade missions in Archenbridge
- The kingdom has Darkhold (Zhentarim) within its western borders — an ongoing irritant

### Adventure Hooks
- A Purple Dragon Knight has gone missing in High Dale's mountain passes
- Cormyrian diplomats are seeking a quiet meeting with the Dales Council — what do they want?
- Evidence surfaces that Netherese agents have gained a foothold in a Cormyrian noble house

## 7. The Harpers of Luruar (Remnant)

**Type:** Secret society / resistance organization  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Seat:** Moongleam Tower, Everlund (far north; distant from the Dales)  
**Status:** Effectively defunct as an organization — individual agents and sympathizers persist  

### Overview
The Harpers were founded centuries ago at **the Dancing Place** — a sacred valley just north of Highcastle in High Dale, where more than a dozen deities manifested to inspire the creation of the organization. The Dancing Place is still preserved as a sacred garden by priests of Mielikki, Oghma, Selûne, and Silvanus; elves and eladrin make pilgrimages there. *[CG]*

The Harpers as a formal organization are described as "now-defunct" in the sourcebooks, though Moongleam Tower in Everlund serves as the only known surviving Harper holdout. Its clandestine leadership acts as a patron to those who "wish to take the secret pledge against the Shadovar and all ill that emerges from Shadow." *[CG]*

Harper-of-Luruar agents are secretive, experienced, and committed to opposing Netheril. They are not recruiters; they find you. *[PG]*

### Relevance to the Dales
Individual former Harpers — or those who've taken the secret pledge — operate throughout the Dalelands and the Moonsea region. They have no formal infrastructure in the Dales, which means they are dependent on friendly contacts and safehouses that can be compromised.

### The Dancing Place
This valley in High Dale is a significant piece of Harper lore and may serve as a pilgrimage site, a meeting place for resistance contacts, or simply a place of unusual divine power. Priests who tend it may know more than they let on.

### Adventure Hooks
- A figure who claims to represent Moongleam Tower needs the party's help with something in the Dales — off the record
- The Dancing Place has been desecrated; something is wrong with the divine energy there
- A former Harper's coded correspondence has been intercepted — what was she trying to warn the Dales Council about?

## 8. The Eldreth Veluuthra

**Type:** Militant elven supremacist organization  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
**Seat:** The Hullack Forest (west of Deepingdale); the Tangled Trees (Battledale)  
**Status:** Active — a persistent regional threat  

### Overview
The Eldreth Veluuthra ("Victorious Blade of the People") is a militant organization of human-hating elves who believe that humans have no place in Faerûn. They seized control of the Hullack Forest in 1391 DR, briefly provoking a military response from Cormyr before King Azoun V turned his attention to more pressing threats. *[CG]*

Their presence has all but ended the once-profitable timber trade through the Hullack Forest, harming Deepingdale's commerce. Battledale travelers occasionally encounter Eldreth Veluuthra hunting parties from the Tangled Trees. *[CG]*

### Goals
- Purge human settlements from the edges of Cormanthor and its satellite forests
- Disrupt commerce between human dales and elven/eladrin lands
- Assassinate half-elves, whom they view as a corrupting compromise

### Methods
Ambush, kidnapping, and terror. They do not negotiate with humans. Lone travelers and small caravans are their preferred prey.

### Tensions with Myth Drannor
Myth Drannor's coronal does not condone the Eldreth Veluuthra, but openly suppressing them is complicated — some of their members were once loyal citizens. The organization exploits this political difficulty.

### Adventure Hooks
- A trade caravan has vanished on the Hullack road; evidence points to Eldreth Veluuthra
- A captured Eldreth Veluuthra operative is willing to talk — but only to someone who can guarantee something they want
- A half-elf NPC is being hunted; the Eldreth Veluuthra has reached into the party's locale

## 9. The Hidden Hand of Fate

**Type:** Religious vigilante order (cult of Hoar, secretly serving Bane)  
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil (operating under the guise of Lawful Neutral)  
**Seat:** A temple fortress deep in the Arch Wood, near Archendale  
**Status:** Active — quietly influential throughout the Heartlands  

### Overview
The Hidden Hand of Fate is an informal order of bounty hunters, troubadors, and assassins who venerate **Hoar**, god of retribution and revenge. For centuries they have dwelt in the depths of the Arch Wood — appropriate, given that Archenfolk are known for short tempers, grudges, and love of intrigue. *[CG]*

The priests of the Hidden Hand sponsor a network of vigilantes who deal "rough justice" throughout the wilder stretches of the Dalelands, coordinating from the Arch Wood temple. In truth, **Hoar now serves Bane**, and the misconception that the Hidden Hand serves pure retributive justice is one that Bane is quite pleased to let continue. *[CG]*

### Goals
- Exact retributive vengeance on behalf of paying clients (official cover)
- Expand Bane's covert influence throughout the Dalelands under the guise of justice
- Eventually, position the Hidden Hand as an indispensable tool for Archendale's ruling interests

### Public Face vs. Reality
To common Dalesfolk, the Hidden Hand offers a valuable service: if the courts won't help you and the Watch won't move, the Hand will find the person who wronged you. The prices are high, and the methods are not always clean, but results are delivered. Few know that the order's ultimate loyalty runs up through Hoar to Bane.

### Adventure Hooks
- The party is hired by (or against) a Hidden Hand operative pursuing a target in the Dales
- A troubador who works as a Hand informant has learned something too dangerous — and is now the one being hunted
- The order's temple fortress in the Arch Wood is hiring outside talent for a job it can't staff internally

## 10. The Drow Underdark Factions

**Type:** Underdark hostile factions  
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil to Chaotic Evil  
**Relevant Entry Points:** Old Skull (Shadowdale), caverns beneath Battledale, Deepingdale's forest floor  
**Status:** Active — a persistent threat below the Dales  

### Overview
The drow plague **Battledale, Mistledale, and Shadowdale** via Underdark passages. Old Skull in Shadowdale is a volcanic headland that descends to the Underdark; Elminster's power once kept the drow bottled up, but since the Spellplague and his diminishment, drow are "whispered to be once again wending their way up the narrow stairs." *[CG]*

Two primary factions operate in the Dales region:

**Vhaeraunian Drow** — Male drow who venerate Vhaeraun, god of thievery and arrogance. They launched a significant assault on the Abbey of the Sword in Battledale, coming up through an unwatched portal. The assault was repelled, but the attack killed Priest General Eldan Ambrose. These drow use surface access points for clandestine operations and supply runs. *[CG]*

**Spider-Cult (Lolthite) Enclaves** — Standard Lolth-worshipping drow society. Less interested in raiding than in territorial expansion and the slow corruption of surface communities through enslaved intermediaries. Historically had controlled Shadowdale via Old Skull; that grip was loosened by Elminster and has not fully been reestablished — yet. *[CG]*

### Goals
- **Vhaeraunians:** Surface-side raiding, theft of arcane knowledge, and eliminating worship sites of rival deities (hence the Abbey of the Sword attack)
- **Lolthites:** Slow, patient expansion of Underdark territory; eventual surface beachheads

### Tensions Between Factions
The Vhaeraunian and Lolthite drow are not allies. Adventurers who know this can potentially set one against the other.

### Adventure Hooks
- Old Skull is showing signs of renewed drow activity; what's changed below?
- A Vhaeraunian agent has been operating disguised in a dale community — their goal is unclear but probably arcane
- A hidden portal beneath a farmstead near the River Ashaba has been activated

## 11. The Abbey of the Sword — Church of Tempus

**Type:** Religious institution / military base  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Seat:** The town of Ambrose (population 3,000), Battledale  
**Status:** Active — recovering from a significant drow assault  

### Overview
The Abbey of the Sword was established by the priest **Eldan Ambrose**, who traced the path of the god Tempus following the deity's appearance on the battlefield of Swords Creek in Mistledale during the Time of Troubles (1358 DR). Ambrose rebuilt a shattered castle in Battledale and consecrated it to the Foehammer. *[CG]*

Ambrose was killed when **Vhaeraunian drow** launched a surprise attack through an unwatched portal beneath the abbey. The defenders repelled the assault, but could not resurrect their leader. Today the Abbey continues in his name, and the town of Ambrose has grown up around it — "hearty folk rich in their worship of the Foehammer." *[CG]*

### Role in the Dales
The Abbey is a training ground for Tempus-devoted warriors and a source of healing and martial aid across Battledale. It is also a visible statement that the drow cannot take the surface unopposed.

### Current Status
The abbey lacks a Priest General of Ambrose's stature. The position is contested between a conservative faction that wants to fortify and a militant faction that wants to push the drow back underground.

### Adventure Hooks
- The party is asked to clear and seal the portal the drow used — but something down there doesn't want it closed
- The succession dispute for Priest General has become dangerous — someone's using poison
- The Abbey needs an escort for a sacred relic being transported to Ashabenford

## 12. The Stellar Fellowship

**Type:** Adventurers' guild and social club  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Seat:** The Fall of Stars, New Velar (Harrowdale)  
**Status:** Active — prominent institution in the free Dales  

### Overview
The Stellar Fellowship is a famous society of adventurers based in New Velar, Harrowdale. Their club — **the Fall of Stars** — occupies a row of luxurious old townhouses whose interiors have been converted into a feasting hall, rooming house, tavern, and restaurant. The club looks unremarkable from the street; the vast interior is a surprise. *[CG]*

Members in good standing receive free access to the Fellowship's stock of potions, antidotes, and medicines when injured. Non-members can rent rooms and enjoy the social scene, but the best contacts and hiring opportunities are reserved for Fellows.

### Role in the Dales
The Stellar Fellowship is the closest thing the free Dalelands has to a formal adventurers' guild. Its members hear rumors from across the Sea of Fallen Stars and the northern trade routes. The Fall of Stars is a natural hub for information, employment, and mutual aid.

### Membership
Membership is earned through demonstrated competence and a sponsor within the Fellowship. The annual dues are not cheap. The Fellowship does not ask about the politics of members' employers, which makes it genuinely neutral ground.

### Adventure Hooks
- The party can join the Stellar Fellowship — doing so provides recurring benefits and contacts but occasional obligations
- A mission board at the Fall of Stars carries a posting that shouldn't exist — someone is using the Fellowship to recruit for something covert
- A famous Fellow has gone missing on a job; his companions need outside help

## 13. The Church of Bane (Mulmaster)

**Type:** Religious power bloc (external, but exerting reach)  
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
**Seat:** The House of the Black Lord, Mulmaster (northern Moonsea)  
**Status:** Active — growing, sees itself as the ideological heir to the Zhentarim  

### Overview
Mulmaster is a Moonsea city-state built on iron foundries and fishing, nestled in the barren Earthspur Mountains. It is "a center for commerce, intrigue, and the worship of Bane." The House of the Black Lord dominates the Council of Blades that governs the city, using its voting bloc and purchased influence to shape policy. *[CG]*

Since Netheril destroyed the Zhentarim's power base, Mulmaster has positioned itself as the natural successor to the Black Network's role in regional commerce and coercion — minus the Cyricist baggage. "Mulmaster is the center of Bane's worship in Faerûn and, some say, the ideological heir to the Zhentarim." *[CG]*

### Reach into the Dales
Mulmaster does not yet control Dales territory, but Bane's priests have long operated as a silent shadow behind the Zhentarim (note: the Hidden Hand of Fate unknowingly serves Bane already). A Mulmaster expansion south via trade and religious infiltration is a plausible long-term threat.

### Adventure Hooks
- A Banite merchant from Mulmaster is offering suspiciously favorable terms to a Dales trading house — what does he actually want?
- Evidence connects the Hidden Hand of Fate to Bane's clergy in Mulmaster
- A Mulmaster inquisitor has arrived in Ashabenford, looking for someone

## 14. The Fey of the Living Wood

**Type:** Fey community (benevolent)  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Seat:** The Living Wood, Shadowdale (especially near the River Ashaba and Druid's Grove)  
**Status:** Growing — increasingly present since the Spellplague  

### Overview
The **Living Wood** is what Shadowdale's forest is now called, and with good reason. A "burgeoning population of fey and sylvan creatures" has appeared throughout Shadowdale since the Spellplague, drawn by the unusual concentration of geomagical energy along the banks of the River Ashaba. They were led to the dale by the retired Lord **Azalar Falconhand**. *[CG]*

The fey congregate near **the Druid's Grove** — a ring of standing stones surrounding a copse of ancient trees north of Shadowdale village. It "visibly exudes great primal power" and is particularly sacred to the fey. The border with the Feywild is weak along the River Ashaba; crossings into the Feywild are possible here.

### Current Ruler Connection
Lady **Addee Ulphor** rules Shadowdale, having gained the Pendant of Ashaba when Azalar Falconhand resigned. She maintains a cautious, generally positive relationship with the fey community. *[CG]*

### Role in the Dales
The Living Wood's fey are not formal political actors, but their goodwill is worth cultivating — particularly for parties needing Feywild knowledge, ancient lore, or passage through Shadowdale's increasingly fey-touched wilderness.

### Adventure Hooks
- Something from the Underdark (Old Skull) is corrupting the Druid's Grove — the fey are retreating
- A powerful fey lord has laid claim to a stretch of Shadowdale farmland the locals don't want to give up
- The Feywild border near the Ashaba has opened unexpectedly; something came through
